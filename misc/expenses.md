# Starship Monthly Expenses

## Summary

Operating a starship involves numerous regular expenses that must be paid every **Maintenance Period** (4 weeks/monthly). These costs can quickly mount up and force crews to make difficult decisions to meet their financial obligations.

**Key Monthly Expenses:**
- **Mortgage Payments**: Ship loan repayments (if applicable)
- **Life Support**: Food, water, and environmental systems  
- **Fuel**: Refined or unrefined hydrogen fuel
- **Maintenance**: Regular upkeep to prevent breakdowns
- **Crew Salaries**: Wages for hired personnel
- **Berthing Fees**: Starport docking and landing costs
- **Low Berth Costs**: Cryogenic passenger support

## Detailed Expense Breakdown

### Mortgage Payments
**Cost**: Total purchase price ÷ 240  
**Frequency**: Every Maintenance Period for 40 years  
**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, page 149

The monthly mortgage payment is calculated by dividing the total purchase price of the ship by 240. This represents a 40-year loan with payments due every 4 weeks.

*Example: A MCr37 Free Trader has monthly payments of Cr154,167*

### Life Support
**Cost**: Cr1000 per occupied stateroom + Cr1000 per person not in low berth  
**Frequency**: Every Maintenance Period  
**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, page 154

Covers supplies for environmental systems, food, and water. Meals at this level are described as "rather spartan."

### Low Berth Support  
**Cost**: Cr100 per occupied low berth  
**Frequency**: Every Maintenance Period  
**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, page 154

Costs for maintaining cryogenic passengers in low berths.

### Fuel Costs
**Refined Fuel**: Cr500 per ton  
**Unrefined Fuel**: Cr100 per ton (but risky for jump drives)  
**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, pages 154 & 258

Required for both jump drives and power plants. Unrefined fuel carries risks during jump operations.

*Fuel capacity varies by ship class - a Free Trader requires 20 tons for a Jump-1*

### Maintenance
**Cost**: 0.1% of total purchase price per year (÷ 12 for monthly)  
**Frequency**: Every Maintenance Period (monthly), annual shipyard service  
**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, page 154

Regular maintenance prevents critical failures. Skipping maintenance risks equipment breakdowns and potential critical hits to ship systems.

*Example: MCr37 Free Trader = Cr3,083 per month*

### Crew Salaries
**Standard Rates per Maintenance Period:**
- Pilot: Cr6000
- Astrogator: Cr5000  
- Engineer: Cr4000
- Steward: Cr2000
- Medic: Cr3000
- Gunner: Cr1000
- Marine: Cr1000

**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, page 154

### Berthing Fees
**Cost**: Varies by starport class (weekly rates)
- **Class A**: 1D × Cr1000 per week
- **Class B**: 1D × Cr500 per week  
- **Class C**: 1D × Cr100 per week
- **Class D**: 1D × Cr10 per week
- **Class E/X**: Free

**Rules Reference**: Core Rules 2022, page 257

Roll once per starport and record for future visits. Costs are due weekly and vary wildly between locations.

## Additional Considerations

### Cargo Operations
- **Speculative Trade**: Purchase goods at one world, transport and sell at another
- **Freight Contracts**: Fixed-rate cargo hauling (typically Cr1000 per ton)
- **Mail Contracts**: Flat-rate delivery service for data packets

### Passenger Service
- **High Passage**: Cr10,000 per passenger (stateroom required)
- **Middle Passage**: Cr8000 per passenger (stateroom required)  
- **Low Passage**: Cr1000 per passenger (low berth required)

### Emergency Expenses
- **Repairs**: Varies based on damage severity and starport facilities
- **Legal Fees**: Fines, bribes, or legal representation
- **Unexpected Delays**: Extended berthing fees, crew overtime

## Monthly Budget Planning

A typical Free Trader (MCr37) with basic crew might face:
- Mortgage: Cr154,167
- Life Support: ~Cr4000-6000  
- Fuel: Cr10,000 (Jump-1 + power)
- Maintenance: Cr3,083
- Crew (Pilot/Engineer): Cr10,000
- Berthing: Variable (Cr40-24,000 per month)

**Total Baseline**: ~Cr181,250-207,250 per month

*This assumes minimal crew and doesn't include cargo costs, repairs, or additional expenses.*
