# The Alibi (Upscale Bar)

**Referee's Note:** This is a modular encounter for a high-class, corporate, or political bar. It's a place for making influential contacts, overhearing sensitive information, and spending a lot of money. "The Alibi" is a high-end chain, often found in the highports or financial districts of major worlds.

> **Read Aloud:** The entrance to "The Alibi" is discreet, marked only by a single, elegant glyph. Inside, the contrast with the bustling starport is immediate. The air is cool and smells faintly of expensive perfume and real wood. The lighting is low and artfully arranged, illuminating minimalist furniture and holographic art that shifts slowly on the walls. The ambient music is a complex, soothing symphony. Patrons speak in low, confidential tones—corporate executives in sharp suits, high-ranking naval officers in dress uniforms, and elegant individuals who might be diplomats or high-class courtesans.

### The Staff

#### The Bartender: Anya

> **Read Aloud:** The woman behind the polished chrome bar moves with the grace of a dancer. She is impeccably dressed, and a sophisticated cybernetic earpiece glints beneath her hair. She makes eye contact, offers a warm, knowing smile, and can mix three different complex cocktails at once without ever seeming rushed. She is the calm, discreet center of this web of power and influence.

Anya is a master of her craft, and her craft is information. She knows who everyone is, what they drink, and who they're trying to impress. She is a vault of secrets, but she is loyal to her clientele and her employers. She will never betray a confidence for a simple bribe. However, she respects ambition and a clever play.

#### The Host: Julian

> **Read Aloud:** A man in a perfectly tailored suit stands near the entrance, a data-slate in his hand. He greets regulars by name and finds tables for new arrivals with an air of effortless authority. His smile is charming, but his eyes are constantly scanning the room, assessing the status and intent of every person present.

Julian is the gatekeeper. He knows the unwritten rules of the establishment: who can be seated where, who should not be introduced to whom, and when a conversation is private. Getting on his good side is the key to getting a good table or a crucial introduction.

### Task Chain: Extracting Information from Anya

Anya will not give up secrets for cash. She trades in a more valuable currency: information and influence.

#### Stage 1: Proving Your Worth (TN 9+)
The players must demonstrate that they are players, not just tourists.
*   **The Check:** A **Broker**, **Diplomat**, or **SOC** check (DM+1 if SOC is 10+).
    *   **Broker:** Order an expensive and obscure drink and casually mention a recent, high-level corporate merger as if you were involved.
    *   **Diplomat:** Compliment her on the bar's ambiance and subtly drop the name of a political figure you know she'd recognize.
    *   **SOC:** Simply act like you belong. Your bearing and confidence are enough.
*   **Success (9+):** Anya's professional smile becomes slightly more genuine. She lingers for a moment, engaging in light conversation. She now sees the players as part of the "in-crowd." This grants a **+1 bonus** to the check in Stage 2.
*   **Failure (8-):** The players' attempt to impress falls flat. Anya remains polite but distant, treating them like any other customer. She will not engage further.

#### Stage 2: The Trade (TN 8+)
The players must offer Anya a piece of valuable information to get one in return. The information they offer must be genuine and relevant to the interests of her clientele (e.g., a hot stock tip, a shift in shipping routes, a political scandal).
*   **The Check:** A **Broker** or **Persuade** check, modified by Stage 1.
*   **Outcome:**
    *   **Minimal Success (Failure):** The information is not valuable enough. Anya nods politely. "Interesting. Thank you for sharing." She offers nothing in return.
    *   **Standard Success (8+):** The information is solid. Anya processes it, then subtly returns the favor. "Speaking of shipping, I overheard Mr. Kane's pilot complaining about unexpected maintenance delays on their route to the Lanth subsector. He seemed quite frustrated." She has given the players a valuable, related secret.
    *   **Exceptional Success (Effect 4+):** The information is a major revelation. Anya is visibly impressed. She not only provides a counter-secret but also makes an introduction. "You know, the person you should really be talking to about that is Captain Valerius of the INV *Steadfast*. He's at the corner table. Let me send him a drink on your behalf."
