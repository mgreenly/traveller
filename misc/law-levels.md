# Law Levels Explained

The Law Level of a system is one of the most important pieces of information a traveller can have. It dictates what you can legally own and, more importantly, what you can carry. Getting it wrong can lead to fines, confiscation of expensive gear, imprisonment, or worse.

Law Levels are rated from 0 (no law) to 9 (extreme), with 'A' and higher representing even more restrictive environments.

---

### Law Level 0-1: No Law / Anarchy
*   **Description:** These are the truly lawless places. There is no central authority, only the rule of the strongest. Warlords, pirate gangs, or simple chaos reign.
*   **Weapons:** Anything goes. Openly carrying a combat rifle is not just legal, it's a fashion statement and a deterrent. Military-grade weapons and explosives are common.
*   **Armor:** If you're not wearing heavy combat armor, you're considered an easy target.
*   **Example:** Uncharted worlds, systems in collapse.

---

### Law Level 2-3: Low Law (The Frontier)
*   **Description:** Typical of frontier worlds or backwaters. There is law enforcement, but it's often underfunded, spread thin, or only concerned with major crimes.
*   **Weapons:** Most personal firearms are legal to own. Openly carrying longarms like rifles and shotguns is generally acceptable in public, though perhaps not in a high-class establishment. Heavy and military-grade weapons (e.g., machine guns) are likely restricted.
*   **Armor:** Visible armor like flak jackets or combat armor is common and won't attract undue attention.
*   **Example:** **Jenghe (Law Level 3)**.

---

### Law Level 4-6: Moderate Law (Civilized Standard)
*   **Description:** This is the standard for most civilized worlds in the Imperium. Law enforcement is professional and capable.
*   **Weapons:** Ownership of firearms is generally legal, but public carry is restricted. Openly carrying any firearm is illegal. Pistols may be carried with a permit, but they must be concealed. Longarms are strictly forbidden in public spaces.
*   **Armor:** Visible armor is illegal. Discreet armored clothing is the accepted norm for personal protection.
*   **Example:** **Dinom (Law Level 5)**.

---

### Law Level 7-9: High Law (Core Worlds)
*   **Description:** These are highly regulated, bureaucratic core worlds. Law enforcement is efficient, well-equipped, and technologically advanced. Expect to be scanned for weapons in public buildings and transit systems.
*   **Weapons:** All firearms are heavily restricted or outright illegal for civilians to carry, even with a permit. Blades are often restricted by length. Getting a permit for anything is a bureaucratic nightmare reserved for the well-connected.
*   **Armor:** All armor, including most forms of armored clothing, is illegal for personal use.
*   **Example:** **Regina (Law Level 9)**, **Wypoc (Law Level 7)**.

---

### Law Level A+: Extreme Law (Totalitarian)
*   **Description:** The state has a total monopoly on force. All personal weapons and armor are forbidden.
*   **Weapons:** No personal weapons of any kind are permitted. This can be interpreted to include large tools or anything that could be used as an effective weapon.
*   **Armor:** No personal armor of any kind is permitted.
*   **Example:** **Rech (Law Level A)** is a strange anomaly. There is no government, but the powerful factions that control the planet enforce this law level with brutal efficiency in their own territories to prevent rivals from arming themselves.

---
**Golden Rule:** When you arrive in a new system, your first question should always be: "What's the Law Level here?" Your survival might depend on the answer.
