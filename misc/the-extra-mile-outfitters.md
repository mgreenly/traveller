# The Extra Mile Outfitters

**Referee's Note:** This is a modular encounter that can be dropped into any starport with a Law Level of 3 or higher. It provides a reliable place for players to purchase gear and a potential recurring ally in the owner, Milo.

> **Read Aloud:** You find "The Extra Mile," a franchise outfitter common in starports across the sector. The storefront is clean and professional, its sign a simple, reassuring blue and white. Inside, the air smells of oiled synth-leather and fresh ration packs. The store is well-lit and meticulously organized. One wall is lined with vacc suits, environment gear, and survival equipment. Another displays personal electronics, from comms units to advanced sensor scanners. Behind a reinforced counter, a selection of tools, medkits, and other essentials are neatly arranged.
>
> The clientele is a typical starport mix: a grizzled free trader is quietly examining the stitching on a set of cargo straps, while a young couple in colonists' garb argues softly over the merits of different water purifiers.

### The Proprietor: Milo

> **Read Aloud:** The man behind the counter appears to be in his late 50s, with a calm, steady demeanor. He has a neatly trimmed grey beard, and his eyes, magnified slightly by his work glasses, miss nothing. He wears the simple, practical jumpsuit of an ex-Scout, and a faded tattoo of the IISS sunburst is visible on his forearm. He nods as you enter, offering a quiet, professional greeting. "Welcome to The Extra Mile. If you can't find it, you probably don't need it. If you do need it, I can probably find it. What are you looking for?"

Milo is a retired Imperial Scout who has seen enough of the galaxy to know that good gear is often the only thing between a traveller and a shallow grave. He is a source of excellent, practical advice. If the players ask for his opinion, he will offer insights like:
*   *"That blade is fancy, but the grip gets slippery when wet. This one is uglier, but it'll never fail you."*
*   *"You're heading to a desert world? Don't bother with that high-capacity water reclaimer. Too many moving parts. You want this one. It's less efficient, but it's built like a rock and you can fix it with a multitool."*

### Available Gear

The gear available on the shelves is strictly determined by the local **Law Level**. Milo is a legitimate businessman and will not openly display illegal equipment.
*   **Low Law (3-5):** Pistols, shotguns, and most personal weapons are available. Flak jackets and basic combat armor may be on display.
*   **Moderate Law (6-8):** Only very basic firearms (if any) are available, and likely require a permit check. No visible armor is sold. Discreet armored clothing is available.
*   **High Law (9+):** No weapons or armor are on display. The counter might have a sign that reads "All regulated items by special order only."

---

### Task Chain: Haggling for Special Items

Milo has a network of contacts and can acquire almost any piece of equipment, regardless of Legality. However, he will not risk his business for just anyone. This task chain is for convincing him to sell the players "off-menu" items.

#### Stage 1: The Test (TN 8+)
The players must first prove they are serious professionals, not reckless troublemakers. Milo will test them with a seemingly innocent question based on their request. For example, if they ask for an illegal weapon, he might ask, "What are you planning on shooting with that?"
*   **The Check:** This requires a **Persuade**, **Streetwise**, or **Profession (any)** check.
    *   **Persuade:** Represents convincing him of their good intentions or desperate need.
    *   **Streetwise:** Represents speaking the quiet language of the fringe, showing they understand the risks.
    *   **Profession:** Represents demonstrating a level of technical knowledge that proves they are not amateurs.
*   **Success (8+):** Milo gives a slight nod. He is satisfied they are not idiots or cops. "I might know a collector who is looking to offload a piece like that. It won't be cheap. Come back in an hour." This grants a **+1 bonus** to the check in Stage 2.
*   **Failure (7-):** Milo holds up his hands. "Sorry, can't help you. What you see is what I've got. Now, if you're interested in a top-of-the-line medkit..." He will not deal with them further on illegal items at this time.

#### Stage 2: The Negotiation (TN 8+)
This stage determines the price and availability of the requested item. The check is modified by the result of Stage 1.
*   **The Check:** This requires a **Broker** or **Streetwise** check.
    *   **Broker:** A straightforward negotiation over price.
    *   **Streetwise:** An appeal to mutual discretion and the understanding of how things work on the fringe.

#### Outcome Tiers
*   **Tier 1: Minimal Success (Failure on Stage 2 check):** "My 'collector' is nervous. The price is high, and it's going to take a day or two to arrange the delivery." The item costs **150% of its standard price** and will arrive in 1D3 days.
*   **Tier 2: Standard Success (Success on Stage 2 check):** "A fair price for a quality piece of equipment." The item costs **125% of its standard price** and will be available for pickup in a few hours.
*   **Tier 3: Exceptional Success (Success on Stage 2 check with Effect 4+):** Milo is impressed. "I like your style. For you, a professional discount." The item costs **100% of its standard price** and he has it in the back room right now. If the players have made a good impression, he may also throw in a piece of valuable advice related to their mission.
