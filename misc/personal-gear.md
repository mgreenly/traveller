# Personal Gear: What You Carry

This document outlines the typical gear a traveller would have on their person while walking the streets of a starport, a downport, or a city. This isn't your full mission loadout, but the everyday essentials for navigating the Imperium.

## The Comm
Everyone has a personal communication device, usually just called a "comm." It's a thin, durable device about the size of a deck of cards, with a holographic display. It's your link to the local network, handling everything from audio and video calls to messaging, news feeds, and data searches. More advanced (and expensive) models can have military-grade encryption or connect to satellite uplinks in remote areas. For most, it's the indispensable tool of daily life.

## Wallet & Credentials
Physical currency is rare. Your financial life is tied to a **Cred-Chip**, a small, durable card that is biometrically linked to you. When inserted into a comm or a payment terminal, it gives you secure access to your personal and business bank accounts from anywhere on the network.

Just as important is your **ID**. This can be a physical card or, more commonly, a digital file stored on your comm. It proves your identity, citizenship, and legal status. In many systems, you are legally required to present it to law enforcement upon request. Not having a valid ID is a fast way to end up in a holding cell.

## Protective Gear
In the safer, high-tech core worlds like Regina, visible armor is a major social faux pas and will attract immediate police attention. However, in the rougher downports and on the frontier, some level of discreet protection is common sense.

Most socially acceptable protection comes in the form of **Armored Clothing**. This can range from a simple mesh-lined jacket that can stop a knife to a business suit woven with advanced fibers that can diffuse the energy of a pistol round. While it won't stop a rifle shot, it might be the difference between a nasty bruise and a trip to the morgue. Anything more overt, like a flak jacket worn on the outside, is considered military gear and is illegal for civilians in most systems.

## Weapons & The Law
Carrying weapons is a serious matter. Every system has a **Law Level**, and this dictates what is legal to own and carry. In a high-law system like Regina (Law Level 9), carrying *any* weapon is a serious crime. In a lawless place like Rech (Law Level A), the authorities might not care, but the local gangs will view you as a threat.

As a general rule, openly carrying a weapon is a bad idea unless you are in a designated combat zone or a very, very dangerous part of the frontier. Most travellers who feel the need to be armed will opt for a small, concealable weapon like a snub pistol or a combat knife. Be warned: even these are illegal to carry in many places, and being caught with one will lead to fines, confiscation, and possibly jail time. Always be aware of the local Law Level.
