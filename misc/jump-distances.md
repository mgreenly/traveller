# Jump Distances

Ships cannot jump within 100 diameters of any object larger than themselves. This document shows the distance from various celestial bodies to their jump limits.

## Notes

- Jump distance = 100 × diameter
- Distance from surface = Jump distance from center - radius
- All distances rounded to nearest kilometer
- Actual stellar sizes vary significantly within each class
