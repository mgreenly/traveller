# Jump Distances and Travel Times

## Overview

In Traveller, ships cannot jump within 100 diameters of any object larger than themselves. This document provides travel times from various celestial bodies to their jump limits.

## Travel Time Formula

Since ships can jump with velocity, the optimal approach is continuous acceleration to the jump point.

**Time = √(2 × Distance/Acceleration)**

Where:
- Distance is in kilometers
- Acceleration is in G (1G = ~10 m/s²)
- Time is in seconds

This formula assumes constant acceleration all the way to the jump point, with the ship jumping at maximum velocity.

## Key Observations

1. **Continuous Acceleration**: Ships accelerate all the way to the jump point, entering jump space at significant velocity (often hundreds of km/s).

2. **Final Velocities**: Jump velocities range from tens of km/s (small bodies) to thousands of km/s (stellar jumps).

3. **Time Savings**: Continuous acceleration reduces travel time by about 30% compared to the accelerate-decelerate profile.

4. **Gas Giant Challenges**: The extreme velocities (500-1,400 km/s) when jumping from gas giants present navigation challenges for arriving ships.

5. **Energy Considerations**: Ships carry significant kinetic energy into jump space. The rules don't specify what happens to this energy.

## Strategic Implications

**High-Speed Pursuit**: Ships jumping at high velocity are harder to intercept, as pursuers must match both position and velocity.

**System Defense**: The predictable acceleration paths to jump points create natural choke points for system defense.

**Emergency Jumps**: Ships under attack benefit from continuous acceleration, reducing exposure time.

**Navigation Hazards**: Ships emerging from jump space at high velocity must plan their arrival carefully to avoid collisions.

## Practical Examples

**Fast Courier** (Earth departure, M3 drive):
- Accelerates continuously for 2.8 hours
- Reaches jump point at 305 km/s
- Jumps while still accelerating

**Merchant Trader** (Jupiter refueling, M2 drive):
- Departs gas giant atmosphere
- Accelerates for 11.6 hours
- Jumps at 826 km/s velocity

**Military Intercept** (Small moon base, M6 drive):
- Emergency launch
- Full burn for 0.9 hours
- Jumps at 201 km/s to pursue target
