## Usage Notes

These tables and references are designed for quick lookup during gameplay:
- Jump distances help determine where ships can safely enter jump space
- Travel time tables assist in calculating journey durations
- Law level explanations help referees adjudicate local restrictions
- Equipment lists provide standard gear availability and costs
