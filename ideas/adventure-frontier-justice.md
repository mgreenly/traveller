# Frontier Justice
*Inspired by The Magnificent Seven/Firefly but on a border colony*

## Concept
The crew answers a distress call from Prosperity, a frontier mining colony on the edge of the Sword Worlds border. The colony is being terrorized by "The Company" - a mining consortium using hired mercenaries to force independent miners off their claims. With the Imperial Navy weeks away and local law enforcement bought off, the colonists pool their meager resources to hire protection. The crew must train the colonists, fortify the settlement, and face off against increasingly dangerous waves of corporate enforcers.

## Key Elements
- The colony sits on rich deposits of a rare mineral used in jump drives
- Mix of Imperial colonists, Sword Worlder immigrants, and Vargr prospectors creates tension
- The Company has legal documents but acquired them through murder and intimidation
- Limited resources force creative solutions: mining equipment as weapons, natural terrain as defense
- A ticking clock: The Company's main force arrives in 10 days

## Memorable NPCs
- **Mayor Sarah Brennan**: Former Imperial Marine, lost her leg in the Fourth Frontier War
- **Jonas Krieger**: Sword Worlder blacksmith who can modify mining equipment into weapons
- **Rrak-Tooth**: Vargr prospector who knows every cave and tunnel in the region
- **Victoria Cross**: The Company's local enforcer, cultured and ruthless
- **"Doc" Chen**: Colony doctor who's actually a former combat medic with a dark past
- **Little Amy**: 12-year-old orphan who becomes the crew's most enthusiastic student

## The Colony Sections
- **Main Street**: The commercial heart with saloon, general store, and makeshift courthouse
- **The Diggings**: Scattered mining claims across brutal terrain
- **Hydroponics Valley**: The colony's food source, vulnerable and vital
- **The Foundry**: Ore processing facility that could be fortified
- **Spaceport**: Just a landing pad and fuel depot, but the only way off-world
- **Old Mine**: Abandoned shaft system that could hide civilians or stage ambushes

## Complications
- Half the colonists want to negotiate, half want to fight
- The Company has a spy among the colonists feeding them information
- A Sword Worlds patrol ship arrives, complicating the political situation
- Some colonists are actually claim jumpers with their own agenda
- The minerals cause hallucinations with prolonged exposure
- Local wildlife becomes aggressive during the planet's storm season

## Training Montage Elements
- Teaching miners to use their tools as weapons
- Building fortifications from mining equipment and local materials
- Creating early warning systems using prospecting sensors
- Training civilians in basic combat tactics
- Establishing supply caches and fallback positions
- Building unity between Imperial, Sword Worlder, and Vargr factions

## The Company's Escalation
1. **Week 1**: Intimidation tactics, sabotage of equipment
2. **Week 2**: Hired thugs attack isolated claims
3. **Week 3**: Professional mercenaries arrive with military equipment
4. **Week 4**: The Company's executive arrives with a full corporate military unit
5. **Final Confrontation**: All-out assault on the colony

## Environmental Factors
- Dust storms that ground aircraft and disable sensors
- Extreme temperature variations between day and night
- Local predators that could be threats or tools
- Unstable mine shafts useful for traps
- Limited water supplies that both sides need
- Radio interference from mineral deposits

## Moral Choices
- Accept a Company bribe to walk away
- Some colonists have committed crimes but are still worth protecting
- Choose between defending outlying claims or consolidating at the main settlement
- Deal with colonists who want to surrender or collaborate
- Decide the fate of captured Company operatives
- Balance keeping the peace versus letting colonists take revenge

## Potential Outcomes
- Victory establishes the colony's independence and the crew's reputation
- Pyrrhic victory: the colony survives but at great cost
- Negotiated settlement where some colonists keep claims, others are bought out
- The crew takes over the colony as its new protectors
- Imperial or Sword Worlds intervention based on crew's actions
- The Company retreats but promises this isn't over

## Resources & Improvised Weapons
- Mining lasers converted to weapons
- Seismic charges used as explosives
- Ore haulers armored as fighting vehicles
- Prospecting drones repurposed for reconnaissance
- Industrial chemistry for improvised explosives
- Mining exoskeletons modified for combat
