# The Regina Job
*Inspired by Ocean's Eleven but set in the Spinward Marches*

## Concept
The crew must infiltrate the Grand Regina Casino & Resort during the annual Sector Trade Summit to steal incriminating data about a corrupt Imperial noble who's been selling military secrets to the Zhodani. The twist: they're actually being set up by Imperial Intelligence as a distraction while the real operation happens elsewhere.

## Key Elements
- Multiple entry vectors: service staff, high-roller guests, entertainers, security contractors
- The casino uses TL-15 security systems but has deliberate TL-11 "aesthetic" areas for nostalgic gamblers
- A rival crew is also attempting the heist, leading to comedic near-misses and potential cooperation
- The "MacGuffin" keeps changing - first it's data, then it's a person, then it's an Ancient artifact
- The crew's patron turns out to be the target's estranged daughter seeking revenge

## Memorable NPCs
- **Duchess Evalina Torren**: The target, a charming sociopath who genuinely likes the crew
- **"Lucky" Chen**: A down-on-his-luck gambler who keeps accidentally helping the crew
- **The Castellan**: The casino's AI that has developed a gambling addiction itself
- **Marcus "The Ghost" Vallen**: Leader of the rival crew, overly dramatic and theatrical

## Complications
- The casino is in zero-g for "Float Night" during the heist
- A Vargr pack celebration is happening in the main gaming floor
- The crew's inside person is actually a triple agent
- The Imperial Navy decides to do a "routine" inspection mid-heist
- The data is stored in a vintage TL-9 computer that no one knows how to operate

## Potential Outcomes
- Success reveals the whole thing was a test by Imperial Intelligence to recruit them
- Failure leads to them owing a massive debt to the casino, setting up future adventures
- They steal the wrong thing entirely but it turns out to be more valuable
- They prevent an actual Zhodani terrorist attack and become heroes

## Setting Details
- Takes place on Regina, the subsector capital
- The casino orbits Regina in a repurposed luxury liner
- Mix of high-tech security and deliberate retro areas creates interesting infiltration challenges
- The Trade Summit brings together merchants, nobles, and spies from across the sector
