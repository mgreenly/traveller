# The Omicron Particle

## Overview
A race against time to prevent an Ancient terraforming system from wiping out all life on an inhabited world in the Spinward Marches. The players must decipher Ancient technology, navigate corporate conspiracies, and potentially evacuate millions before a 300,000-year-old doomsday protocol completes.

## The Hook
While exploring Dr. Aros's data core, the players discover Project Omicron Particle - a quantum key that unlocks dormant Ancient communication nodes. When activated, it revealed a terrifying countdown already in progress.

## The Crisis

### The Ancient Message
*"System Reset Initiated - Contamination Threshold Exceeded - Species 7731 Industrial Markers Detected at Protected Site [DESIGNATION CORRUPTED] - Automatic Reclamation Sequence Commencing in 1,827 Cycles - All Non-Baseline Organic Matter Will Be Reconfigured to Original Template"*

### What Triggered It
- 1,820 "cycles" ago, a human mining operation breached an Ancient "Protected Site"
- The breach triggered an automatic terraforming reset protocol
- The planet will be restored to its ecosystem from 300,000 years ago
- All current life (human, animal, plant) will be "reconfigured"

### The Mystery
- **Unknown Timeline:** What is a "cycle"? Days? Months? Planetary rotations?
- **Unknown Location:** Partial coordinates "...327.4...[CORRUPTED]...Marches..." 
- **Three Candidates:** Mining records narrow it to three possible worlds
- **Warning Signs:** Spectacular atmospheric auroras will begin months before reclamation

## Key Clues

### From the Data Core
1. **Dr. Aros's Research:**
   - Identified 17 dormant Ancient nodes across known space
   - One node exists beneath Regina's capital city
   - Mining scanner frequencies that match Ancient sensors triggered the reset
   - Seven worlds in the Marches have the right mineral signatures

2. **Failed Communications:**
   - "Request Abort Protocol" → "Authority Not Recognized"
   - "Identify Species 7731" → "Invasive. Quarantine Failed. Reclamation Authorized"
   - "Define Original Template" → "[DATA STREAM TOO LARGE - 400 YOTTABYTES]"

3. **The Final Note:**
   - "The auroras should begin soon. We might have months, or mere days."
   - "Need Imperial survey data to identify which world"
   - "The seventeen nodes might hold override authority"

## Investigation Paths

### Path 1: Imperial Records
- Access Imperial Survey Service archives on Regina
- Cross-reference mining operations from 1,820 "cycles" ago
- Identify which world has deep core resonance mining
- Risk: Alerting authorities might cause panic or coverup

### Path 2: The Three Candidates
Players must investigate three possible worlds:
- **World A:** Industrial mining world with recent atmospheric anomalies
- **World B:** Agricultural colony reporting "unusual weather patterns"
- **World C:** Frontier settlement with unexplained equipment failures

### Path 3: Ancient Node Network
- Activate the node beneath Regina (highly illegal)
- Risk triggering other Ancient systems
- Possibility of finding override codes
- Each node activation provides more clues but less time

## Escalating Stakes

### Phase 1: Investigation (Sessions 1-2)
- Strange reports from multiple worlds
- Corporate interests trying to suppress information
- First aurora sightings dismissed as "solar activity"

### Phase 2: Confirmation (Sessions 3-4)
- Identify the doomed world
- Auroras intensify, becoming undeniable
- Evacuation plans deemed "impossible" by authorities
- Population: 2.3 million colonists

### Phase 3: Race Against Time (Sessions 5-6)
- Find Ancient site with override authority
- Navigate active Ancient defenses
- Corporate sabotage (they want the terraforming tech)
- Mass panic beginning on affected world

### Phase 4: The Choice (Session 7)
- Override requires a sacrifice (one consciousness must replace the template)
- Alternative: Trigger evacuation, save who you can
- Wild card: Redirect the reset to an uninhabited world
- Corporate offer: Let it happen, they'll extract "important" people

## Complications

### Corporate Interference
- **Makhidkarun:** Wants to study the process, will prevent interference
- **SuSAG:** Sees opportunity for terraforming contracts
- **Imperial Intelligence:** Section 86 monitoring, may help or hinder

### Technical Challenges
- Ancient technology doesn't recognize human authority
- Each attempt to communicate accelerates the countdown
- The Omicron Particle burns out after too many uses

### Moral Dilemmas
- Save the many or preserve Ancient sites for humanity's future?
- Trust the corporations who claim they can help?
- Risk activating more Ancient systems to save one world?

## NPCs

### Allies
- **Dr. Lyra Zhou:** Xenoarchaeologist who believes Ancients left a failsafe
- **Commander Reyes:** Navy officer willing to bend rules for evacuation
- **The Cultivators:** Mysterious group that knows more than they should

### Antagonists
- **Director Voss (Makhidkarun):** Sees this as the opportunity of a lifetime
- **The Preservation Society:** Fanatics who believe the reset is meant to happen
- **Panicked Colonists:** Desperate people doing desperate things

## Outcomes

### Success
- Override the reset, saving millions
- Gain access to Ancient terraforming knowledge
- Become targets for every corporation and government

### Partial Success
- Evacuate key population centers
- Reset occurs but casualties minimized
- Players branded as heroes or terrorists depending on perspective

### Failure
- World undergoes complete reclamation
- 2.3 million casualties
- Players possess terrible knowledge of other potential reset sites

## Rewards
- Ancient technology (Omicron Particle if not destroyed)
- Coordinates to other Ancient sites
- Massive reputation changes (positive or negative)
- Potential access to Ancient terraforming capabilities

## Referee Notes
- Keep the location ambiguous initially to build tension
- Use news reports and rumors to show escalating crisis
- The "cycle" timing should create urgency without being impossible
- Consider making it personal - someone the players care about is on the doomed world
- The Ancient reset is impartial - it's not evil, just following 300,000-year-old programming
