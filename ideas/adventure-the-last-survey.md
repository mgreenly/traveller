# The Last Survey
*Inspired by Indiana Jones/The Mummy but with Ancient technology*

## Concept
The crew discovers the final survey logs of the legendary scout Jamison Keth, who vanished 200 years ago while investigating Ancient sites in the Darrian subsector. His encrypted data points to an intact Ancient facility that supposedly contains a working matter transmitter. Racing against a Darrian archaeological team, Zhodani psi-corps operatives, and a megacorporation's "recovery specialists," the crew must decode a series of clues across multiple systems.

## Key Elements
- Each clue is hidden in a different environment: underwater cities, gas giant mining stations, asteroid monasteries
- The survey logs are corrupted and partially in Ancient script that changes meaning based on psionic resonance
- Keth left behind a series of tests to ensure only "worthy" explorers could follow his path
- The Ancient facility is actually still occupied by its automated guardians
- Time pressure: a stellar phenomenon will make the final location inaccessible in 30 days

## Memorable NPCs
- **Dr. Kaelen Tash**: Darrian archaeologist who alternates between rival and reluctant ally
- **The Collector**: A mysterious figure buying Ancient artifacts, actually Keth's uploaded consciousness
- **Captain Sara "Sandstorm" Mitchell**: Leader of the corporate team, pragmatic and willing to cooperate
- **Brother Dominic**: A monk who's been guarding one piece of the puzzle for generations
- **XK-7742**: An Ancient robot that thinks the crew are its long-lost maintenance team

## Complications
- Each group has one piece of essential equipment the others need
- The clues were intentionally scattered to prevent any single government from claiming the prize
- Local folklore has turned each clue site into a religious pilgrimage destination
- The matter transmitter only works one way - it's actually an evacuation device
- Keth's descendants have legal claim to anything discovered and they're very litigious

## Archaeological Sites
1. **Gandar's Folly**: A frozen world with Ancient ruins preserved in glaciers
2. **The Lighthouse**: An Ancient beacon still functioning in an asteroid belt
3. **Darkmoon Station**: Built into an Ancient structure, the locals don't know what's behind the sealed sections
4. **The Garden of Memory**: A biodome where Ancient plants react to visitors' thoughts
5. **Terminal Echo**: The final site, phasing in and out of reality

## Potential Outcomes
- The matter transmitter leads to a preserved Ancient colony ship with ethical implications
- Success triggers an Ancient distress beacon, attracting unwanted attention
- They discover Keth is still alive, preserved by Ancient technology but changed
- The facility is a test, and passing it grants access to the Ancient information network
- They must choose between personal gain and preventing dangerous technology from being weaponized

## Puzzle Elements
- Navigation calculations that require understanding Ancient mathematics
- Environmental puzzles that respond to specific actions in sequence
- Social challenges with isolated communities protecting "sacred" sites
- Decoding Keth's personal cipher mixed with Ancient symbols
- Each site contains a fragment of an Ancient star map showing the final location
