# Silent Running
*Inspired by The Hunt for Red October but in deep space*

## Concept
The crew is hired to track down the INS Whisper, an experimental stealth corvette that went dark during its maiden voyage. The ship's crew claims they've defected to protect a secret: they've discovered evidence of a conspiracy within the Imperial Navy to stage a false flag operation that would justify war with the Zhodani Consulate. The crew must navigate a deadly game of cat and mouse through asteroid fields and nebulae while deciding who to trust.

## Key Elements
- The Whisper uses experimental "ghost drive" technology that makes it nearly undetectable
- Multiple factions are hunting the ship: Imperial Navy, Zhodani agents, and corporate interests
- The crew must use old-fashioned tracking methods: gravitational analysis, crew psychology, supply needs
- Communications are compromised - any transmission might reveal their location
- The Whisper's crew is divided - some want to defect, others want to return, some want to disappear

## Memorable NPCs
- **Commander Alexei Volkov**: Whisper's captain, brilliant but paranoid, possibly justified
- **Lt. Commander Priya Singh**: Whisper's XO, secretly working for Naval Intelligence
- **Admiral Marcus Stone**: Officially wants the ship recovered, actually wants it destroyed
- **"Mother" Teresa**: Information broker who knows more than she should
- **Chief Engineer Otto Braun**: The only one who truly understands the ghost drive

## The Hunt Zones
- **The Shoals**: Dense asteroid field with sensor shadows and mining operations
- **Nebula K-7741**: Electromagnetic interference makes standard sensors useless
- **The Graveyard**: Decommissioned ship storage facility, perfect for hiding
- **Ice Field Terminus**: Comet fragments provide cover but are navigationally hazardous
- **Deep Black**: Empty space between systems where a ship could drift forever

## Complications
- The ghost drive is slowly killing the Whisper's crew with exotic radiation
- A second stealth ship is hunting everyone - but whose is it?
- The conspiracy evidence is fragmentary and could be interpreted multiple ways
- The crew's own ship develops a sympathetic malfunction from proximity to the ghost drive
- Local system governments want to capture the technology for themselves
- The Whisper is carrying someone or something else that's the real prize

## Detection Methods
- Analyzing supply depot records for unusual purchases
- Tracking gravitational anomalies from the ghost drive
- Following a trail of dead drops and encrypted messages
- Interviewing the families and associates of Whisper's crew
- Using ancient stellar cartography to predict hiding spots
- Exploiting the ghost drive's need for rare coolant materials

## Moral Dilemmas
- The conspiracy might be real, but exposing it could start a war anyway
- Some of Whisper's crew are innocent of the conspiracy but guilty of mutiny
- The ghost drive technology could revolutionize travel or be weaponized
- Destroying the evidence maintains peace but lets conspirators go free
- The defectors have families being held as leverage

## Environmental Challenges
- Silent running protocols: minimal power usage, no active sensors
- Debris fields that could hide the Whisper or damage pursuing ships
- Time pressure: the ghost drive will fail catastrophically in 15 days
- Communication lag makes coordination with allies difficult
- False sensor ghosts and decoys left by the Whisper

## Potential Outcomes
- The crew brokers a deal where the truth comes out but war is avoided
- They help the Whisper escape to a neutral system, making powerful enemies
- The conspiracy is revealed to be a Zhodani intelligence operation
- They capture the ship but let the crew escape with the evidence
- The ghost drive technology is destroyed to prevent its weaponization
- They discover the Whisper already suffered catastrophic failure and they're chasing echoes

## Chase Mechanics
- Each jump leaves quantum traces that fade over time
- The Whisper must surface periodically for supplies and maintenance
- Crew can split up to cover more search areas but risk being outgunned
- Intelligence gathering in ports and stations provides leads
- The ghost drive has a unique signature only detectable at close range
