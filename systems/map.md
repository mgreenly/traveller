# Subsector Map

This map shows the jump-1 connections between the systems relevant to the "Inheritance" adventure.

```
                  R
                 /
                J     I
                |    /|
                D   W |
                 \ / \|
                  N   R
                       \
                        K
```

## Map Key
*   **R:** [Regina](./regina.md) (1910)
*   **J:** [Jenghe](./jenghe.md) (1810)
*   **D:** [Dinom](./dinom.md) (1811)
*   **N:** [Dinomn](./dinomn.md) (1912)
*   **W:** [Wypoc](./wypoc.md) (2011)
*   **C:** [Rech](./rech.md) (2112)
*   **I:** [Djinni](./djinni.md) (2111)
*   **K:** K'Kirka


## Jump Routes

### Primary Route (Regina to Djinni)
1.  **(R)** to **(J)** - 1 Parsec - Regina to Jenghe
2.  **(J)** to **(D)** - 1 Parsec - Jenghe to Dinom
3.  **(D)** to **(N)** - 1 Parsec - Dinom to Dinomn
4.  **(N)** to **(W)** - 1 Parsec - Dinomn to Wypoc
5.  **(W)** to **(C)** - 1 Parsec - Wypoc to Rech
6.  **(C)** to **(I)** - 1 Parsec - Rech to Djinni
