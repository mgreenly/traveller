# RECH (D9957AA-6)
*   **Sector:** Spinward March
*   **Subsector:** Lanth
*   **Coordinates:** 2112
*   **Astronomics:** Single G-class star (a yellow, Sol-like star). Rech is the third of 8 planets. A stormy gas giant, "Maelstrom," is the sixth planet.
*   **Description:** A large, tainted world with a population in the millions but a poor starport. The most notable feature is its state of anarchy.
*   **World Type:** Large Rocky World (14,400km diameter)
*   **Jump Distance:** 1,440,000 km from center (100-diameter limit)
    *   **M-Drive Travel Times:** M1: 39.7 hours | M2: 28.1 hours | M3: 22.9 hours
*   **UPP (D9957AA-6):**
    *   **Starport:** D (Poor)
    *   **Size:** 9 (14,400km diameter)
    *   **Atmosphere:** 9 (Dense, Tainted)
    *   **Hydrographics:** 5 (46-55% water)
    *   **Population:** 7 (Tens of millions)
    *   **Government:** A (Charismatic Dictator / Anarchy)
    *   **Law Level:** A (Severe: All weapons and armor prohibited in planetary territories) / 3 (Machine guns and assault weapons prohibited in downport district)
    *   **Tech Level:** 6 (Industrial)
*   **Lore:** Rech was not always a lawless backwater. Official records show a UPP of B86889A-B, a prosperous, high-tech world with a population in the billions. That civilization was erased by the "System Quarantine," a catastrophic event triggered by a military-grade data-plague.
    *   **The Collapse:** The virus, deployed by **Makhidkarun** as a scorched-earth tactic after a failed corporate takeover, targeted the planet's sophisticated, networked infrastructure. It bricked everything from power grids to governmental systems, causing a complete technological regression to a pre-network, industrial age (TL-6).
    *   **The Aftermath:** Billions died or fled during the chaos. The survivors were forced to rely on dirty, inefficient industrial technology, leading to the current tainted atmosphere. The digital government was annihilated, leaving a power vacuum filled by factions controlling the remaining physical assets (factories, water purifiers, etc.).
    *   **The Secret History:** Unknown to all but the highest echelons of Makhidkarun, their decision to deploy the virus was subtly engineered by **The Cultivators**. Seeing Rech's stable society as stagnant, the Cultivators fed Makhidkarun falsified intelligence, manipulating them into triggering the collapse to create a more "novel" and chaotic environment.
    *   **The Hot War:** Following the collapse, dozens of factions emerged from the chaos, each controlling vital industrial resources. What began as survival coalitions devolved into brutal territorial warfare as groups fought for control of the crucial downport facilities - the planet's lifeline to off-world resources.
*   **The Treaty of Necessity (1105):** After years of devastating conflict that threatened everyone's survival, the major factions signed a comprehensive peace accord. The treaty created the **Starport Authority** as a neutral organization to control port facilities and established a complex jurisdictional framework.
*   **The Cold War:** Open warfare ended, but tensions remain high. The major factions maintain their territorial Law Level A controls while engaging in constant political maneuvering, espionage, and proxy conflicts through smaller gangs.
*   **The Starport Authority:** Created by treaty as a compromise solution, this neutral organization controls the actual downport and orbital starport facilities. They maintain heavy security, moderately good facilities, and operate under technical Law Level 3 with strict enforcement. The Authority also guarantees safe passage along designated highways through the lawless zone.
*   **The Lawless Zone:** Surrounding the port facilities is a 30 square kilometer area of true Law Level 3 where the Starport Authority does not patrol. Numerous small gangs with constantly shifting boundaries exploit this treaty loophole, making it a hub for smuggling and black market trade. Roads from this zone lead to major faction territories, but the treaty prevents those factions from conducting law enforcement here.
*   **Neighbors:**
    *   **[Djinni](./djinni.md):** North
    *   **[Wypoc](./wypoc.md):** North-West
    *   **K'Kirka:** South-East

### Arrival Experience

Arrival in the Rech system reveals a smog-choked world of industrial grey and polluted brown. The orbital starport, while functional, shows clear signs of factional compromise - different sections bear the markings of various groups, but Starport Authority personnel maintain professional control of operations.

**The Port District:** The actual downport facilities are surprisingly well-maintained, with Starport Authority guards providing visible, professional security. Landing pads are properly marked, and basic services function reliably. However, the heavy security presence and frequent patrols make it clear this order is actively enforced.

**The Lawless Zone:** Beyond the port's security perimeter lies 30 square kilometers of true chaos. This sprawling maze of temporary structures, black markets, and gang territories operates under the simple rule that might makes right. Faction checkpoints, smuggler dens, and constantly shifting territorial boundaries make every venture into this area dangerous.

**Safe Highways:** The Starport Authority maintains secured routes from the port facilities through the lawless zone to the major faction territories. These roads are patrolled and guaranteed safe, but stepping off them means entering gang territory.

### Factions

#### Major Territorial Factions (Law Level A)
*   **The Smelters' Union:** A powerful workers' collective controlling the vast industrial zones and water purification facilities. They maintain brutal discipline within their territory and are known for their sophisticated intelligence network.
*   **The Reclamation Syndicate:** Controls the largest scrap processing facilities and maintains a fleet of salvage vessels. They have significant influence over what technology is permitted on-planet.
*   **The Agricultural Collective:** Despite the tainted atmosphere, they control the sealed hydroponic facilities that feed the population. Their territory includes the cleanest air on the planet.

#### The Starport Authority (Neutral Zone)
*   **The Starport Authority:** Created by the Treaty of Necessity, this neutral organization controls the downport and orbital facilities. They maintain heavy security with professional guards, modern equipment, and strict but fair enforcement. They guarantee safe passage on designated highways and provide the only reliable law enforcement in the port district.

#### Lawless Zone Gangs (True Law Level 3)
*   **The Free-Traders' Alliance:** A loose coalition of independent captains, brokers, and merchants operating in the lawless zone. They maintain black markets and serve as information brokers.
*   **The Rust Runners:** Smugglers specializing in moving restricted technology between faction territories. They know every back route through the zone.
*   **Various Small Gangs:** Dozens of constantly shifting micro-factions control street corners, warehouses, and temporary territories. Their boundaries change weekly based on violence, negotiation, and opportunity.

### Starship Operations

**Berthing Fees:** Class D Starport - Cr500 per week (Starport Authority standard rates, no bribes required in port district)
**Fuel:** Unrefined fuel at Cr100 per ton (refining facilities destroyed in collapse). Refined fuel available through smugglers in lawless zone at Cr200+ per ton
**Maintenance:** Basic maintenance available at port facilities. Advanced parts and services available through black market contacts in lawless zone
**Repairs:** Emergency repairs at port facilities. Major repairs possible through faction contacts but require negotiation
**SECURITY ZONES:**
- **Port District:** Starport Authority maintained, professional security, Law Level 3 strictly enforced
- **Lawless Zone:** No central authority, gang territories, true Law Level 3, extreme caution advised
- **Faction Territories:** Law Level A, all weapons prohibited, require proper documentation
- **Safe Highways:** Starport Authority guaranteed routes, armed escort provided on request
