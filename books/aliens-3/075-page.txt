REHLD COURIER
Using a 100-ton hull, the Rehld courier is a rapid
communications carrier. It requires a crew of
two – a pilot/astrogator and an engineer. The
ship is equipped with highly sensitive, long range
communications gear and oversized jump drives.

The majority of its volume incorporates large
fuel tanks so that several short jumps can be
made sequentially without needing to refuel. The
vessel can also carry two passengers if needed, a
requirement imposed and used by the Special Arm.
