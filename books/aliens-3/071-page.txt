GRAV BIKE

72

A common form of transport on Darrian worlds, grav
bikes are fast and manoeuvrable. Fairings dynamically
reshape to control aerodynamic flow and act as
spoilers for slowing or rapid changes of direction. If a
power failure occurs, the fairings return to a default
wing configuration, allowing the grav bike to glide
safely to the ground. Ejector seats are included for
circumstances where no safe landing is possible. Grav
bikes include life support for high altitude flying and are
capable of reaching orbit.
