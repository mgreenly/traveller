Cargo Handler: Cargo handlers are found in docks,
airports, spaceports and starports across the Third
Imperium, loading and unloading cargo to free
personnel to handle more demanding tasks. Large
and heavy enough to carry a standard one-ton cargo
container, the cargo handler robot can work as long
as is needed and, if properly maintained, has a useful
lifespan of approximately 40 years.
