EFAWEAPE CLASS SCOUT
The Efaweape uses a 100 ton hull and is intended
primarily to explore and identify trade routes. Its
secondary role is the survey of Bwap dominated
worlds and Bwap enclaves on other worlds. It
has three crew; a pilot/astrogator, engineer and a

TL15

merchant/broker. The ship has a large enough cargo
hold to make it a profitable venture for Travellers.
Normally it is unarmed but it has a single hardpoint
on which a turret may be mounted.
