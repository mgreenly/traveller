been designed specifically for use without waldos. A
Dolphin with waldos is able to use any ranged weapon
a human can.
Battering Ram: This weapon functions almost like
brass knuckles. The Dolphin variety is held in the
mouth, clenched between the teeth.

sound and focusing it into a tight beam.
