DA-WE-PA WAR WALKER

War walkers are a popular vehicle among the
Guardians of Order, used in both warfare and policing.
The relatively light frame causes less damage to the
environment than larger vehicles. War walkers are
armed appropriately for the task they are assigned.
Those used in combat have a heavier loadout, with an
example fitout below. Those which are used for policing
duties will have lighter armament, with most armed with
non-lethal weapons.
