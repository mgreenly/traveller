EAAETATKA-KA CLASS MONITOR
A compliment of eight Apa-pakwa fighters, piloted
by the marine contingent, provides additional
offensive and defensive capabilities to this
already impressive monitor.

The Eaaetatka-ka class monitor is the mainstay of
Bwap in-system defence and are frequently seen
patrolling busy spacelanes around populated worlds.
Built around a 5,000 ton hull, the heavily armoured
vessel bristles with weapons, making it an intimidating
sight. Boasting four large missile bays, four fusion
barbettes and 16 triple laser turrets, the Eaaetatka-ka
is an equal to any starship its size and poses a serious
threat to starships twice or even three times its size.

TL15

Each Eaaetatka-ka possesses a command bridge
as they are often assigned to lead a squadron
of up to six system defence boats on extended
system patrols.
