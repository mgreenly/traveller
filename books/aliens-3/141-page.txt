ADRAT SCOUT
These scouts are the most common starship found
within ADRAT, their main mission to search for lost
advanced technology. Each ship has a fully equipped
laboratory large enough for two researchers and the
computer is programmed to assist in research. A
detachable base module allows crew to establish a

temporary research facility on the surface of a planet,
freeing the scout for other duties and the vault is a
security measure used to protect valuable finds. The
scout has a nominal crew of three but has enough
staterooms for a scientist and can accommodate a
total of eight individuals if double bunking.
