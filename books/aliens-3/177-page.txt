Sea Shepherd: The sea shepherd is a dolphinoid robot
that Dolphins use to assist them in herding and guarding
schools of marine life. Several sea shepherds will work
with a single Dolphin, responding to commands much
like a Terran sheepdog to a farmer. Sea shepherds are
also capable of guarding schools of marine life and
fighting off predators. These robots are quite capable at
the tasks they were designed for, and able to respond
to quite complex commands, but are easily confused if
placed in situations they are not programmed for.
