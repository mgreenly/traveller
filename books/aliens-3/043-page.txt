Skills and training

Choose one of the following:
Advocate: You work in the law courts trying to negotiate
solutions between conflicting opinions or establish mutually
agreeable punishments to those found guilty of committing a crime.
Diplomat: You are sent to settle issues with foreign powers or
between planetary governments and the Confederation Ministries.
You are the voice of reason, trained to defuse tension and petty
bickering, to cement an atmosphere of reasonable compromise.
Observer: You watch the activities of those who have agreed
to conciliation to ensure all members complete their part of the
settlement. You also act as a judge of character, discovering the
true purpose of organisations or individuals.
