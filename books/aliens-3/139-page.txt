FUELLING SHUTTLE
Fuelling shuttles are essentially large flying fuel
tanks. A streamlined hull assists in providing lift
whilst skimming a gas giant for hydrogen before
delivering its payload to a mother vessel for

processing. Fuelling shuttles are often found on
larger starships incapable of skimming fuel for
themselves, although some are also found at
starports and orbital fuelling depots.
