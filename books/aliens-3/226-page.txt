VEHICLE WEAPONS
All weapons described in this section require the Heavy
Weapons (vehicle) skill to use effectively. All ranges are
listed in kilometres.
Vehicle Weapon Harness: The vehicle weapon
harness is designed to mount vehicle weapons and is
specifically reinforced to absorb any recoil. The harness
is controlled in a similar manner as the weapon harness
but only one vehicle weapon of up to two tons may be
mounted on it.
