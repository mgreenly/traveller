SURVIVAL GEAR
Darrians have a number of devices designed to enable
them to survive on worlds outside the Confederation.
Exoskeleton: These ubiquitous devices are used
by Darrians travelling to worlds with a Size of two or
more above their homeworld, until their physiology
can adapt. The exoskeleton supports their fragile bone
structure against the rigours of higher gravity, providing
additional strength to pick up objects that weigh more
in higher gravity environments, adding STR+2 for the
purposes of lifting objects only.
