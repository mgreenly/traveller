Darrians living in the Imperium or other humandominated societies may use careers in the
Traveller Core Rulebook instead. Most such
individuals are exiles or children of exiles.
However, many Darrians decide to leave the
safety of the Confederation and seek their
fortunes elsewhere. For example, while rare,
it is not unknown for individual Darrians to
join a branch of the Imperial military or civil
administrations. This service is accepted by the
Confederation, because of the experiences and
different viewpoints that are brought back when
they eventually return.
