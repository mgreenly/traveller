KRAN SYSTEM TORPEDO BOAT
The Kran STB is found almost exclusively in
Geonee systems. At 2,000 tons and with no jump
drive, the Kran can prove to be a valid threat to
vessels up to three times its size and in groups can
threaten much larger craft. The heavy torpedo and
missile load is designed to provide an intimidating

display as well as provide an overwhelming attack.
The Kran is often stationed underwater or below
the clouds of a gas giant, relying on powerful
thrusters to take it where it is most needed as
quickly as possible.
