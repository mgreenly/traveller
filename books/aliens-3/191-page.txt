VOID JUMPER TRANSPORT
Built on a 2,000 ton frame, the Void Jumper is
commonly used to transport Dolphin military units.
The vessel is well-armed but primarily intended
for defensive purposes only and is unable to

protect itself in protracted battles. This ship can
also be used in a civilian capacity once weapons
have been removed, often transporting Dolphin
colonists to new worlds.
