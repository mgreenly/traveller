TAGOR FAST DESTROYER
The Tagor is based on the spaceframe of the
Gormandoacy-class, which is the predecessor
for the Midu Agashaam-class destroyer. The
Gormandoacy has been considered obsolete for
some time and when several were made available
by the Imperial Navy, the Geonee Self Defence Navy
purchased as many as they could.

The process of refitting and upgrading the class
proved successful and in almost every way the
Togor is far superior. While the main weaponry was
saved, laser turrets were upgraded along with the
manoeuvre drive, creating a fast response vessel.
Currently the Geonee Self Defence Navy have five
Tagors in their fleet but hope to increase the number
as more Gormandoacy vessels are removed from
service in favour of the Midu Agashaam.
