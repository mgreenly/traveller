TECH LEVEL

The Tech Level on Sufren is a mature TL15, verging on
TL16, which is one of the highest in the subsector.
Sufren’s medical technology, particularly in the fields of
genetic manipulation and engineering are at an early
TL16 level of sophistication due to research made
available by GenAssist, which has major facilities in the
system. Genetic therapy and modification for not only
congenital issues but cosmetic desires, is easily and
readily available and genetic engineering is available
for all manner of applications. Anagathics are freely
available, although remain expensive.
