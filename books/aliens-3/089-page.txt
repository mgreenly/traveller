LDIL PASSENGER LINER
The modestly sized Ldil is the backbone of long haul
passenger transport throughout the Confederation.
Like most small Darrian vessels, the Ldil is designed
to be capable of landing on a planetary surface if

required. The vessels cater solely to passenger
cruising, lacking any significant capacity to trade
merchandise on the side.
