PIHOLZ SYSTEM DEFENCE BOAT
The Piholz SDB is a vicious ship and the backbone of
many Darrian system defence squadrons. As it has no
jump drive, the majority of its volume is dedicated to
heavy armament and shielding, enabling it to pack a

heavy punch while taking a serious beating. The ship
has been over-engineered to allow it to lurk under water
or deep in the atmosphere of gas giants, using the
environment to shield it from sensors and weapons fire.
