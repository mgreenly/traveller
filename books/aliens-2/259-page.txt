Checking for Advancement

Each term, a Hiver must check for advancement. The
more driven individuals call it that, whereas ordinary
citizens of a nest tend to think of it in terms of status,
which ‘just happens’ rather than being sought out. To
see if a Hiver is advanced or reduced in status, make an
Average (8+) RES check with any positive or negative
modifiers for qualification or other factor such as an
unsuccessful nest transition manipulation. The Effect of
this check is added to a 2D roll on the table below.

Senior and Adult status can change each term. Once
attained, Manipulator status is for life. A Hiver who goes
straight from Adult to Manipulator also gains the bonus for
being a Senior in their nest type – and also gains a place
on all manner of watch-lists.
Permanent Manipulator status is gained by achieving the
status a second time in succession. It is possible for a
Hiver to fall from Manipulator status and regain it, perhaps
multiple times, but only those who gain the status and
keep it in their next term are Manipulators-for-Life.

MUSTERING OUT

At the end of its service, a Hiver gains two rolls on the
Mustering Out Benefits table per term served, plus any
additional benefits gained in the course of Traveller
creation, plus or minus the Hiver’s Resolve DM. Half
(round down) of a Hiver’s benefits must be taken as
rolls on the cash table. This is more than most other
races get, reflecting the fact that a successful Hiver
will have played the system from the moment they
became a yearling.
Cash and benefits are determined by an unmodified 2D
roll, and can actually be negative. Debts are assumed
not to have caught up with the Hiver yet. Clever
manipulations might deflect it permanently, or the Hiver
might keep moving to stay ahead. The debt is probably
owed to the Hiver’s nest but could be the result of some
other activity.
