FLYING-ARMOUR

Flying-armour is composed of a light but highly resilient
fabric stretched between rods that mostly parallel major
bones. It includes a solid helmet. Flying-armour allows
normal use of the wings, which are unprotected, and
is primarily intended to protect against the effects of
crashing into foliage or similar obstacles. Particularly
daredevil Droyne will sometimes crash headlong through
small branches, opening their wings at the last second.
Although risky, this allows an attack to be made by
surprise or pursuit evaded.
