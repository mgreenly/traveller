The Thalassa-class is a standard small
transport and passenger ship in use
throughout the Confederation but quite
rare outside it. Imperial databases
designate it Type SA, which translates
roughly as ‘Solomani equivalent to the
Type A free trader’. This designation is
reasonably accurate when referring to
some variants of the class but others are
sufficiently different that they need to be
classified differently. Most ships are named
after seas, with the concept interpreted
creatively. Thus there are vessels named
for rivers, lakes and even ponds, and also
‘seas’ of the Lunar sort.
