The Siyoparttwi-type, as non-Droyne
observers designate vessels of this sort, is
named after the first example encountered.
It is more commonly referred to as a ‘Droyne
Scout’, although there are several vessels
that fit this rather loose designation.

HULL POINTS

The Siyoparttwi is a utilitarian craft
normally employed by Sports or
small groups of Droyne to carry out
communications, diplomatic and
reconnaissance missions. Examples
have also been sighted in the hands of
a Kroyloss and even orphaned Droyne,
making this one of the most common
Droyne vessels encountered by outsiders
and also possibly creating a false
impression of Droyne culture.
