The UB-75 is built on the same flattenedcylinder hull design as its smaller cousin
and is similar in performance. It is
normally set up as a cargo shuttle with
space for a handful or rather cramped
passengers. The vessel can be run by
a single pilot but normally carries a
technician/co-pilot for shuttle duty.
When running other errands, the UB-75
may gain additional personnel. In this
configuration the cabin space is used by
the crew as a working area.
