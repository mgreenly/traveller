DROYNE LIFE

EVENTS

Droyne do not use the standard Life Events system.
Instead, a Droyne Traveller rolls 2D, with their caste
number as a positive DM, at the end of every term.
On a result of 10+, roll on the Droyne Life Events
table. Unusual events are the only way most Droyne
can develop Contacts, Rivals, Allies or Enemies
outside the Oytrip.

AGING

Droyne become adults soon after casting, which
occurs at age 12, and begin aging at age 24. Aging is
handled the same as for humans; roll 2D at the end
of the fourth term and every term thereafter, with a
negative DM equal to the number of terms served.
