The Thousand Freighter is an in-system
vessel built around a 1000-ton cargo
hold; all other functions are dedicated to
moving this hold and its contents around
the system. The vessel is normally operated
by a small crew and typically just one or
two passengers will be aboard on any given
trip. More can be carried if the crew move
into double-occupancy but most Thousand
Freighters have one or two ‘guest rooms’
and fill the rest up with the belongings of
the crew, spare parts and whatever else is
lying around. These ships have a reputation
for being a very basic form of transport but
they move cargo cheaply and efficiently,
which is all their operators really care about.
