The term ‘dromedary’ is given to support
vessels that carry both fuel and dry stores,
typically used to support small squadron
or task force operations where a fleet
tanker and auxiliaries would be wasteful.
Dromedaries are inefficient when used to
support major fleets but are often pressed
into that role. Most replenishment tasks are
undertaken directly with the ship’s UNREP
system but there is hangar space for six
100-ton craft such as Ilauus-class shuttles,
which can be used for resupply tasks.
The Shuigabumudkhar-class is large enough
to resupply destroyers but is not sufficient
for cruiser or capital ship operations. In
peacetime, dromedaries can sometimes
be encountered ferrying cargo on behalf of
Imperial armed forces, acting as additional
resupply ships for army deployments or
undertaking other mundane tasks.
