DARRIAN

space, with access requiring either passage through
the Sword Worlds Confederation (not always possible)
or transit via an arm of the Spinward Main and into the
subsector from Vilis-Querion or District 268-Five Sisters.

centring on Ilium and Mire, the original area into which
