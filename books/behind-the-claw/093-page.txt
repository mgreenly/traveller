DESCRIPTION
The staple civilian air transport in
the Sword Worlds, the Hnaefir is a
simple design with two ideas in mind
– being cheap and being sturdy. The militarised
version provides little protection, has less in
the way of sensors and somewhat ‘unforgiving’
controls, but can ferry half a Confederation unit
and their gear much quicker than an equivalent
ground vehicle. Some examples in military
service sacrifice their cargo space for a forwardfacing fixed mount capable of mounting a small
(1 Space) weapon.
