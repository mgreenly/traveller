or transport vessel optimised for low-cost
haulage. Although it has spare cabins when
running with a small crew, it is rare to
find this vessel carrying passengers in the
normal manner. More commonly, the extra
cabin space is used for the patron of a
charter (or their representative), or as extra
living space for the crew.
