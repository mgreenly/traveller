INAR

F

The 36 star systems of Inar lie entirely within
the Imperium, with most forming part of a large
concentration sprawling across both Inar and Sabine.
Inar itself, the subsector capital, is not part of this

concentration but has easy jump-2 access to it by way of
Pecena. Indeed, jump-2 communications with the rest
of the sector and beyond are good.
