LAMAS

The Lamas subsector is sometimes referred to as
‘Pretoria’s headache’. About half of its 31 star systems
lie outside the Imperial border, with varying attitudes
towards the Imperium. These attitudes shift over time
and can be heavily influenced by the activates of
raiders, rebels or forces that attempt to counter them.
