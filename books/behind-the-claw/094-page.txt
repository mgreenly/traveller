The Confederation has dozens of these
ships in tours around the subsector at all
times, their sensors sweeping for threats.
When enemies are found, the Sleipnir is
a more than capable small-engagement
vessel that can handle auxiliary craft and
small warships – even Imperial ones.
There is little comfort inside these ships,
especially when conscripted to hold
marines or kommandos under the low
ceiling and amidst crash-webbing and
storage racks. It is not used for full-scale
battles against battlecruisers or other
massive foes but can hold its own against
more common threats stumbled upon
during patrols.
