Built on the same hull as the cargo variant,
the passenger lighter is an interface craft
designed to move personnel from port
to starship. It has a small amount of
living space and some cargo capacity but
otherwise facilities are basic. The crew will
usually include a steward who looks after
the passengers during their flight
to and from their ship. To accommodate
this extra crewmember the craft has a
second stateroom.
