PRETORIA
The Pretoria subsector contains 31 star systems, of
which all but three are Imperial territory. The Imperial
Coreward border runs diagonally across the subsector,
with a mix of primarily human and Vargr worlds beyond.
This border has traditionally been troubled, with the
tumultuous politics of the Vargr Extents spilling into
Imperial territory.
