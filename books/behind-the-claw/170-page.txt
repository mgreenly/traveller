This trader is typical of Gl’lu design and
many like it can be found in the Sabine
and Inar subsectors. Noticeably larger
than a free trader, its size is necessary
to compete with comparative merchants
from the Imperium, due to the notorious
design features of the Gl’lu. While cramped
inside, the trader is supremely reliable
and a well-maintained vessel can keep on
plying trade routes for decades at a time.
