The Brilliance class is a long liner, a highjump passenger vessel with very little cargo
capacity. It is built on an 800 ton hull to
a very high standard, with an impressive
jump-4 performance that allows it to travel
directly between important worlds. Long
Liners typically follow the x-boat routes
or ply between major political or trade
centres, although some are available for
charter. Others are used for VIP personnel
transfers or to host small conferences, with
common areas configured for discussion,
presentations or planning sessions.
Passenger accommodation is extremely
luxurious, with the very best food and
drink prepared by skilled stewards. It is not
uncommon for liners to provide specialist
entertainment or education on for a voyage,
often tailoring the choice to the passengers’
preferences. A voyage between two systems
might be a chance to meet a famous author
or former sports personality, take a course
from a high-end artist or enjoy performances
by professional entertainers.
