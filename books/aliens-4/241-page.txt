CLASS: GRATHRISS

The Grathriss serves two roles in the Tezcat navy –
as a combat ship and customs vessel. As a combat
ship, several escort fighters normally accompany
the gunboat to protect it from aggressors but as a
customs ship, Grathriss normally travel in groups of
two or three. The staterooms and large fuel capacity
allow it to perform long patrols or remain hidden
