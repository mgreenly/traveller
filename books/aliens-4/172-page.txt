FAMILY TRADER

CLASS: LUAXWRAUA
Normally, three of the vessel’s staterooms are
combined into a communal stateroom for the
family to sleep in. Another stateroom is set aside
as maternity quarters for a female. The remaining
four staterooms are designed for passengers,
containing either single or double bunks.

The Luaxwraua-class family trader is as common in
the Hive Federation as free traders are in the Third
Imperium. The vessel is, as the name suggests,
normally crewed by a family of Gurvin.
