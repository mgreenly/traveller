LIGHT FIGHTER DRONE

CLASS: KLARONT

The Klaront-class light fighter drone is a small fast
vessel designed for making quick strikes deep within
enemy lines or used as a defensive screen against
missile ordnance for larger ships. Bearing moderate
armour and armed with a single fixed-mount beam
laser, the Klaront is designed as an expendable

TL14

asset. Equipped with high thrust engines, the
drone can dedicate some of its manoeuvrability to
avoid incoming fire while effectively undertaking its
assigned mission. If communications are cut with
the operator, the fighter does not have the ability to
operate independently.
