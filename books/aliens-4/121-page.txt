NUDAONSK HEAVY TANK DRONE
The Nudaonsk is the heaviest ground drone used by
the Za’tachk military machine. Constructed to have the
likeness of a huge Za’tachk, the tank is supported on
four legs, making it surprisingly nimble for its size. Two
heavy laser cannons slung across the drone’s back
provide significant firepower, while two light laser guns
on turrets can damage opposing infantry considerably.
