VEHICLES OF THE ZA'TACHK
ANTHA RECON DRONE
The Antha Recon Drone is most often used as a
surveillance device. With its stealth technology and
long-range sensors, the drone can observe an area
from some distance without being spotted. The
drone is equipped with two hardpoints but is rarely
armed. The Za’tachk armed forces are not above
using Antha drones armed with razor missiles as a
tool for assassination.
