MEDIUM FIGHTER DRONE

CLASS: IESTYNIN

The Iestynin-class medium fighter drone is designed
to provide a secondary screen of defence for larger
vessels, attacking any craft or missiles which evade
the first screen provided by light fighter drones.
Heavily armoured and equipped with two fixed

TL14

mount beam lasers, the craft is still intended as a
disposable commodity for the craft they defend.
With no cockpit or robot brain, the drone requires a
remote operator to function and becomes useless if
communications are jammed or cut.
