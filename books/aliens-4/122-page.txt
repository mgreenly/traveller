ULMINIXON LIGHT AIRBORNE
ATTACK DRONE
The Ulminixon light airborne attack drone is the ariel
superiority vehicle of choice among Za’tachk armed
forces. The vehicle is lightly armed but, in the hands
of a competent operator, is manoeuvrable enough to
avoid enemy fire. Armed with a gatling laser to two
linked hardpoints, the Ulminixon can be equipped
for many mission types. The drone is customarily
deployed in threes.
