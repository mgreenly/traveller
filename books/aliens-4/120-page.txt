IDINPYX HEAVY AIRBORNE
ATTACK DRONE
The Idinpyx is an armoured drone used primarily to
soften targets before heavy ground artillery and infantry
arrives. Armed with missiles, the drone is also armed
with a linked pair of gatling lasers which can be used
to defend itself from other aerial opponents. Grav lifters
allow the drone to operate regardless of atmosphere,
and even into orbit if necessary. These drones are
normally used in threes.
