BOARDING SKIFF

CLASS: RASHKAAHT

The Rashkaaht boarding skiff is intended to swiftly
transport troops to enemy ships and board them,
forcibly if necessary. It has enough armour to
provide some protection for the maximum of 40

troops it can transport. The Rashkaaht is capable
of defending itself to a small degree with a single
beam laser turret but is normally accompanied by
several fighters for additional protection.
