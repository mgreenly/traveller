HEAVY VACC SUIT

Heavy vacc suits are designed for use in hostile environments,
much like the human HEV suit. The suya’uiyekhva includes powered
actuators to aid the wearer’s movements, as well as a limited builtin artificial intelligence to advise male wearers on correct operation.
It offers 12 hours of life support, although an Aslan may not use a
dewclaw while wearing this armour.
