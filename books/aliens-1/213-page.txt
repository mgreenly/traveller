VARGR COMBAT ARMOUR

Vargr combat armour is a complete armour suit primarily
used by military divisions but obtained with relative ease by
civilians. This combat armour offers the same functionality as
the Vargr combat vacc suit but is significantly lighter and has
greater protection. It comes with the TL12 electronics suite
detailed on page 19 of the Central Supply Catalogue.
