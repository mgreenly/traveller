PASSENGER SHUTTLE
Intended for routine passenger transport this shuttle
fills the need at a reasonable price point. Capable of
carrying up to 240 passengers.
The shuttle has a small cargo bay, passenger area
and crew section seperated by internal bulkheads.

interact with the passengers except to pass on
