## Chapter Summaries

### Introduction (Pages 3-7)
Overview of Traveller as a science fiction RPG set in the Third Imperium of Charted Space. Covers campaign types (Trader, Military, Explorer), basic game conventions including dice notation (2D, D66), rule zero, and Tech Levels 0-15. Essential foundation for understanding the game's scope and philosophy.

### Traveller Creation (Pages 8-57)
Complete life-path character creation system where players guide 18-year-olds through career terms. Includes characteristic generation, background skills, career paths (Agent, Army, Citizen, Drifter, Entertainer, Marines, Merchant, Navy, Noble, Rogue, Scholar, Scout), advancement, mishaps, aging, and character connections. Emphasizes group creation philosophy.

### Skills and Tasks (Pages 58-72)
Core task resolution using 2D+modifiers against target numbers (usually 8+). Details skill levels, specialties, characteristic checks, difficulty levels, extended actions, and comprehensive descriptions of all available skills from Admin to Survival. Essential reference for action resolution during play.

### Combat (Pages 73-79)
Fast-paced personal combat emphasizing weapon lethality. Covers initiative, combat rounds (6 seconds), actions, range bands, cover, damage, ambushes, tactics, grappling, and healing. Designed to be dangerous and encourage tactical thinking over direct confrontation.

### Encounters and Dangers (Pages 80-96)
Non-combat dangers including diseases, poisons, environmental hazards (gravity, atmosphere, radiation), and alien animal encounters. Features detailed animal generation rules, behavior patterns, and encounter tables for creating dangerous worlds beyond simple combat.

### Equipment (Pages 97-135)
Comprehensive catalog from primitive tools to high-tech devices. Covers weapons (melee, ranged, explosives), armor, survival gear, electronics, medical equipment, and tools. Includes Tech Levels, mass, cost, statistics, weapon customization, and Credits currency system.

### Vehicles (Pages 136-148)
Vehicle operation rules covering movement, speed bands, terrain effects, and combat. Sample vehicles from carts to grav tanks with performance statistics. Special rules for different vehicle types and planetary condition interactions.

### Spacecraft Operations (Pages 149-163)
Daily starship operations including ownership, mortgages, maintenance (0.1% annually), fuel consumption, jump travel, astrogation, refueling, repairs, crew requirements, passengers, cargo, and ship economics. Essential for space-faring campaigns.

### Space Combat (Pages 164-175)
Spacecraft combat using 6-minute rounds and crew positions. Covers initiative, maneuvers, weapons, damage, boarding actions, long-range battles, dogfighting, and crew duties (pilot, gunner, engineer). Emphasizes teamwork importance.

### Spacecraft Construction (Pages 176-187)
Complete ship design system for vessels up to 5,000 tons. Hull selection, armor, drives, weapons, equipment, costs, construction timeframes, and design examples. Allows creation of custom ships from traders to warships.

### Common Spacecraft (Pages 188-227)
Pre-designed ship catalog with full statistics, deck plans, and operational notes. Free traders, patrol ships, military vessels, civilian craft, older ship rules, quirks, and modifications. Ready-to-use vessels for campaigns.

### Psionics (Pages 228-237)
Complete psionics system: telepathy, telekinesis, clairvoyance, awareness, teleportation. Psionic Strength characteristic, power costs, training, social/legal implications in the Imperium, and campaign integration rules.

### Trade (Pages 238-245)
Commerce system covering passenger types (high, middle, basic, low passage), freight hauling, speculative trade, broker rules, cargo types, trade goods, and profit calculations. Provides income and adventure opportunities through trade.

### World and Universe Creation (Pages 246-261)
World generation using Universal World Profile format. Sector mapping, starport types, government forms, law levels, population, atmosphere, hydrography, trade codes, cultural quirks, bases, trade routes, and travel codes (Amber/Red zones).

### Index (Pages 262-265)
Comprehensive alphabetical reference for quick rule lookups during play.
