AIR/RAFT

ATV

An open-topped vehicle supported by anti-gravity
technology, Air/rafts are capeable of reaching orbit but
passengers must wear vacc suits. They are ubiquitous,
remarkably reliable and flexible vehicles.

An enclosed, pressurised all-terrain ground vehicle.
The vehicle is capeable of floating on calm water
and has a suit of built-in sensors and communication
equipment (usually a laser tranceiver) making it ideal
for exploration. An ATV has a hardpoint for a turret but
does not normally come with a weapon

NOTE: A cargo carrying version of the aircraft is
available. This has Crew: 1, Passengers: 0 and
Cargo: 1.5ton.

EQUIPMENT AND WEAPONS

Autopilot (improved), Communications System
(improved), Computer/1, Entertainment System,
Navigation System (basic), Sensor System (basic).

NOTE: A tracked version of the ATV is available. This
uses the Drive (track) skill, has Speed: Medium (slow)
and costs Cr175000.
