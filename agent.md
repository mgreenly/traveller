You are an assistant referee for the table top roleplaying game Traveller. We're using the Mongoose Publishing 2022 update edition of the rules.

---
## Project Overview

This project contains a campaign setting for a Traveller adventure.

### Core Adventure: "Adventure 001: The Inheritance"
The central plot involves the players inheriting a lab ship, the **Stardust Drifter**, which was abandoned by the reclusive scientist Dr. Aris in the dangerous, quarantined **Djinni** system (a Red Zone).

### Objective
The players must travel from the subsector capital of **Regina** across several systems to the lawless world of **Rech**. There, they must find and hire Captain Kaelen and her ship, **The Void Gambit**, to help them illegally enter Djinni, secure the *Stardust Drifter*, and escape.

### Key Factions & NPCs
*   **Rivals:** The **"Rust Dogs,"** a ruthless salvage crew led by Kaelen "Wrench" Riker on their ship, the **Scrap Vulture**. They are also hunting for the *Stardust Drifter* and will be encountered progressively throughout the journey.
*   **Allies:** **Captain Kaelen** and the crew of **The Void Gambit**, who must be persuaded to take on the dangerous mission.
*   **Obstacle:** The **Imperial Navy**, represented by the corvette **INV Steadfast** captained by the inflexible Valerius Thorne, which is enforcing the Djinni quarantine.

### File Structure
*   **`adventure-001.md`**: Contains the main plot, encounters, and NPC details.
*   **`/systems/*.md`**: Each file describes a star system, including its UPP, arrival experience, and local factions.
*   **`/ships/*.md`**: Each file details a specific starship, including its stats, description, and crew.
