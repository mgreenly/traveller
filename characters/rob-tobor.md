# Rob Tobor

*   **Role:** Lab Assistant, Keeper of Secrets
*   **Full Name:** Robert "Rob" Tobor
*   **Age:** 43 standard years
*   **Description:** The perfect counterbalance to Dr. Aros's chaotic genius - methodical where Aros is abstract, practical where Aros is theoretical, and surprisingly empathetic where Aros is distant. Rob serves as translator between Aros's exponential intellect and the mundane world, often finishing the doctor's sentences or explaining his theories in comprehensible terms.

## Background
*   **Origin:** Former Imperial Navy engineer who suffered a catastrophic decompression accident that left him with extensive cybernetic replacements - nearly 40% of his body is now synthetic, including his left arm, both legs below the knee, and several internal organs.
*   **Education:** Masters in Applied Engineering and Xenotechnology Integration. Self-taught in multiple fields to keep up with Dr. Aros's research.
*   **How They Met:** Rob was assigned to investigate Dr. Aros's "unsafe" laboratory practices. Instead of shutting him down, Rob became fascinated by the work and resigned his commission to become Aros's assistant.

## Personality & Traits
*   **Demeanor:** Unflappably calm with a dry sense of humor. Has developed an almost supernatural ability to anticipate Dr. Aros's needs, often preparing equipment for experiments the doctor hasn't even conceived yet.
*   **Hidden Depths:** Despite his mechanical precision, Rob is deeply philosophical. His near-death experience and subsequent augmentation led him to question the nature of consciousness - a perfect complement to Aros's research.
*   **Loyalty:** Absolutely devoted to Dr. Aros, not out of mere employment but genuine respect and a shared vision. He's one of the few people who can challenge Aros's theories without triggering paranoid reactions.
*   **The Altaira Connection:** Rob was fond of Altaira, serving as a surrogate uncle figure. Her death affected him deeply, and he's the one who ensures Aros takes care of himself during his guilt-driven research binges.

## Unique Abilities
*   **Cybernetic Advantages:** His augmentations allow him to interface directly with Ancient technology in ways baseline humans cannot. His synthetic neurons can process Ancient data streams without the psychological damage that affects organic minds.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Has developed an uncanny ability to spot patterns in Aros's seemingly random research notes, often finding connections the doctor himself missed.
*   **Emergency Protocols:** Programmed his own cybernetics with "survival overrides" after Altaira's death - his systems will automatically take control if they detect Ancient defense mechanisms activating.

## Current Status
*   **Last Known Location:** Disappeared with Dr. Aros, but left behind subtle clues only the player characters might recognize - a pattern of purchased supplies that suggests they're heading toward the Zhodani border.
*   **Hidden Messages:** Has been leaving encrypted messages in routine maintenance logs of the *Stardust Drifter*, readable only by someone who knows his old Navy service number.
*   **The Contingency:** Unknown to Aros, Rob has been maintaining a "dead man's switch" - if he doesn't check in every 30 days, a data packet will be sent to [Ash](../players/ash.md) containing Aros's real research notes and a warning about "Project Prometheus."

## Quirks & Habits
*   **Coffee Ritual:** Makes Dr. Aros's coffee exactly 3.7 minutes before the doctor realizes he wants it, having memorized his biorhythms over the years.
*   **Tool Organization:** Arranges tools not alphabetically or by size, but by the probability of Aros needing them, constantly adjusting based on current research.
*   **Speech Pattern:** Often speaks in technical specifications when stressed, a holdover from his Navy days. "Doctor's stress levels approaching critical threshold. Implementing caffeine protocol in T-minus-30 seconds."
*   **The Notebook:** Keeps a physical paper notebook (anachronistic in the high-tech environment) where he writes letters to Altaira, updating her on her father's work.

## Relationships
*   **[Dr. Aros](./dr-aros.md):** More than an employer - Rob understands Aros in ways no one else can. He serves as confessor, caretaker, and the only person who can pull Aros back from his obsessive spirals.
*   **The Players:** If encountered, Rob would evaluate them based on whether they're worthy of Aros's legacy. He might test them with puzzles or challenges that mirror Aros's thinking patterns.
*   **Corporate Contacts:** Still maintains connections from his Navy days, using them to stay one step ahead of corporate trackers.

## Secrets
*   **The Real Experiment:** Rob suspects that Dr. Aros's research into Ancient consciousness transference isn't just theoretical - he believes Aros may have partially succeeded in uploading Altaira's consciousness from Ancient machinery that recorded her final moments.
*   **Cybernetic Evolution:** His augmentations have begun self-modifying after exposure to Ancient technology, developing capabilities neither he nor their manufacturers intended.
*   **The Override:** Has a hidden protocol that would allow him to take complete control of the *Stardust Drifter* if he believes Dr. Aros has become a danger to himself or others.

## Quotes

*   "Altaira once told me that her father's greatest weakness was his inability to see the human cost of knowledge. I've been trying to be his conscience ever since."
*   "My biological components fear death. My synthetic ones have already experienced it. The dichotomy is... instructive."
*   "The doctor says 'impossible' at least six times before breakfast. By lunch, he's usually proven himself wrong."

## Game Notes
*   **If Encountered:** Rob would initially observe the party from hiding, testing their intentions and capabilities before revealing himself.
*   **Potential Ally:** Could become a valuable source of information about Aros's research and the true nature of the Ancient technology.
*   **Walking Database:** His cybernetic memory contains complete records of all Aros's experiments, including the ones that "officially" never happened.
*   **The Beacon:** His cybernetics emit a unique signature that certain Ancient sites recognize, potentially opening sealed areas or triggering dormant systems.
