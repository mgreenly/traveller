# Altaira

*   **Role:** The Lost Daughter, Living Ghost, Key to Immortality
*   **Full Name:** Altaira Aros
*   **Status:** Unknown (Presumed dead, potentially preserved in multiple forms)
*   **Former Profession:** Xenobiologist specializing in Ancient biological systems
*   **Age at Incident:** 28 standard years
*   **Description:** Brilliant and intuitive scientist who inherited her father's intellect but retained the emotional intelligence he lacked. She had an uncanny ability to understand Ancient technology through pattern recognition rather than pure analysis.

## Background
*   **Education:** Doctorate in Xenobiology with specialization in Ancient biosystems from Regina University
*   **Personality:** Compassionate, methodical, and deeply ethical. Unlike her father, she understood the human cost of scientific advancement and often served as his moral compass.
*   **Appearance:** (From recovered images) Medium height with her father's grey eyes but warmer. Dark hair often tied back for lab work. Preferred practical field gear over formal attire.
*   **Trait:** Always hummed ancient Terran classical music while working - a habit that annoyed her father but calmed everyone else.
*   **Relationship with Father:** Complex - she loved him but grew increasingly concerned about his obsessive tendencies and disregard for safety protocols.

## The Djinni Incident
*   **Initial Event:** Seven years ago, while investigating an Ancients site in the Djinni system, she detected unusual energy patterns that concerned her
*   **Survival:** Contrary to official reports, she initially survived the dormant defense system activation but suffered severe neural trauma
*   **Death:** Died three days later aboard the *Stardust Drifter* despite Dr. Aros's desperate attempts to save her
*   **Body:** Preserved in an experimental medical cryo pod that Dr. Aros had been developing
*   **Current Location:** Unknown - the cryo pod was removed from the *Stardust Drifter* before Dr. Aros's disappearance

## The Experiments
*   **Cloning Attempts:** Dr. Aros created at least seven clones using her preserved genetic material
*   **Consciousness Transfer:** Each clone received partial consciousness transfers using data recovered from Ancient sensors at the Djinni site
*   **Results:** 
    - Clone 1-3: Immediate rejection, bodies survived less than 48 hours
    - Clone 4-5: Retained fragmentary memories, psychological collapse within days
    - Clone 6: Survived two weeks, spoke in Altaira's voice, recognized Rob Tobor, then suffered catastrophic neural degradation
    - Clone 7: Status unknown, referenced in final logs as "the most promising iteration"
*   **Ethical Violations:** Each experiment violated Imperial laws on human cloning and consciousness manipulation

## The Altaira Protocols
*   **True Purpose:** Not just resurrection, but the development of true consciousness preservation and transfer - achieving human immortality
*   **Key Discovery:** The Ancient sensors at Djinni didn't just record events - they captured complete neural patterns of beings in their vicinity
*   **Breakthrough:** Dr. Aros discovered how to extract and reconstruct consciousness patterns from Ancient data streams
*   **The Problem:** Human consciousness degrades when transferred - the mind rejects the artificial substrate
*   **The Zhodani Connection:** Dr. Aros secretly acquired Zhodani Consulate telepathic consciousness transfer technology through black market channels
*   **The Solution:** The protocols detail how to create a hybrid consciousness matrix combining:
    - Ancient data preservation methods
    - Zhodani psionic stabilization techniques
    - Human neural architecture
*   **Success Rate:** Clone Seven achieved 73% consciousness integrity - the threshold for a stable, immortal mind

## Residual Consciousness
*   **Ancient Data:** The Djinni sensors contain a complete scan of her consciousness at the moment of the defense system activation
*   **Ship's Computer:** Fragments of her personality may have been uploaded to the *Stardust Drifter*'s systems during experiments
*   **Unknown Preservation:** Dr. Aros's final logs hint at a successful preservation method but don't specify the medium or location
*   **The Question:** Is she truly dead, preserved in stasis, existing as data, or something else entirely?

## Corporate Interest
*   **Makhidkarun:** Believes the Altaira Protocols hold the key to practical immortality for their executives
*   **The Stakes:** Whoever controls this technology could offer eternal life to the highest bidders
*   **The Danger:** Incomplete transfers create unstable consciousnesses that can become dangerous
*   **Imperial Concerns:** The technology could destabilize the entire social order if nobles achieve immortality

## Relationships
*   **Dr. Philonius Aros:** Her father, driven to madness by guilt and the need to resurrect her
*   **Rob Tobor:** Surrogate uncle who still writes letters to her, may be the only one who truly mourned her as a person rather than a lost asset
*   **Unknown Associates:** Her research notes reference colleagues who helped her understand Ancient biological systems

## Legacy
*   **Scientific Contributions:** Her dissertation on Ancient biosystems remains classified but is considered groundbreaking
*   **Moral Questions:** Her fate raises fundamental questions about death, consciousness, and what makes someone human
*   **The Warning:** Her original concerns about the Djinni site proved correct - some knowledge comes at too high a price

*   (Final recording) "Tell my father... tell him I understand now. The Ancients didn't die. They just... changed."

## Mystery Elements
*   **The Missing Pod:** Where is her original body?
*   **Clone Seven:** What happened to the final experimental clone?
*   **The Upload:** Did Dr. Aros succeed in preserving her consciousness?
*   **The Voice:** Some claim to have heard her voice in Ancient sites across the sector
*   **The Truth:** Is Altaira truly gone, or has she become something else?
