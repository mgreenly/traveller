# Dr. Aris

*   **Role:** Plot Catalyst, Mysterious Benefactor
*   **Description:** A reclusive and paranoid scientist who was conducting secret research in the [Djinni](./systems/djinni.md) system. His work was so dangerous it caused the Imperium to declare the entire system a Red Zone.
*   **Actions:** He legally transferred his lab ship, the [*Stardust Drifter*](./ships/stardust-drifter.md), to his former student, [Keith](./characters/keith.md). He also sent [Keith](./characters/keith.md) a cryptic, biolocked message warning him of danger and instructing him to find the information broker [Roric Vance](./characters/roric-vance.md). He has since disappeared.
*   **Legacy:** He has left booby traps, a dangerous "guest" (a native life form from [Djinni](./systems/djinni.md)), and a cryptic log aboard the [*Stardust Drifter*](./ships/stardust-drifter.md).
