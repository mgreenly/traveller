# Roric Vance

*   **Role:** Information Broker, Initial Contact
*   **Description:** A washed-up, aging information broker. His face is a roadmap of wrinkles, his eyes are bloodshot, and a permanent tremor makes his hands shake. He wears a stained, synth-leather jacket that was probably fashionable two decades ago and smells faintly of stale booze and cheaper cigarettes. He is highly suspicious of strangers but holds a hidden loyalty to [Dr. Aris](./dr-aris.md).
*   **Location:** He can be found in a secluded booth in "The Gilded Spacer," a noisy bar in the seedy underbelly of the [Regina](../systems/regina.md) downport.
*   **Function:** When given the code phrase "The Whiskey Jack needs a fast ship," he will provide the players with the name of the only captain crazy enough to help them: **[Kaelen](./the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md)**, and her last known location, the anarchic world of **[Rech](../systems/rech.md)**.
