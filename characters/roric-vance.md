# Roric Vance

*   **Role:** Information Broker, Initial Contact
*   **Description:** A washed-up, aging information broker. His face is a roadmap of wrinkles, his eyes are bloodshot, and a permanent tremor makes his hands shake. He wears a stained, synth-leather jacket that was probably fashionable two decades ago and smells faintly of stale booze and cheaper cigarettes. He is highly suspicious of strangers but holds a hidden loyalty to [Dr. Aris](./dr-aris.md).
*   **Background:** Roric Vance was not always the burnt-out case nursing a drink in the back of a dive bar. He was once a premier information broker in the Regina subsector, dealing in corporate and political secrets that could make or break fortunes. His downfall came when he uncovered a sensitive piece of intelligence linking a series of "pirate" attacks back to a shell corporation—a thread that led dangerously close to Makhidkarun. Before he could sell the information, his network was dismantled, his reputation ruined, and he was forced into hiding. His one remaining loyalty is to Dr. Aris, a long-time, clandestine client who valued Vance's discretion and occasionally pulled him out of trouble. Now, Vance lives in a state of constant, alcohol-fueled paranoia, selling low-level scraps of information to survive, forever looking over his shoulder for the corporate ghosts he knows are still hunting him.
*   **Location:** He can be found in a secluded booth in "The Gravity Well," a noisy bar in the seedy underbelly of the [Regina](../systems/regina.md) downport.
*   **Function:** When given the code phrase "The Whiskey Jack needs a fast ship," he will provide the players with the name of the only captain crazy enough to help them: **[Kaelen](./the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md)**, and her last known location, the anarchic world of **[Rech](../systems/rech.md)**.
