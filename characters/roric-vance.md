# Roric Vance

*   **Role:** Information Broker, Fallen King of Secrets
*   **Full Name:** Roric Alastair Vance
*   **Age:** 61 standard years
*   **Description:** A walking cautionary tale about flying too close to the corporate suns. His face is a topographical map of hard living—deep lines carved by stress, eyes perpetually bloodshot from cheap alcohol and cheaper stims. The tremor in his hands isn't just from the drinking; it's neurological damage from a corporate "warning" that included experimental interrogation drugs. His synth-leather jacket, a burgundy number with silver clasps, was haute couture twenty years ago. Now it's held together by duraplast patches and stubborn pride.
*   **Trait:** Taps his fingers against surfaces in complex patterns - actually encrypted messages in old Imperial Navy code that only trained intelligence officers would recognize.

## Background
*   **The Golden Years:** Once operated from a suite in Regina Highport, brokering information between megacorps, nobility, and government agencies. Had a network of 200+ assets across the subsector.
*   **The Thread That Unraveled Everything:** Discovered that a series of "random" pirate attacks were actually Makhidkarun operations designed to manipulate shipping insurance rates and eliminate competitors.
*   **The Fall:** In 48 hours, his accounts were frozen, his assets turned or killed, his reputation systematically destroyed through planted evidence of selling false intel. The only reason he's alive is that killing him would have legitimized his findings.
*   **Current State:** Lives in a constant state of functional paranoia, using alcohol to quiet the part of his brain that sees patterns everywhere—because sometimes the patterns are real.

## Personality & Survival Mechanisms
*   **Paranoid Genius:** His paranoia isn't mental illness—it's an accurate assessment of reality. He's developed elaborate routines to detect surveillance, including seemingly random route changes that are actually calculated to reveal tails.
*   **Information Junkie:** Can't help but collect secrets, even worthless ones. Knows which port workers are having affairs, which customs officials take bribes, which bartenders water their drinks.
*   **Loyalty Through Trauma:** Dr. Aros once saved him from a Makhidkarun kill team, no questions asked. That debt created the only unbreakable loyalty in Vance's fractured world.
*   **The Act:** Plays up the drunk burnout angle—people dismiss drunks, talk freely around them. Half his intelligence gathering happens because people think he's too wasted to remember.

## The Gravity Well Operations
*   **Why This Bar:** The Gravity Well's location deep in the port structure creates natural signal interference. Corporate surveillance has trouble penetrating the layers of metal and running machinery.
*   **The Booth:** His regular spot has been modified—signal dampener under the table, escape route through a maintenance panel, sight lines to all entrances.
*   **The Network Remnants:** Still maintains contact with a handful of survivors from his old network—a few loyal assets who went underground when he fell.
*   **Information Categories:** Deals in three tiers—public (worth drinks), gray (worth credits), and black (worth lives). Knows exactly which category everything falls into.

## Hidden Capabilities
*   **Eidetic Memory:** Before the neurological damage, had perfect recall. Now it's selective—can't remember what he had for breakfast but can recite every corporate connection in the subsector.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** Sees connections others miss. The tremor in his hands gets worse when he's processing complex patterns—his brain literally overheating.
*   **Dead Drops:** Maintains seventeen different dead drops across Regina Downport, each with different levels of encryption and security.
*   **The Emergency Kit:** Has a go-bag hidden in the bar containing: false identities, untraceable credit chips, a military-grade encrypted communicator, and a single-use virus that can crash local surveillance networks.

## The Whiskey Jack Protocol
*   **Origin:** "Whiskey Jack" was Aros's code name in Vance's files—a reference to a mythical bird that steals from more powerful predators.
*   **The Response:** When given the phrase, Vance doesn't just provide information—he activates a series of pre-planned actions to help Aros's designated allies.
*   **Layered Intelligence:** The information about pilots is just the first layer. He also provides: safe routes to avoid corporate surveillance, which officials to bribe, what ships to avoid.
*   **The Warning:** Always includes a warning about the real danger—not the obvious threats, but the hidden ones. In this case, that Kane and Makhidkarun are connected.

## Relationships
*   **[Dr. Aros](./dr-aros.md):** The only person he truly trusts. Aros treats him as an equal, not a resource, and that respect means everything.
*   **"Dusty" Chen:** Bartender at The Gravity Well who ensures Vance's booth stays reserved and his drinks stay reasonably priced. Former military intelligence.
*   **Corbin Vance:** His younger brother (See [Corbin Vance](./corbin-vance.md)), a legitimate businessman who thinks Roric is dead. Roric watches over him from the shadows.
*   **Inspector Torres:** A port security officer who Vance feeds just enough real information to keep valuable. Their relationship is purely transactional.
*   **The Ghost Network:** Six surviving assets from his old network who still feed him information, believing that one day he'll rise again.

## Survival Tactics
*   **The Drunk Walk:** Has perfected a stumbling gait that's actually designed to check for tails and scan for threats.
*   **Information Bombs:** Has several information caches set to release if he doesn't check in every 72 hours—his insurance against permanent disappearance.
*   **The Rotation:** Never sleeps in the same place three nights in a row. Has arrangements with seven different flophouses, each thinking he's a different person.
*   **Chemical Cocktail:** The specific mix of alcohol and stims he takes isn't random—it's calculated to keep him functional while appearing incapacitated.

## What He Knows
*   **About the Inheritance:** Knew it was coming. Aros told him months ago, though not the specifics. Has been preparing the ground, ensuring certain people are in certain places.
*   **About the Ship:** Knows more than he tells—including that there's something in the lab that Aros valued more than the ship itself.
*   **About the Players:** Has already researched them all. Knows their backgrounds, their skills, their weaknesses. Information he'll parcel out if they prove worthy.
*   **About the Real Game:** Understands this isn't about a ship—it's about what Aros discovered. Something that has the megacorps terrified.

## Game Notes
*   **Initial Suspicion:** Will test the players multiple times before truly helping—false information to see if they're being followed, questions only Aros's allies would know the answers to.
*   **Graduated Trust:** The more the players prove themselves, the more he reveals. Start with pilot names, end with corporate conspiracy theories that happen to be true.
*   **Potential Ally:** If the players treat him with respect and keep him in the loop, he becomes a valuable ongoing contact.
*   **The Trigger:** If Makhidkarun learns he's involved, they'll send a kill team. This could become a dramatic scene where the players must protect their information source.

## Current Assets
*   **Credit Balance:** 847 credits (keeps exactly this amount—more attracts attention, less limits options)
*   **False Identities:** Three functional false IDs at varying levels of quality
*   **Information Cache:** Encrypted data worth millions if he could safely sell it
*   **The Black Book:** Physical notebook with handwritten intelligence—purposely anachronistic to avoid digital tracking
*   **Emergency Beacon:** One-use transmitter that will bring Dr. Aros running—if he's still alive to receive it
