## Resolution

The adventure concludes when the players escape the [Djinni](../systems/djinni.md) system with the [*Stardust Drifter*](../ships/stardust-drifter.md). To make their ownership official, they must file a salvage and inheritance claim at the starport on [K'Kirka](../systems/k-kirka.md). This sets the stage for future adventures, where they must deal with their massive debt to [Silas Kane](../characters/silas-kane.md), the unwanted attention from [Makhidkarun](../factions/makhidkarun.md), and the true nature of [Dr. Aris](../characters/dr-aris.md)'s research.

### If the Data Core is Lost

Without the data core, the players face severe consequences:

*   **Ship is Immobile:** The *Stardust Drifter* cannot calculate jumps without its custom core. Towing to the nearest starport costs Cr500,000-1,000,000.
*   **Debt Escalates:** Kane's "partial salvage recovery" clause activates immediately, increasing interest to **50% monthly** compounding.
*   **Makhidkarun Wins:** They recover their research through the Rust Dogs and will view the players as loose ends to be eliminated.
*   **No Leverage:** Without the Kane Contingency file, players have no defense against the crushing debt.
*   **Reduced Ship Value:** The ship is worth only 20% of its full value without its advanced lab systems and proprietary data.
*   **Navy Complications:** Without jump capability, evading the [INV *Steadfast*](../ships/inv-steadfast.md) becomes nearly impossible, likely resulting in arrest.

### Dealing with the Debt

**If Players Have the Kane Contingency File:**
*   **Total Forgiveness:** Confront Kane with the evidence. Debt is canceled but Kane becomes a bitter enemy who will seek revenge.
*   **Negotiated Settlement:** Reduce debt to 10% of original (Cr300,000) in exchange for returning the files and signing NDAs.
*   **Ongoing Relationship:** Become Kane's "problem solvers" - each mission reduces debt by Cr100,000-500,000.
*   **Turn the Tables:** Use the evidence to take over Kane's operation (requires extensive roleplay and likely a full campaign arc).

**If Players Lost the Data Core:**
*   **Asset Seizure:** Kane claims the ship after payment deadline (60/90/120 days based on loan terms).
*   **Indentured Service:** Work for Kane at 50% normal rates until debt is paid (approximately 2-3 years of missions).
*   **Desperate Measures:** Flee to non-Imperial space, becoming fugitives with bounties.
*   **Find New Leverage:** Future adventures to discover other compromising information about Kane or Makhidkarun.

**Alternative Solutions:**
*   **Sell to Makhidkarun:** They'll pay the debt plus Cr500,000 for the ship and all research data.
*   **Imperial Witness Protection:** Trade information about Makhidkarun's illegal operations for debt relief and new identities.
*   **Rival Financier:** Find another lender to buy out Kane's loan (requires 150% of debt, worse terms).
*   **Insurance Fraud:** Fake the ship's destruction (highly illegal, requires elaborate planning).
*   **Noble Patron:** Seek backing from a Noble in exchange for service (introduces new obligations).
