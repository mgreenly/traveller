## Prologue: The Letter

At the start of the adventure, each of you is going about your life without any recent contact with the others. You all live and work somewhere in the Regina subsector of the Spinward Marches.
Then, several days ago, each of you received the same secure, personalized message on your personal data-slate.

**The Recipients:**
 **[Keith](../players/keith.md)**, **[Ashley](../players/ashley.md)**, **[Eric](../players/eric.md)**, **[Mike](../players/mike.md)**, **[Pete](../players/pete.md)**, **[Quinn](../players/quinn.md)**

---

> **Subject: A Matter of Utmost Importance — Regarding [Dr. Aris](../characters/dr-aris.md)**
>
> **[Recipient's Name],**
>
> This message serves as a formal notification from the law offices of Haskins & Associates, representing the interests of [Dr. Aris](../characters/dr-aris.md).
>
> A legal directive filed by our client includes a provision that we believe you will find to be of significant material interest. Pursuant to this directive, you have been named as a beneficiary in a transfer of significant assets.
>
> To claim this asset, your presence is required at our offices on [Regina](../systems/regina.md). A secured, pre-paid transport ticket has been reserved in your name. Please present this message at any licensed starport ticketing agent to claim your passage.
>
> We understand this summons may be unexpected. Be assured of its legitimacy and of the value of the asset in question. The matter is time-sensitive and requires your discretion.
>
> We await your arrival at our offices located in the Capital district, 87th Floor, Commerce Tower Spire, [Regina](../systems/regina.md).
>
> Sincerely,
>
> **Haskins & Associates**
> *Fiduciary & Legal Services*

> **Read Aloud (optional):** Your data-slate chirps and decrypts an official-looking notice. The Haskins & Associates crest rotates above crisp text. Someone named Dr. Aris has named you in a transfer of assets. A paid ticket to Regina waits at the nearest starport. The message is terse, legitimate, and very clear: come at once.

You all know Dr. Aris. He was an eccentric research scientist whose path crossed each of yours at some point in your careers.

### Arrival on [Regina](../systems/regina.md)

Given the distances involved, the beneficiaries arrive on [Regina](../systems/regina.md) at different times over several days. Upon arrival at the [Regina](../systems/regina.md) Downport, each individual is directed to a waiting ground car, which whisks them to the opulent **Celestial Plaza Hotel** in the heart of the Capital district.

*[ The players can make a 2D roll to determine the order of arrival. Ties arrive together. ]*

The law firm has arranged for their accommodations. Each beneficiary is checked into a comfortable, well-appointed suite. A polite but reserved hotel concierge named **Lian** informs them that all expenses are covered and that Haskins & Associates will contact them once all parties have arrived.

This provides a window of a few days for the characters to rest, explore the city, or perhaps meet each other in the hotel's lounge.

While waiting, they might visit some of [Regina](../systems/regina.md)'s famous attractions (aligned with the city's Highport/Downport hub and its academic focus on the Ancients):
*   **The Regina Museum of Imperial Endeavor:** Flagship museum with rotating exhibits curated in partnership with the University of Regina. Features a permanent wing on the Ancients, including artifacts from a site near Capital and holo-reconstructions of pre-Imperial dig finds.
*   **The Crystal Gardens:** Climate-controlled botanical domes woven through Capital’s arcologies, irrigated by Assiniboia condensers. Native flora grow beneath latticework crystal canopies that refract the city’s neon into a glittering forest.
*   **The Grand Concourse:** The commercial spine linking the Downport mag-lev terminus to Capital’s arcology plazas and up to the Highport concourses. Luxe boutiques and legal firms on the main levels; gambling dens, data fences, and gray-market brokers one level down.

#### Referee Notes: Hotel/Lounge Encounters and City Interludes
Use one or two of these as quick, optional beats during downtime. Keep them tight and informative.
*   Security Sweep (Recon or Deception 8+): Plainclothes officers conduct a random scan in the hotel lobby. Success avoids scrutiny; failure invites routine questioning about recent travel (no consequences if honest).
*   Corporate Head-hunter (Persuade or Streetwise 8+): A recruiter mistakes a PC for a prospect and fishes for background. Success nets a rumor: Haskins has recently offloaded several “fringe assets.”
*   Old Acquaintance (Social or Persuade 8+): A familiar face from a prior career buys a drink. Success yields a lead: whispers that [Dr. Aris](../characters/dr-aris.md) was cut off by multiple firms months ago.
*   Downport Brush (Streetwise or Recon 8+): A pickpocket attempt or slipped tracker at the Grand Concourse. Success spots it; failure means the PCs are quietly tagged until they notice (Electronics (Comms) or Recon 8+ to remove).
*   Newsburst: Djinni Red Zone (Investigate or Admin 8+): A newsfeed segment about tightened Navy protocols around the [Djinni](../systems/djinni.md) exclusion. Success gleans timing: additional patrols rotate in 4–6 weeks.
*   University Exhibit (Education or Investigate 8+): Grad students presenting Ancients pieces at the museum. Success uncovers that corporate access to recent digs was “bought out,” souring the University’s mood toward certain firms.

Once the final recipient has checked into the hotel, a formal, encrypted message is sent to each of their rooms:\n> **Read-aloud:** "All parties are present. Please be at our offices tomorrow at 0900 sharp. A car will be waiting."
