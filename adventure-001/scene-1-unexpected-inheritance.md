## Scene 1: An Unexpected Inheritance

**Location:** [Regina](../systems/regina.md), Subsector Capital (Law Level 9)

> **Read Aloud:** Whether your journey was a long haul from the fringe or a short trip from an adjacent system, you've all now arrived at the law offices of Haskins & Associates. The reception area is a study in minimalist wealth—white marble, imposing furniture, and utter silence.
>
> A severe-looking receptionist checks your credentials, one by one, with an air of detached efficiency.  Then you all are escorted into a conference room. A lawyer in an impeccably tailored suit waits at the head of a long, polished table, a thin data-slate resting before them. They do not smile. "Thank you for coming," the lawyer begins, their voice as sterile as the room. "Please, be seated. We have much to discuss regarding a matter for our client, [Dr. Aros](../characters/dr-aros.md)."

### Task Chain: Dealing with Haskins & Associates
The meeting with the lawyers is the players' first challenge. The goal is not to "win," but to extract as much information as possible. The lawyers will volunteer nothing beyond the legal minimum.
**Baseline Outcome (Automatic Success):** The players receive the legal documents for the [*Stardust Drifter*](../ships/stardust-drifter.md) showing joint ownership among all six beneficiaries, and the sealed data wafer from Dr. Aros.

#### Stage 1: Reading the Room (TN 8+)
As the lawyer presents the case, players can make an **Investigate** or **Advocate** check to gauge the atmosphere.
*   **Investigate:** Represents observing the lawyer's body language and other out-of-place details.
*   **Advocate:** Represents sensing the legal implications behind the lawyer's carefully chosen words.
*   **Success (8+):** The players notice the lawyer's unusual stiffness; it's a carefully constructed wall. They sense a desire to sever all ties with the name "Dr. Aros." This grants a **+1 bonus** to the check in Stage 2.
*   **Failure (7-):** The lawyer is a perfect corporate drone. This imposes a **-1 penalty** to the check in Stage 2.

#### Stage 2: The Inquisition (TN 8+)
After the presentation, the players can probe for more information with a **Persuade** or **Advocate** check (modified by Stage 1).
*   **Persuade:** An attempt to break through the corporate facade with a friendly or intimidating approach.
*   **Advocate:** An attempt to use precise legal questioning to corner the lawyer.

#### Outcome Tiers
*   **Tier 1: Minimal Success (Failure on Stage 2 check):** The lawyer stonewalls.
    > **Read-aloud:** "All pertinent information is contained within the provided documentation. We have no further comment."
*   **Tier 2: Standard Success (Success on Stage 2 check):** The lawyer concedes that Dr. Aros was a "problematic" client and that the firm is **terminating its relationship** with him, a major red flag.
*   **Tier 3: Exceptional Success (Success on Stage 2 check with Effect 4+):** The lawyer makes a critical slip, mentioning they've been instructed to "monitor any transponder activations" before catching themselves and refusing to elaborate.

#### The Transfer
The lawyer presents the legal documents transferring the title of the *Stardust Drifter* to all six beneficiaries as joint owners. 
> **Read-aloud:** "We are executing the transfer of ownership of the laboratory vessel *Stardust Drifter*, registry number SX-4471-D, to all six beneficiaries as specified by our client. Each of you has been allocated shares according to Dr. Aros's instructions. Each of you will need to sign. The vessel's current location is... unspecified in our records. Dr. Aros handled all operational details personally. We are simply fulfilling our legal obligation to transfer the title as instructed."
>
> The lawyer continues, clearly uncomfortable: "Due to the unique legal circumstances of this transfer, it was filed through our sub-sector branch specializing in fringe assets. Therefore, any salvage or title claim for this vessel must be certified in person at the designated court of record in the K'Kirka system. You must present both these ownership documents AND the physical vessel for inspection. The ship will be examined to verify its identity and assess any liens or encumbrances. Filing elsewhere will be considered invalid, and without both the documents and the ship present, the claim cannot be processed."

When pressed for more details about the ship's whereabouts, the lawyer's fingers drum nervously on the table. "Dr. Aros was quite... particular about compartmentalizing information. We have the ship's registration and specifications, but its current berthing location was never disclosed to us. That information, presumably, is in his personal correspondence." They gesture toward the data wafer.

Finally, they provide the data wafer, explaining it is sealed with a biolock keyed to recognize any of the six beneficiaries. 
> **Read-aloud:** "This contains... personal correspondence from Dr. Aros. We have not reviewed its contents, as per client confidentiality protocols. The biolock will recognize any of you as authorized recipients."

Once one of the beneficiaries opens it, a holographic recording begins to play:

### Dr. Aros's Final Message

> **Read-aloud:** The hologram flickers to life. Before the image even stabilizes, Dr. Aros's voice cuts through with urgent intensity:
>
> *"STOP. If anyone else is watching this—lawyers, corporate agents, anyone—shut it off NOW. You six—my chosen crew—watch this alone. Lock the door. Check for surveillance. The walls have eyes and the eyes have owners."*
>
> [The image resolves, showing [Dr. Aros](../characters/dr-aros.md) on what appears to be the bridge of a starship. His white hair is disheveled, his lab coat torn. In the background, you can hear faint alarms—proximity warnings, maybe pressure alerts.]
>
> *"Still watching? Good. If you're seeing this, then the transfer went through and you are now the new crew of the Stardust Drifter. The lawyers know nothing—I made sure of that. They're parasites, but useful parasites. By now they've probably already assigned someone to follow you. Check your six."*
>
> [His grey eyes dart nervously off-screen before focusing directly on you with uncomfortable intensity.]
>
> *"Listen carefully—we don't have much time."
>
> [A distant explosion rumbles through the recording. Emergency lighting casts red shadows across his gaunt face.]
>
> *"I've had to abandon the ship. It's powered down in the Djinni system, hiding in the inner asteroid belt—coordinates are embedded in this message along with a transponder wake-up code. When you activate it, the ship will broadcast its location for exactly 73 seconds before going dark again. Use that window wisely."*
>
> [The ship shudders. A console behind him sparks, showering him with electrical discharge. He doesn't even flinch.]
>
> *"They'll come for it. The lawyers, the bank, the corporations—none of them can be trusted. Even the contacts I'm giving you..."* [he pauses, choosing his words carefully] *"...even Roric Vance can only be trusted so far. Guard what you know. Share nothing unless absolutely necessary."*
>
> [A new klaxon begins—louder, more urgent. The synthetic undertones of [Rob Tobor's](../characters/rob-tobor.md) voice cut through the chaos: "Doctor! Containment field at 12% and falling. The hostile contacts are now three minutes, forty seconds out. We must leave NOW!"]
>
> *"I have critical work that must be completed. The data core contains everything—the Kane Contingency, yes, but also so much more. Project Prometheus, the Altaira Protocols, the consciousness matrices—it's all there. But they don't know that. They think it's just blackmail material."*
>
> [The lights flicker. Dr. Aros grabs the recording device, bringing his face uncomfortably close. His eyes are wild with a mixture of fear and something else—triumph?]
>
> *"Find Roric Vance. Tell him 'Whiskey Jack needs a fast ship.' He'll know what it means. I'll try to contact you once you have the ship, but until then—trust no one. Not even each other, if it comes to that."*
>
> [Rob's voice again, more urgent: "Doctor! Hull breach in section seven! Two minutes to minimum safe distance!"]
>
> *"Remember—the code is a trap, but one you must spring. They're tracking it, but you need it to find the ship. Move fast, think faster, and whatever you do, don't let them—"*
>
> [The recording cuts to static, then ends. A small data packet blinks on the wafer's display: NAV_COORDINATES_DJINNI_BELT_327.451.892 and TRANSPONDER_ACTIVATION_CODE_WHISKEY-JACK-7739]

*   **Optional Action (Computers TN 8+):** Before opening the wafer, a successful check will reveal its military-grade encryption and confirm it has no traps. A failure reveals nothing.
*   **Optional Action (Investigate TN 10+):** After viewing, a successful check notices that the timestamp on the recording is from 47 days ago, and background stellar positions visible through the bridge viewport suggest he was near the Zhodani border, not Djinni.

### Leaving the Law Office

#### Potential Surveillance (Referee Notes)
After the meeting, Haskins & Associates will attempt to have the party followed. While they've fulfilled their legal obligation to transfer the ship, they desperately want to know where it actually is - both to protect themselves from liability and potentially to sell that information to interested parties.

*   **Immediate Tail (Recon TN 8+):** As the party leaves the building, a successful check spots a well-dressed individual who exits moments after them and maintains a consistent distance.
*   **Electronic Tracking (Electronics TN 9+):** A scan of their belongings reveals a microscopic tracker placed on one character's clothing during the handshake or document exchange.
*   **Data Mining:** The law firm will monitor any transponder activations and attempt to trace any financial transactions the party makes.

**If Confronted:** The tail is a junior associate named Marcus Trent. If caught, he'll claim he's "just heading to lunch" but will fold under pressure (Persuade TN 7+), admitting the senior partners want to know "what mess Aros left behind" and offering 10,000 credits for the ship's location.

**Long-term Consequences:** If the party doesn't detect or shake the surveillance:
- The lawyers will sell the ship's location to interested parties (including Makhidkarun)
- Additional "random" encounters with corporate agents increase by +1 per system
- Silas Kane will know about their activities before they reach him
