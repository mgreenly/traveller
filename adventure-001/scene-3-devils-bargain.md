## Scene 3: The Devil's Bargain

**Location:** [Regina](../systems/regina.md), The Gilded Cage

> **Read Aloud:** The Gilded Cage sits in Regina's mid-port like a predator among prey. Its polished black exterior and gold-trimmed doors speak of wealth and danger in equal measure. Inside, the air is thick with expensive smoke and quiet desperation. Crystal chandeliers cast harsh light on patrons who avoid eye contact, each nursing private debts and dashed dreams. You're directed to a private booth where [Silas "The Gilded" Kane](../characters/silas-kane.md) waits. He's immaculately dressed in a suit that costs more than most ships' annual maintenance, his silver hair slicked back, and his smile as sharp as a monofilament blade. Two augmented bodyguards flank him, their cyber-eyes tracking your every movement.
>
> "Ah, the inheritors," Kane purrs, gesturing to the seats across from him. "I've been expecting you. Word travels fast when someone needs three million credits for a salvage operation in a Red Zone. Please, sit. Let's discuss how I can help you achieve your dreams... for a price."

### Task Chain: Negotiating the Loan

This is a critical negotiation that will determine the terms of the players' debt. Kane will loan them the money regardless, but the terms can vary dramatically based on their performance.

#### Stage 1: Due Diligence (TN 9+)

Before the main negotiation, players can try to gain leverage by researching Kane or making a strong first impression.

*   **Investigate:** Research Kane's past deals and reputation to find leverage points.
*   **Admin:** Use legal knowledge to understand loan contracts and identify potential loopholes.
*   **Streetwise:** Tap into the criminal underground for dirt on Kane.

*   **Success (9+):** The players uncover useful information. They gain **+2** to their Stage 2 check and learn one of the following (player's choice):
    - Kane has a 43% default rate on his loans, suggesting he profits more from seizures than repayments
    - He has connections to [Makhidkarun](../factions/makhidkarun.md) and often acts as their "procurement specialist"
    - Three previous borrowers died under suspicious circumstances after mentioning "insurance policies" against Kane
*   **Failure (8-):** Kane's security notices their inquiries. He's amused but wary. **-1 penalty** to Stage 2 check.

#### Stage 2: The Negotiation (TN 10+)

The main negotiation determines the loan terms. Kane will provide the 3 million credits regardless, but the terms vary.

*   **Broker:** Professional negotiation using market knowledge.
*   **Persuade:** Emotional appeal or logical argument.
*   **Carouse:** Try to get Kane drunk and careless (risky - failure gives additional -2).

#### Outcome Tiers

*   **Tier 1: Crushing Terms (Failure on Stage 2 check):** 
    - Interest: **40% monthly**, compounding
    - Collateral: The [*Stardust Drifter*](../ships/stardust-drifter.md), all personal assets, **and** future earnings
    - Term: **60 days** before first payment
    - Special clause: Kane's representatives have the right to "inspect" the salvage operation
    - Kane's parting words:\n    > **Read-aloud:** "Don't disappoint me. I do so hate having to collect collateral... personally."

*   **Tier 2: Harsh Terms (Success on Stage 2 check):**
    - Interest: **25% monthly**, compounding  
    - Collateral: The *Stardust Drifter* and all personal assets
    - Term: **90 days** before first payment
    - Special clause: Weekly progress reports required
    - Kane's parting words: "A fair deal for both parties. Remember, I'm investing in your success... and I protect my investments."

*   **Tier 3: Survivable Terms (Success on Stage 2 check with Effect 4+):**
    - Interest: **15% monthly**, simple interest
    - Collateral: The *Stardust Drifter* only
    - Term: **120 days** before first payment
    - Special clause: None
    - Kane's parting words: "You negotiate well. Perhaps there's hope for you yet. Don't make me regret this generosity."

#### The Contract

Regardless of the outcome, Kane produces an ornate datapad with the contract. As the players sign, he adds one final comment:

> "Oh, one more thing. I've heard rumors about some... competing interests in your salvage operation. The Scrap Vultures, I believe they're called? Such a shame when salvage crews fight over claims. So many accidents can happen in deep space. Still, I'm sure professionals like yourselves can handle a little competition."

The way he says it makes it clear he's the one who leaked information to the Scrap Vultures, ensuring the players will be desperate enough to accept his terms.

**Important:** If any player succeeds on a **Difficult (10+) Investigate or Admin** check while reviewing the contract, they notice a subtle clause that gives Kane the right to "renegotiate terms in the event of partial salvage recovery." This hints that recovering the data core is even more critical than they thought.
