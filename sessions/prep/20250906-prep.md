## Shopping List for Djinni System Recovery Mission

The crew is currently on **[Rech](../../systems/rech.md)** preparing to travel to the quarantined **[Djinni](../../systems/djinni.md)** system to recover the derelict lab ship *[Stardust Drifter](../../ships/stardust-drifter.md)*. Based on the mission parameters and hazards, they should consider acquiring the following equipment:

### Essential Equipment

#### Vacc Suits & Environmental Protection
- **Vacc Suits (TL10)** - Cr10,000 each
  - Essential for boarding derelict ship in vacuum
  - 6 hours life support
  - Protects against vacuum and radiation
  - *Note: TL12 "tailored" suits (Cr20,000) are more comfortable and flexible*
- **Hostile Environment Vacc Suits (TL9)** - Cr20,000 each  
  - Better protection if ship has toxic atmosphere or extreme conditions
  - 6 hours life support
  - Enhanced radiation protection
- **Rescue Bubbles (TL9)** - Cr600 each
  - Emergency backup for suit failures
  - Portable emergency shelter

#### Medical Supplies
- **Medikit (TL10)** - Cr1,500
  - DM+1 on first aid checks
  - Essential for field medicine
  - Contains diagnostic devices, surgical tools, drugs, antibiotics
- **Medikit (TL12)** - Cr5,000
  - DM+2 on first aid checks
  - Better for serious injuries
- **Anti-radiation drugs** - Part of medikit
  - Critical given radiation hazard from quarantine zone

#### Weapons & Defense
*Note: Rech has Law Level 3 - most weapons are legal but may attract unwanted attention*

**Sidearms:**
- **Snub Pistols (TL8)** - Cr150 each
  - Zero-G capable (won't push user in vacuum)
  - 6 round magazines (Cr10 per reload)
  - Good for ship combat
- **Body Pistols (TL8)** - Cr500 each
  - Easily concealed
  - 6 round magazines

**Long Arms:**
- **Advanced Combat Rifles (TL10)** - Cr1,000 each
  - 40 round magazines (Cr15 per reload)
  - Auto-fire capability
  - Built-in scope
- **Shotguns (TL4)** - Cr200 each
  - Devastating at close range (4D damage)
  - Good for boarding actions

**Melee Weapons:**
- **Cutlasses (TL2)** - Cr200 each
  - Standard shipboard blade
  - Won't breach hull like firearms
  - 3D damage

**Ammunition:**
- Extra magazines for all weapons (at least 3-5 per weapon)

#### Armor
- **Flak Jackets (TL7)** - Cr100 each
  - Basic ballistic protection (+3)
  - Obviously military garment
- **Cloth Armor (TL10)** - Cr500 each
  - Better protection (+8)
  - Can pass as normal clothing
- **Reflec Armor (TL10)** - Cr1,500 each
  - Specifically protects against lasers (+10 vs lasers)
  - Can be worn over other armor

#### Communications & Sensors
- **Commdots (TL10)** - Cr250 each
  - 500km range communicators
  - Essential for team coordination
- **IR Goggles (TL6)** - Cr500 per pair
  - See heat signatures in darkness
- **Light Intensifier Goggles (TL9)** - Cr1,250 per pair
  - See in near-total darkness
- **Geiger Counters (TL10)** - Cr150 each
  - Detect radiation (quarantine zone hazard)
- **EM Probe (TL10)** - Cr1,000
  - Detect electromagnetic emissions
  - Useful for finding hidden systems/traps

#### Computer & Hacking Equipment
- **Portable Computer (TL12/Computer-3)** - Cr1,000
  - For accessing ship systems
  - Can run hacking software
- **Intrusion Software/2 (TL11)** - Cr10,000
  - For breaking into ship's computer systems
  - Often illegal - be discrete
- **Agent Software/1 (TL12)** - Cr2,000
  - Automated hacking assistant
- **Data Wafers (TL10)** - Cr5 each (get 20+)
  - For copying ship data
  - Backup storage

#### Tools & Technical Equipment
- **Electronics Toolkit (TL12)** - Cr2,000
  - For repairs and bypassing systems
  - Essential for dealing with traps
- **Mechanical Toolkit (TL12)** - Cr1,000
  - For physical repairs
- **Engineering Toolkit (TL12)** - Cr4,000
  - For ship system repairs
- **Breaching charges** - Variable cost
  - For forcing airlocks if necessary

#### Survival & Support Gear
- **Emergency rations** - Cr100 per person per week
  - Backup food supply
- **Grav Belts (TL12)** - Cr100,000 each (if budget allows)
  - Mobility in zero-G
  - Major tactical advantage
- **Rope/Cable** - Cr50 per 100m
  - Tethering in zero-G
- **Magnetic boots** - Part of vacc suit
  - Movement on hull
- **Portable lights/torches** - Cr50 each
  - Ship will be dark
- **Climbing Kit (TL8)** - Cr500
  - Includes pitons, ropes, harnesses

### Budget Considerations

**Minimum Essential Kit (per person):**
- Vacc Suit: Cr10,000
- Medikit (shared): Cr1,500
- Snub Pistol + ammo: Cr200
- Flak Jacket: Cr100
- Commdot: Cr250
- **Subtotal: ~Cr11,000 per person**

**Recommended Kit (per person):**
- Vacc Suit (TL12): Cr20,000
- Medikit (TL12, shared): Cr5,000
- Combat Rifle + ammo: Cr1,100
- Cloth Armor: Cr500
- Full sensor suite: Cr2,000
- **Subtotal: ~Cr25,000 per person**

### Special Considerations for Rech

- **Law Level 3**: Most weapons legal but conspicuous
- **Lawless environment**: Visible weapons may deter or attract trouble
- **[Scrap Vultures](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md) present**: Expect ambush - armor essential
- **No reliable medical facilities**: Stock up on medical supplies

### Mission-Specific Requirements

Based on adventure intelligence:
1. **Ship is depressurized** - Vacc suits mandatory
2. **[Dr. Aros](../../characters/dr-aros.md) left traps** - Electronics toolkit essential for disarming
3. **Ship's data core missing** - Will need to retrieve from [Scrap Vultures](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md)
4. **Naval patrol present** - May need to run silent (no active sensors)
5. **Radiation hazard** - Anti-rad drugs and detection equipment critical
6. **Zero-G combat likely** - Zero-G weapons (snub pistols) recommended

### Shopping Priority Order

1. **Vacc Suits** - Cannot board without them
2. **Medical supplies** - No medical facilities in Djinni
3. **Weapons & ammo** - Scrap Vultures will attack
4. **Communications** - Team coordination essential
5. **Computer/hacking gear** - Need to access ship systems
6. **Tools** - For repairs and trap disarmament
7. **Sensors** - For detecting threats
8. **Armor** - Protection in firefights
9. **Luxury items** - Grav belts, better computers, etc.

### Notes on Availability

- Rech is TL8-10 with some higher tech black market items
- Military gear may require streetwise/criminal contacts
- Prices may be inflated due to lawless nature
- Watch for counterfeit or damaged goods
- Consider hiring local guide for best suppliers

## Shopping Encounters on Rech

### Shopping Locations & Surveillance

Due to [Rech's](../../systems/rech.md) limited tech level (TL8-10) and poor trade classification, the crew must visit multiple suppliers to gather all necessary equipment. The [Scrap Vultures](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md) have been tipped off about the crew's presence and have assigned members to shadow them at different locations.

**The Scrap Vulture Logo:** A vulture with outstretched wings clutching various tools (wrench, cutting torch, pry bar) in its talons. This logo appears on patches, tattoos, equipment, and their ship hull.

#### Location 1: Vacuum Solutions Ltd.
- **Description:** The only legitimate vacc suit dealer, prices reflect their monopoly
- **Proprietor:** Marcus Fitzgerald III - Immaculately dressed corporate rep who treats Rech assignment as punishment, desperately maintains "standards"
- **Scrap Vulture Observer:** [Zina "Sparks" Petrova](../../characters/scrap-vulture/zina-sparks-petrova.md) (medic/tech) - Very Difficult (12+) to spot
  - **Cover:** Browsing emergency gear, checking seal ratings
  - **Detection Success:** Notice someone paying unusual attention to your group
  - **Detection Effect 4+:** Spot the small Scrap Vulture logo patch on her toolkit bag

#### Location 2: Korvac's Military Surplus
- **Description:** A fortified bunker-shop selling "decommissioned" military hardware from various conflicts
- **Proprietor:** Sgt. Korvac (Ret.) - Grizzled Imperial Marine veteran with cybernetic arm, respects military bearing and hates pirates
- **Scrap Vulture Observer:** [Joric "Slick" Fen](../../characters/scrap-vulture/joric-slick-fen.md) (smooth talker) - Difficult (10+) to spot
  - **Cover:** Haggling loudly with Korvac over armor prices
  - **Detection Success:** Recognize the loud customer keeps glancing your way
  - **Detection Effect 4+:** Notice the Scrap Vulture logo tattoo partially visible on neck when he turns

#### Location 3: Doc Hazel's Medical Exchange
- **Description:** Former combat medic's shop, half legal clinic, half pharmaceutical fence
- **Proprietor:** Dr. Hazel Okonkwo - Battle-scarred field surgeon who lost her license for "excessive compassion," treats anyone for the right price
- **Scrap Vulture Observer:** [Grak](../../characters/scrap-vulture/grak.md) (vargr enforcer) - Average (8+) to spot
  - **Cover:** Waiting outside, "bodyguarding" for someone inside
  - **Detection Success:** The Vargr outside is definitely watching your group
  - **Detection Effect 4+:** Spot the Scrap Vulture emblem on his combat knife sheath

#### Location 4: The Circuit Breaker
- **Description:** Electronics black market hidden behind a failing noodle shop, password entry only
- **Proprietor:** "Whisper" Chen - Paranoid ex-corporate hacker who speaks in hushed tones and constantly checks for surveillance
- **Scrap Vulture Observer:** [Kara "Wrench" Riker](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md) (leader) herself - Difficult (10+) to spot
  - **Cover:** Examining high-end hacking equipment
  - **Detection Success:** That woman with the expensive gear is following you
  - **Detection Effect 4+:** Notice the Scrap Vulture logo on her custom multitool

#### Additional Locations (No Surveillance)

5. **Big Bruno's Boom Boutique** - Weapons dealer who asks no questions if the price is right
   - **Proprietor:** "Big" Bruno Kowalski - Surprisingly small man with extensive burn scars who tests every weapon personally before sale
   
6. **TechScav Emporium** - Salvaged ship parts and computers, quality highly variable
   - **Proprietor:** Zeek "Fingers" Patel - Hyperactive tinkerer with prosthetic fingers who claims every piece of junk is "vintage tech, very rare!"

#### Detection Mechanics

**Applicable Skills for Spotting Surveillance:**
- **Investigate:** Primary skill for noticing suspicious behavior
- **Recon:** Military/scout training for threat assessment
- **Streetwise:** Understanding criminal surveillance tactics
- **INT check:** Raw intelligence to notice patterns

**Detection Difficulties:**
Each observer has a base difficulty based on their surveillance skills (see location descriptions above):
- **[Zina "Sparks" Petrova](../../characters/scrap-vulture/zina-sparks-petrova.md):** Very Difficult (12+) - She's cautious and technical
- **[Joric "Slick" Fen](../../characters/scrap-vulture/joric-slick-fen.md):** Difficult (10+) - He's overconfident and chatty  
- **[Grak](../../characters/scrap-vulture/grak.md):** Average (8+) - He's conspicuous, hard to miss a Vargr
- **[Kara "Wrench" Riker](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md):** Difficult (10+) - She's experienced but distracted by expensive tech

**Detection Results:**
- **Success:** Notice the surveillance (see specific descriptions at each location)
- **Effect 4+:** Also spot the Scrap Vulture logo (see telltale signs at each location)

**Progressive Awareness Modifiers:**
These stack as players encounter more surveillance:

- **First surveillance encounter (any location):** No modifier
- **Second surveillance encounter:** DM+2 ("This feels familiar...")
- **Third surveillance encounter:** DM+4 ("We're definitely being watched")
- **Fourth surveillance encounter:** DM+6 ("They're following us everywhere!")

**Logo Recognition Bonuses:**
- Spotted 1 logo previously: DM+1 to spot logos
- Spotted 2 logos previously: DM+2 to spot logos
- Spotted 3+ logos: Automatic logo recognition

**Skill Modifiers:**
- Streetwise: DM+1 (familiar with criminal surveillance)
- Previous military/scout career: DM+1 with Recon
- Currently paranoid/alert: DM+1

**Connecting the Dots:**
- After spotting 2 different observers: Intelligence check (8+) to realize it's coordinated
- After spotting 3 different observers: Automatic realization they're working together
- After spotting 2+ logos: Intelligence check (8+) to connect them as same group
- After spotting 3+ logos: Automatic connection - "The Scrap Vultures are tracking us!"
- Investigate skill grants DM+2 to connecting the dots

### Store Security Systems

All shops on lawless Rech have automated defense systems to prevent violence on premises. Store owners won't tolerate firefights that damage inventory.

#### Automated Defense Responses

**Warning Phase (Round 1 of violence):**
> **Store PA System:** "VIOLENCE DETECTED. CEASE IMMEDIATELY. LETHAL COUNTERMEASURES ENGAGING IN 5 SECONDS."
- Red lights flash, targeting lasers paint all combatants
- Audible weapon systems coming online

**Suppression Phase (Round 2):**
- **Stunner Turrets:** 4 ceiling-mounted stunners fire at ALL combatants
  - Attack: Gun Combat (energy) 2, DM+4 (computer assisted)
  - Damage: 2D stun, ignores armor
  - Targets everyone involved in violence

**Lethal Phase (Round 3+):**
- **Laser Turrets:** Replace stunners
  - Attack: Gun Combat (energy) 3, DM+4
  - Damage: 4D
  - Store owner usually watching via cameras, may negotiate

**Store Owner Reactions:**
- **Korvac:** Ex-military, respects quick surrender, lifetime ban for troublemakers
- **Circuit Breaker Owner:** Demands 200% compensation for any damage, calls street gang allies
- **Doc Hazel:** Offers to patch up wounded for triple price, then kicks everyone out
- **Vacuum Solutions Manager:** Corporate type, calls whatever passes for police, presses charges
- **Big Bruno:** Finds it amusing, but doubles prices for "hot customers"
- **TechScav Owner:** Panics, triggers everything including halon fire suppression

### Tactical Considerations

**For the Players:**
- Shopping separately reduces chance of full party surveillance
- Disguises or varying shopping patterns might help
- Confronting shadows outside stores avoids security
- Quick, efficient shopping minimizes exposure time

**For the Scrap Vultures:**
- They're trying to determine if crew is going for the same prize
- Won't initiate violence in stores (they know the security)
- If spotted, may try to misdirect or offer "friendly" advice
- Gathering intelligence for the ambush mentioned in Scene 5

### Escalation Options

**If crew spots all surveillance:**
- Scrap Vultures know they've been made
- Ambush in Scene 5 becomes more aggressive/desperate
- Might try to sabotage purchases or spread rumors

**If crew remains oblivious:**
- Scrap Vultures learn exact equipment purchased
- Can prepare specific countermeasures
- Ambush perfectly tailored to crew's loadout

**If violence erupts in store:**
- Immediate reputation as troublemakers
- Other stores may refuse service or demand premium
- Scrap Vultures use chaos to escape and regroup
- Local gangs may get involved

### Haggling Mechanics

**Base Prices:** All items start at 120% of book price due to Rech's poor trade status

**Haggling Skills:**
- **Broker:** Primary skill for commercial negotiations
- **Persuade:** Social approach to better prices
- **Streetwise:** Understanding black market pricing
- **Carouse:** Building rapport through shared drinks/stories
- **Admin:** For corporate locations (Vacuum Solutions)
- **Deception:** Lying about competing offers

**Haggling Results (Opposed Broker or Persuade vs. Proprietor's skill):**
- **Failure by 6+:** Insulted proprietor, prices rise to 150%
- **Failure:** No discount, pays 120% 
- **Success:** Reduce to 100% (book price)
- **Success by 4+:** Reduce to 90%
- **Success by 8+:** Reduce to 80% plus thrown-in extras

**Proprietor Haggling Profiles:**
- **Korvac:** Broker 2, DM-2 if military background mentioned, DM+2 vs "civilians"
- **Whisper Chen:** Broker 1, DM-2 if you know the right people, auto-fail if too loud
- **Doc Hazel:** Broker 1, DM-2 if medical emergency, DM+2 if buying weapons
- **Marcus Fitzgerald:** Broker 3, DM-2 if maintaining corporate standards, won't go below 100%
- **Big Bruno:** Broker 2, DM-2 if buying in bulk, enjoys haggling as sport
- **Zeek:** Broker 0, DM-2 if you act impressed by his "finds," easily distracted

**Special Modifiers:**
- **Buying in bulk (5+ items):** DM+1 to haggling
- **Repeat customer:** DM+1 on second visit
- **Cash up front:** DM+1
- **Known troublemaker:** DM-2
- **Time pressure (rushing):** DM-2
- **Competing shoppers present:** DM-1

### Sample Shopping Roleplay

*[Marcus won't go below book price (100%) no matter the roll, but success can include free maintenance kits or suit patches]*

## Side Street Confrontation

### The Setup
After visiting 2-3 shops, the [Scrap Vultures](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md) decide to "have a chat" with the crew. They've chosen their ambush site carefully - a narrow side street between warehouses that the crew must pass through to reach their next destination or return to their lodgings.

### Detection Opportunities

**Approaching the Ambush (50 meters out):**
- **Streetwise or Tactics (8+):** "This route feels wrong - too quiet, too few people"
- **Recon (10+):** Notice Grak's bulk poorly hidden behind a dumpster ahead
- **INT check (12+):** Remember seeing that same vagrant (Joric in disguise) three times today

**Entering the Kill Zone (25 meters):**
- **Recon or Investigate (6+):** Spot movement on fire escapes above
- **Streetwise (8+):** Recognize classic ambush positioning
- **Hearing-based check (10+):** Catch the click of a weapon safety being released

**Success Results:**
- **Basic Success:** 1 round to react before ambush springs
- **Effect 4+:** 2 rounds to prepare or retreat
- **Effect 6+:** Spot all four Scrap Vultures' positions

### The Ambush Site

**Street Layout:**
```
[Warehouse A]  [Fire Escape]  [Warehouse B]
     |              ↓               |
     |         [Dumpster]          |
     |             ↓               |
[Loading Dock]--[STREET]---[Rusted Truck]
     |             ↓               |  
     |         [Crates]            |
     |             ↓               |
[Warehouse C]  [Alley Out]  [Warehouse D]
```

**Dimensions:** 
- Street: 8 meters wide, 40 meters long
- Buildings: 3 stories (10 meters high)
- Multiple levels create 3D combat space

### Tactical Terrain

**Cover Options:**
- **Dumpster:** Full cover, Protection +8, can hide 2 people
- **Rusted Truck:** Full cover one side, Protection +6, climbable to roof
- **Crates (stacked):** Partial cover, Protection +4, can be toppled (STR 10+)
- **Loading Dock:** Elevated position (+1 to hit), partial cover
- **Doorways:** Partial cover, Protection +4, may be locked (Security 8+)

**Vertical Elements:**
- **Fire Escapes:** Both sides, movement between levels (1 round to climb)
- **Warehouse Roofs:** Accessible via fire escapes or Athletics (10+)
- **Windows:** 2nd/3rd floor, some broken (easy entry), some locked
- **Overhead Cables:** Athletics (12+) to swing across street

**Environmental Hazards:**
- **Steam Vents:** Obscure vision (-2 to hit through steam)
- **Oil Puddles:** DEX check (8+) or fall prone when running
- **Unstable Scaffolding:** Can be collapsed with explosives or ramming

### Scrap Vulture Positions & Loadouts

**Initial Positions:**
1. **[Kara "Wrench" Riker](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md)** - Fire escape, 2nd floor, overlooking street
   - Stunner rifle (primary), Autopistol (backup)
   - Smoke grenade x2
   
2. **[Grak](../../characters/scrap-vulture/grak.md)** - Behind dumpster, street level
   - Stun baton (primary), Shotgun (intimidation/backup)
   - Role: Blocker/enforcer
   
3. **[Joric "Slick" Fen](../../characters/scrap-vulture/joric-slick-fen.md)** - Pretending to be drunk vagrant, south entrance
   - Stunner pistol (concealed), Body pistol (backup)
   - Role: Cut off retreat
   
4. **[Zina "Sparks" Petrova](../../characters/scrap-vulture/zina-sparks-petrova.md)** - Warehouse roof with good sightlines
   - Stunner rifle with scope, Medikit
   - Role: Overwatch/medical support

### Escape Routes & Pursuit

**Primary Escape Routes:**
1. **North Alley:** Narrow, leads to market district
   - Pursuit check: Opposed Athletics or DEX
   - Obstacles: Vendor carts (can be overturned)
   
2. **South to Main Street:** Wide, leads to crowds
   - Pursuit check: Opposed Streetwise (to lose in crowd)
   - Complication: Grak positioned to intercept
   
3. **Through Warehouse:** Requires breaking in (STR 10+ or Security 8+)
   - Pursuit check: Opposed Stealth inside building
   - Advantage: Multiple exits, easy to lose pursuers
   
4. **Up and Over:** Via fire escapes to rooftops
   - Pursuit check: Opposed Athletics
   - Risk: DEX check (10+) for roof jumps or fall 1d6 damage

**Cut-off Mechanics:**
- If fleeing immediately: Joric attempts to block (opposed DEX)
- After 2 rounds: Kara radios ahead, 50% chance of additional blockers
- After 4 rounds: Local gangs curious, may help either side for pay

### Engagement Rules & Escalation

**Scrap Vulture Goals:**
1. Surround and contain the crew
2. Demand information about Djinni plans
3. Assess if crew is competition for the Stardust Drifter
4. Intimidate them into backing off or cutting a deal

**Opening Dialogue (Kara shouts from fire escape):**
> "Easy there, friends! Weapons down - we just want to talk! Heard you're shopping for some serious EVA gear. Planning a little salvage run to Djinni, are we? That's our turf, and we don't appreciate competition."

**Escalation Triggers:**
- **Stays Stun Weapons:** As long as crew doesn't use lethal force
- **Switches to Lethal:** If any Scrap Vulture takes 6+ damage or crew uses lethal weapons first
- **Full Retreat:** If Kara or 2+ members are incapacitated

**Information They Want:**
- Are you going to [Djinni](../../systems/djinni.md)?
- Are you after the [Stardust Drifter](../../ships/stardust-drifter.md)?
- Who hired you?
- Would you consider a partnership/buyout?

### Combat Modifiers

**Environmental Modifiers:**
- High ground (fire escape/roof): +1 to hit
- Smoke/steam: -2 to hit through
- Moving target (running): -2 to hit
- Full cover: Cannot be targeted
- Partial cover: -2 to hit, +4 Protection

**Morale & Negotiation:**
- After 3 rounds: Kara may call for ceasefire to talk
- If clearly losing: Scrap Vultures offer to "split the score"
- If clearly winning: Demand 20% cut or they'll make trouble

### Aftermath Consequences

**If Crew Escapes Clean:**
- Scrap Vultures increase surveillance
- Ambush in Scene 5 becomes more aggressive
- May try to sabotage hired pilot

**If Crew Wins Fight:**
- Reputation as dangerous (shops may refuse service or charge more)
- Scrap Vultures seek revenge later
- Can interrogate captured members (learn about [Makhidkarun](../../factions/makhidkarun.md) connection)

**If Crew Negotiates:**
- Possible temporary alliance
- Scrap Vultures may share (false) intel
- Sets up betrayal potential later

**If Crew Loses/Captured:**
- Stripped of weapons and some gear
- Interrogated about plans
- Released with warning: "Stay away from Djinni"
- Must re-acquire lost equipment

### Quick Reference Combat Stats

**[Scrap Vultures](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md):**
- **[Kara "Wrench" Riker](../../characters/scrap-vulture/kara-wrench-riker.md):** Gun Combat (energy) 2, Gun Combat (slug) 2, Tactics 2, Leadership 2
  - Stunner Rifle (2D stun, 100m), Autopistol (3D-3, 10m), Smoke grenades
- **[Grak](../../characters/scrap-vulture/grak.md) (Vargr):** Melee 2, Gun Combat (slug) 1, Intimidate 2
  - Stun baton (2D stun, melee), Shotgun (4D, 50m), Natural weapons (1D+1)

## Void Gambit Preparations & Sabotage

### Kaelen's Risk Assessment

When hiring [Captain Kaelen](../../characters/the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md), she will ask pointed questions to assess risk:

**Kaelen's Questions:**
> "Before we finalize terms, I need to know what I'm getting into. Are you hot? Is anyone going to try to stop us - corporate security, rival crews, angry exes? I don't mind a little heat, but I need to know what precautions to take."

**Player Response Effects:**
- **Honest about threats:** Kaelen appreciates honesty, hires guards, +1 to relationship
- **Downplay risks:** Kaelen suspicious but proceeds, minimal security
- **Lie (no threats):** Kaelen finds out later, -2 to relationship, may demand hazard pay
- **Offer extra payment:** Kaelen definitely hires guards, stays loyal even under pressure

### Security Measures (if warned)

**Kaelen's Response to Known Threats:**
- Hires 2-4 dock guards (depends on threat level described)
- Posts rotating watch schedule on the ship
- Installs motion sensors on boarding ramps
- Keeps engines on standby for emergency departure

**Guard Stats:**
- **Rech Dock Guards:** Gun Combat 1, Melee 1, Recon 1
- Equipment: Autopistols, comm units, flak jackets
- Cost: Cr100/day per guard (Kaelen covers if warned, otherwise crew pays)

### Scrap Vulture Sabotage Attempt

**Timing:** Night before departure (or when ship is least guarded)

**Sabotage Goals (in order of priority):**
1. Plant tracking device (to follow to Djinni)
2. Damage jump drive calibration (delay departure 1-2 days)
3. Steal navigation data (learn exact destination)
4. Plant false intel about "better" salvage elsewhere

**Sabotage Team:**
- **[Zina "Sparks" Petrova](../../characters/scrap-vulture/zina-sparks-petrova.md)** - Technical lead
- **[Joric "Slick" Fen](../../characters/scrap-vulture/joric-slick-fen.md)** - Lookout/distraction
- **Two hired local techs** - Expendable help

### Sabotage Resolution

**If No Guards:**
- Automatic success on primary goal (tracking device)
- 50% chance of secondary goal success
- Evidence left behind: Electronics (8+) to find tracker, Investigate (10+) to find signs of tampering

**If Guards Posted:**

**Detection Phase:**
- Guards' Recon vs Saboteurs' Stealth (opposed)
- Joric creates distraction: +2 to saboteur stealth
- Night time: -1 to guards' rolls
- Motion sensors: +2 to guards' rolls

**Outcomes:**
- **Guards Win by 4+:** Saboteurs spotted approaching, can be captured
- **Guards Win:** Saboteurs abort mission, escape
- **Tie:** Partial success - tracker planted but discovered next morning
- **Saboteurs Win:** Plant tracker, attempt secondary goal
- **Saboteurs Win by 4+:** Complete success, no evidence

**Combat (if detected):**
- Saboteurs flee immediately unless cornered
- Will use smoke grenades and stunners
- Local authorities arrive in 2d6 rounds (but won't pursue hard)

### Kaelen's Navy Intelligence Network

[Captain Kaelen](../../characters/the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md) has contacts in the Imperial Navy from her merchant marine days. She can attempt to gather intel on patrol patterns in the [Djinni](../../systems/djinni.md) system.

**Daily Intel Gathering:**
- **Check:** Straight 2D roll, target 8+
- **Success:** Gain +1 to final patrol timing (cumulative, max +3)
- **Failure:** No progress that day
- **Critical Success (12+):** Gain +2 that day
- **Critical Failure (2):** Bad intel, -1 to total

**Modifiers to Daily Checks:**
- Kaelen has Admin 1: +1 to rolls
- Crew assists with Cr1000 "gift": +1 to that day's roll
- After 3 days trying: -1 (sources getting nervous)
- If crew has Navy contacts: +2

**Information Levels:**
- **+0:** "The Steadfast patrols regularly but unpredictably"
- **+1:** "They follow a rough pattern, with gaps"
- **+2:** "My contact knows the general schedule"
- **+3:** "I have the next week's patrol route"

### Navy Patrol Distance Table

**Final Check:** When jumping to [Djinni](../../systems/djinni.md), roll 2D + Intel Bonus

**[INS Steadfast's](../../ships/ins-steadfast.md) Position When Crew Arrives:**

| Roll Result | Hours Away | Initial Status | Notes |
|------------|------------|----------------|-------|
| 5 or less | 8 hours | Active sensor sweep in progress | Worst case - almost immediate detection |
| 6 | 8 hours | Standard patrol | Will detect jump flash quickly |
| 7 | 9 hours | Investigating asteroid | Delayed response possible |
| 8 | 9 hours | Standard patrol | Normal detection time |
| 9 | 10 hours | Crew rotation | -1 to their sensor checks |
| 10 | 10 hours | Maintenance cycle | -2 to initial response |
| 11 | 11 hours | Far side of system | Maximum normal distance |
| 12 | 11 hours | Dealing with mining drone malfunction | Distracted, slow response |
| 13 | 12 hours | Docked at monitoring station | Needs 30 min to undock |
| 14+ | 12 hours | Drive maintenance | Cannot pursue for 2 hours |

**Detection Mechanics:**
- Jump flash automatically detected within 2 hours
- Time to intercept = Hours Away - 2 (minimum 6 hours)
- Crew has this window to complete mission

**Steadfast Response Speeds:**
- **Hot pursuit:** 6G acceleration (if very suspicious)
- **Standard intercept:** 4G acceleration (normal response)
- **Cautious approach:** 2G acceleration (if dealing with problems)

### Information Kaelen Can Provide

**Good (+1 to +2):**

> "I've got their schedule. If we jump in at [specific time], the [Steadfast](../../ships/ins-steadfast.md) should be refueling at the monitoring station. We'll have maximum time, maybe 12 hours if we're lucky."
