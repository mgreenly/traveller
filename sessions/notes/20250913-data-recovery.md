# Session Notes: September 13, 2025
## Data Recovery and Escape

The crew successfully recovered the *Stardust Drifter* and its data core from the Djinni system.

They used the Photovoric Capacitor (alien life form) in the ship's lab to disable the Scrap Vultures' ship with an EMP blast.

The disabled Scrap Vultures were likely picked up by the Imperial Navy corvette INS Steadfast.

The crew escaped the Djinni system and jumped to K'Kirka.
