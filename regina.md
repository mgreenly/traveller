# Regina System

**System:** Regina
**Subsector:** Regina
**Sector:** Spinward Marches
**Allegiance:** Third Imperium

## Overview

Regina is a bustling, high-population, and technologically advanced world, serving as the capital of the Regina subsector in the Spinward Marches. It's a vibrant hub of trade and political activity, making it an ideal starting point for a Traveller campaign. The system's location on the frontier of the Imperium, bordering the Zhodani Consulate and Vargr Extents, adds a constant sense of tension and opportunity.

## Celestial Bodies

The Regina system is a trinary star system, with the primary star being Lusor. The main world, Regina, is a moon orbiting the gas giant Assiniboia. There are several other celestial bodies in the system, including other habitable worlds, but Regina is the most significant.

## The World of Regina

*   **Population:** Hundreds of millions. While not a high-population world by the standards of the Imperium's core worlds, it is highly urbanized.
*   **Government:** An impersonal bureaucracy, with a significant Imperial presence due to its status as a subsector capital. The system is home to a Duke, giving it considerable political importance.
*   **Major Cities:** The capital city is Credo. Other major cities include Kingsbury, Princeps, Corona, Regni, and Atora.
*   **Military:** The system hosts both an Imperial Naval Base and a Scout Base, providing a strong military presence.

## Trade and Travel

Regina is located on the **Spinward Main**, a major trade route that sees a constant flow of ships and goods. This makes it a perfect location for characters looking for transport, cargo, or information.

As requested, Regina has jump routes to two dead-end systems:

*   **Lanth (Jump-1):** A dead-end system that is sparsely populated.
*   **Boughene (Jump-2):** Another dead-end system with its own unique challenges and opportunities.

## Plot Hooks

Regina's rich environment provides numerous opportunities for adventure, especially given the players' starting situations:

*   **The Alien Artifact:** Regina has a known archaeological site with artifacts from the mysterious Ancients. The player's artifact could be a key to this site, or it could attract the attention of researchers, corporations, or even the Zhodani.
*   **The Lab Ship:** The co-owner of the lab ship could be a local corporation, a noble with an interest in exploration, or even the Imperial Navy. The ship itself might be docked at the naval base, requiring the players to negotiate for its release. The lab ship could be the perfect vessel for exploring the dead-end routes from Regina.
