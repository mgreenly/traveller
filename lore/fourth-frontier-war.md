# The Fourth Frontier War (1082-1084)

## Overview
The Fourth Frontier War, also known as the "False War," was a short and inconclusive conflict fought between the Third Imperium and the Zhodani Consulate from 1082 to 1084. Taking place approximately 23 years before the classic Traveller setting year of 1105, this war was the fourth in a series of frontier conflicts in the Spinward Marches sector.

## Timeline
- **Duration:** 1082-1084 (16 months of active fighting)
- **Time from Present:** 21-23 years before the year 1105 (classic Traveller era)
- **Note:** The Fifth Frontier War would later occur in 1107-1110

## Theater of Operations
- Primary fighting occurred in the Jewell Subsector
- Initial Zhodani assaults targeted both Jewell and Regina subsectors
- The attacks stalled at the Imperial borders

## Key Battle
### Battle of Two Suns (1084)
- Fought in the vicinity of Yres (Spinward Marches 1802) and Menorb (Spinward Marches 1803)
- Zhodani commanders attempted to break the Imperial border defense line
- Resulted in decisive Imperial victory
- Zhodani offensive naval forces were badly damaged or destroyed
- Led directly to the armistice agreement

## Armistice Terms
The armistice was signed before instructions for the conduct of the war could be received from Capital, forcing Emperor Strephon to accept the negotiated terms:

### Imperial Losses:
- Lost control of Narval (Spinward Marches 0805)
- Agreed to joint tenancy of Esalin (Spinward Marches 1004) with the Zhodani

### Imperial Gains:
- Regained Margesi (Spinward Marches 1020) from the Sword Worlds
- Regained Saurus (Spinward Marches 1320) from the Sword Worlds
- Both systems had been lost to the Sword Worlds a century before

## Strategic Impact
The war exposed critical weaknesses in Imperial naval strategy:

### Pre-War Strategy: "Crust Defense"
- Major fleet elements positioned well forward at the borders
- Relied on technological and material superiority
- Proven vulnerable when that superiority gap narrowed

### Post-War Strategy: "Elastic Defense"
- Developed in the decade following the war
- Minimum fleet assets initially positioned in border areas
- Much larger reserves held back for strategic commitment
- Provided greater flexibility and defensive depth

## Historical Significance
- Demonstrated the narrowing technological gap between the Imperium and Zhodani
- Forced a complete reassessment of Imperial border defense doctrine
- Set the stage for continued tensions that would eventually lead to the Fifth Frontier War (1107-1110)
- The inconclusive nature of the war left many territorial disputes unresolved
- Earned the nickname "False War" due to its limited scope and inconclusive outcome
