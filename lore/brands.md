## Armor and Protection

### FlexWeave™
**Manufacturer:** Instellarms Ltd.  
**Headquarters:** Regina/Regina (Spinward Marches)  
**Product Type:** Cloth armor material  
**TL:** 7+

FlexWeave™ is the trade name for Instellarms' proprietary ballistic cloth armor fabric. Originally developed for military contracts during the Fourth Frontier War, FlexWeave™ has become the standard for cloth armor throughout the Spinward Marches. The material consists of layered synthetic fibers treated with a proprietary hardening process that maintains flexibility while providing Protection 5.

**Key Features:**
- Lightweight and breathable
- Machine washable at low temperatures
- Available in various colors and patterns
- Maintains effectiveness for 10+ years with proper care

**Common Applications:**
- Military and law enforcement uniforms
- Executive protection vests
- Shipboard emergency gear
- Colonial survival equipment

---

### Guardian Fabric™
**Manufacturer:** SuSAG LIC (Protective Textiles Division)  
**Headquarters:** Rhylanor/Rhylanor (Spinward Marches)  
**Product Type:** Premium cloth armor material  
**TL:** 8-10

Guardian Fabric™ represents SuSAG's entry into the protective textiles market, leveraging their extensive chemical expertise to create an advanced armor cloth. Unlike traditional ballistic weaves, Guardian Fabric™ uses chemically-treated organic polymers that actually harden on impact, distributing kinetic energy across a wider area.

**Key Features:**
- Self-repairing minor tears (TL10 version)
- Temperature regulating properties
- Chemical and flame resistant coating
- 20% lighter than standard cloth armor

**Common Applications:**
- High-end personal protection
- Diplomatic security details  
- Corporate executive protection
- Luxury yacht crew uniforms

**Price Premium:** Guardian Fabric™ typically costs 150-200% of standard cloth armor prices, justified by its superior comfort and additional protective features.

## Weapons

### Havoc Arms Limited
**Manufacturer:** Havoc Arms Limited  
**Headquarters:** Glisten/Glisten (Spinward Marches)  
**Product Lines:** Military small arms, mercenary equipment  
**TL:** 9-12  
**Motto:** "When It Absolutely Must Be Destroyed"

Havoc Arms emerged from the consolidation of three smaller arms manufacturers following the Third Frontier War. Known for rugged, soldier-proof designs that prioritize reliability over refinement, Havoc has become the preferred supplier for mercenary units operating in the Spinward Marches. Their weapons are deliberately over-engineered to function in extreme conditions with minimal maintenance.

**Popular Products:**
- **HA-77 Assault Rifle:** Their signature weapon, featuring tool-free disassembly and the ability to fire after being submerged in mud for 72 hours
- **"Negotiator" Autopistol:** A heavy-frame sidearm favored by shipboard security teams
- **HAL-10 "Peacemaker":** A shotgun designed specifically for shipboard combat with frangible rounds that won't breach hull plating

**Key Features:**
- All weapons come with lifetime warranty (void in Red Zones)
- Modular design allows field conversion between configurations
- Proprietary "BlackBox" ammunition counter and diagnostic system
- Optional biometric locks keyed to registered users

**Corporate Culture:**
Havoc Arms maintains testing facilities on three frontier worlds where weapons undergo extreme environment trials. Their marketing heavily features testimonials from surviving mercenaries and includes a "Wall of Honor" at corporate headquarters displaying weapons returned from combat still functional despite extensive damage.

### Quantum Defense Industries (QDI)
**Manufacturer:** Quantum Defense Industries  
**Headquarters:** Mora/Mora (Spinward Marches)  
**Product Lines:** Experimental energy weapons, smart weapon systems  
**TL:** 12-15  
**Motto:** "Tomorrow's Defense, Today"

Founded by a consortium of university researchers and venture capitalists, QDI represents the cutting edge of personal weapon technology. Their weapons incorporate advanced materials, AI-assisted targeting, and exotic energy systems that push the boundaries of Imperial weapons law. QDI products are extremely expensive and often require specialized training to operate safely.

**Notable Products:**
- **Q-Series Laser Rifles:** Self-adjusting frequency modulation to defeat reflec armor
- **"Mindbridge" Targeting System:** Neural-linked scope that highlights threats based on user's subconscious threat assessment
- **Plasma Pulse Pistol (P3):** Contained plasma bolts with adjustable yield (restricted to military/law enforcement)
- **"Ghost" Gauss Needler:** Near-silent electromagnetic needle rifle for covert operations

**Key Features:**
- Integrated smart-weapon AI requires software licensing (Cr1,000/year)
- Weapons include full diagnostic suite and predictive maintenance alerts
- Quantum encryption on all electronic components
- Factory-only maintenance required to maintain warranty

**Controversies:**
- Several QDI prototypes have been banned after field testing "incidents"
- Accused of industrial espionage against competitors
- Their smart weapon AIs have occasionally exhibited concerning autonomy
- Suspected ties to Imperial intelligence services

**Availability:**
QDI weapons require background checks and are typically only sold at Class A starports. Many systems prohibit civilian ownership of QDI products, and their weapons are automatically flagged by most security scanners.

## Starship Components

### Shipbuilders

**General Products LIC** - Megacorporation producing everything from 100-ton scouts to capital ships; known for standardized, reliable designs with readily available spare parts.

**Bilstein Yards** - Military contractor specializing in system defense boats and patrol corvettes; holds exclusive contracts with several subsector navies.

**Ling-Standard Products** - Megacorporation famous for modular ship designs allowing easy customization; their Type-S Scout is ubiquitous throughout the Imperium.

**Akerut Shipwrights** - Traditional family-owned yards operating for over 400 years; builds bespoke luxury yachts for nobility and wealthy merchants.

**Frontier Vessels Incorporated** - Specializes in ruggedized ships for frontier operations; their designs feature oversized fuel processors and redundant life support.

**Zhunastu Industries** - Vilani corporation modernizing ancient Vilani designs; their ships feature distinctive spherical hulls and are prized for fuel efficiency.

### Component Manufacturers

**Delgado Trading LIC** - Premier jump drive manufacturer; their "WhisperJump" series produces 30% less jump flash, favored by scouts and intelligence services.

**Rhylanor Gravitics** - Leading producer of maneuver drives and artificial gravity systems; their drives are 15% more efficient but require specialized maintenance.

**Vincennes Fusion Systems** - Power plant specialist offering compact, high-output fusion reactors; standard equipment on military vessels despite premium pricing.

**Aegis Defense Solutions** - Ship weapons and defensive screen manufacturer; their triple turrets are considered the gold standard for civilian armed merchants.

**Nakamura Electronics** - Ship computer and sensor array manufacturer; their Model/7 computer includes proprietary threat assessment software banned in some systems.

**Kellerman Life Support Ltd.** - Environmental systems and emergency equipment supplier; their "Last Breath" emergency suits have saved thousands of lives during decompression events.

## Consumer Goods

### Fashion & Clothing

**Stellar Threads™**
**Headquarters:** Regina/Regina (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Mid-range fashion and everyday wear
**Slogan:** "From Core to Rim"

The most ubiquitous clothing brand in the Spinward Marches, Stellar Threads operates factory outlets in every Class B+ starport. Known for durable, culture-neutral designs that won't offend on any world. Their "Universal Traveller" line includes clothing with hidden pockets for documentation and currency, wrinkle-resistant fabrics, and styles that adapt from business to casual wear.

**VoidWear Industries**
**Headquarters:** Rhylanor/Rhylanor (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Shipboard and zero-g apparel
**Slogan:** "Designed for Life Between the Stars"

Specializing in clothing for spacers, VoidWear produces magnetic-soled boots, temperature-regulating undersuits, and the famous "GravShift" jacket that looks professional in any gravity level. Their clothes feature sealed seams compatible with emergency vacc suit attachments and fabrics that resist static buildup common in ship environments.

### Food & Beverage

**Jump Juice™ Coffee**
**Headquarters:** Mora/Mora (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Caffeinated beverages and coffee substitutes
**Slogan:** "Keeps You Flying"

The preferred caffeine delivery system for spacers across the Marches. Jump Juice operates kiosks in most starports, serving everything from traditional Terran coffee to Vilani khafa and Vargr-strength stimulant brews. Their signature "Red-Eye Special" is rumored to cure jump-lag, though medical professionals strongly disagree. Sells portable brew units designed for shipboard use.

**Cosmic Grounds**
**Headquarters:** Lunion/Lunion (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Premium coffee and tea
**Slogan:** "Imported from a Thousand Worlds"

Upscale coffee chain catering to business travelers and naval officers. Each location features a "World of the Month" featuring authentic beans from that system. Known for their exclusive contracts with agricultural worlds and their patented cryo-preservation that maintains flavor during jump transport. Significantly more expensive than Jump Juice but considered a status symbol.

**Quick-Grav Burgers**
**Headquarters:** Glisten/Glisten (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Fast food chain
**Slogan:** "Fast as Falling"

Found in every major starport and most frontier settlements, Quick-Grav serves standardized meals in under 3 minutes. Menu features synthetic meat patties, vat-grown vegetables, and their famous "Meteor Fries." Popular with crews on tight schedules and families traveling with children. Franchise opportunities available on newly-settled worlds.

**Emperor's Table**
**Headquarters:** Capital/Core (Franchise throughout Imperium)
**Product Type:** Standardized dining
**Slogan:** "The Taste of Civilization"

Imperial-wide restaurant chain offering "authentic Core sector cuisine" adapted for local tastes. More upscale than Quick-Grav but still affordable. Each location required to maintain the same 50 core menu items plus 20 local specialties. Popular for business lunches and as a "safe" choice when entertaining offworld guests.

**Parsec Pizza**
**Headquarters:** Regina/Regina (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Delivery and takeout
**Slogan:** "30 Parsecs in 30 Minutes" (Note: Not actually possible)

Specializes in delivery to ships in port and station quarters. Menu includes "traditional" pizzas plus local variations like Vargr-style raw meat toppings or Aslan spiced variants. Their insulated delivery containers maintain temperature for up to 2 hours and are designed to fit through standard airlock pass-throughs.

### Personal Care & Hygiene

**CleanSpace Solutions**
**Headquarters:** Trin/Trin's Veil (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Shipboard hygiene products
**Slogan:** "Because Space Shouldn't Smell"

Produces water-free cleaning products essential for long jumps. Their dry shampoo, sonic toothbrushes, and enzyme-based laundry tablets are standard equipment on most commercial vessels. Also manufactures the "FreshLock" personal hygiene kit required by many passenger lines for their crews.

**Imperial Standard Pharmaceuticals**
**Headquarters:** Rhylanor/Rhylanor (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Over-the-counter medications
**Slogan:** "Your Health, Our Mission"

Largest supplier of basic medications in the subsector. Products include anti-radiation tablets, jump-sickness remedies, and broad-spectrum antibiotics. Every starport pharmacy stocks their distinctive blue-and-gold packages. Known for consistent quality and multilingual labeling that includes pictographic instructions for low-tech worlds.

### Entertainment & Recreation

**StellarStream Entertainment**
**Headquarters:** Mora/Mora (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Digital entertainment subscription
**Slogan:** "A Universe of Entertainment"

Streaming service that pre-loads content onto ship computers before jump. Subscribers can download up to 1000 hours of video, music, and interactive content per month. Includes news broadcasts from major worlds (with jump-lag delay), educational content, and their popular "Traveller Tales" documentary series about merchant crews.

**Galactic Games Ltd.**
**Headquarters:** Vilis/Vilis (Spinward Marches)
**Product Type:** Physical and digital games
**Slogan:** "Fun Across the Stars"

Produces both traditional board games and neural-interface VR experiences. Their "Port to Port" trading game is found in most crew lounges, while their VR combat simulators are used by mercenary units for training. Recently launched "JumpSpace," a horror-survival game set aboard a misjumped trader that's been banned on three worlds for causing excessive anxiety.

**Frontier Spirits Collective**
**Headquarters:** Distributed (Various frontier worlds)
**Product Type:** Alcoholic beverages
**Slogan:** "Every World Has a Story"

Cooperative of small distilleries from frontier worlds, distributing unique local spirits throughout the Marches. Each bottle includes a QR code linking to the story of its origin world and distillery. Popular items include Bowman Blue (a luminescent liqueur), Vargr Bite (extremely high proof), and Regina Royal (whiskey aged in null-g). Available in most starport duty-free shops.
