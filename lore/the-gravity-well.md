# The Gravity Well (Dive Bar)

**Referee's Note:** This is a modular encounter for a typical downport or fringe station dive bar. It's a place for finding low-level contacts, gathering rumors, and getting into fights. The "Gravity Well" is a franchise, so its familiar grimy layout can be found on countless worlds.

> **Read Aloud:** The sign for "The Gravity Well" is a flickering neon that casts a sickly green glow on the grimy street. Inside, the air is thick with the smell of stale beer, cheap synth-whiskey, and ozone from a malfunctioning air scrubber. The lighting is dim, the music is loud and industrial, and the floor is sticky. A holographic shockball game plays silently above the bar, ignored by the patrons: a mix of weary dockworkers, grizzled spacers in stained jumpsuits, and a few quiet, dangerous-looking individuals nursing their drinks in shadowed booths.

### The Staff

#### The Bartender: "Sully"

> **Read Aloud:** The man behind the bar is a mountain of scarred flesh and faded tattoos. He has one real eye and one cheap, buzzing cybernetic replacement that glows a dull red. He cleans a glass with a dirty rag, his movements slow and deliberate. He doesn't smile, he doesn't greet you. He just waits, his expression a permanent mask of bored indifference.

Sully has been a bartender on the fringe for thirty years. He's heard every lie and every sob story. He's not a source of information for the talkative; he's a source for the observant. He communicates in grunts and nods, but his true language is in the drinks he serves. A regular who orders "The Usual" might get a shot of top-shelf whiskey, while a known snitch might get a watered-down beer.

#### The Bouncer: Grek

> **Read Aloud:** Slumped at a small table near the door is a hulking Vargr whose fur is a patchwork of old scars. He's not officially on the payroll, but he's a permanent fixture. He keeps the peace in exchange for a free tab and a place to stay out of the rain. He ignores the usual drunken scuffles but will intervene with brutal efficiency if anyone pulls a weapon.

Grek is a creature of simple loyalties. If the players get on Sully's good side, Grek will consider them under his protection. If they cause real trouble, he will throw them through the door without a word.

---

### Task Chain: Getting Information from Sully

Sully won't respond to direct questions, but he can be persuaded to point the players in the right direction.

#### Stage 1: Getting His Attention (TN 8+)
The players must show they belong.
*   **The Check:** A **Streetwise** or **Carouse** check.
    *   **Streetwise:** Order a specific, obscure drink known only to fringe travellers.
    *   **Carouse:** Buy a round for the bar without making a big show of it.
*   **Success (8+):** Sully gives a slight, almost imperceptible nod. He now considers the players worth his time. This grants a **+1 bonus** to the check in Stage 2.
*   **Failure (7-):** The players order a fancy, off-world drink or otherwise mark themselves as tourists. Sully serves them with contempt and will ignore them from now on.

#### Stage 2: The Question (TN 8+)
The players can now ask their question, but it must be indirect. Instead of "Where can I find a smuggler?", they should ask, "Who's looking to move some cargo, no questions asked?"
*   **The Check:** A **Streetwise** or **Persuade** check, modified by Stage 1.
*   **Outcome:**
    *   **Minimal Success (Failure):** Sully just shrugs and moves to the other end of the bar.
    *   **Standard Success (8+):** Sully doesn't say a word. He simply wipes the bar, then taps his finger on a specific spot on the counter before walking away. A moment later, a person from a nearby table will approach the players.
    *   **Exceptional Success (Effect 4+):** Sully quietly slides a drink down the bar. "On the house," he grunts. He then jerks his head almost imperceptibly toward a booth in the back where the most reliable contact is sitting. He's just saved the players from dealing with a less reputable source.
