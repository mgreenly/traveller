# 100 Traveller Scenes

A collection of modular encounter scenes for Traveller campaigns. Each scene is designed to be dropped into any appropriate setting with minimal preparation. Skill checks and difficulty numbers can be adjusted based on local conditions and player capabilities.

## Starport & Customs Scenes

1. A customs official suddenly develops a strong interest in your cargo manifest after receiving a discrete message on his tablet. **Streetwise (8+)** to notice the message, **Investigate (9+)** to determine its source.

2. The starport's automated baggage system "malfunctions," sending your gear to the wrong loading bay where suspicious figures are already examining it. **Athletics (7+)** to reach it quickly, **Gun Combat or Melee (8+)** if confrontation ensues.

3. A well-dressed corporate executive approaches you at the gate, claiming your ship matches one used in a recent "incident" and offering to "clear up any confusion" for a fee. **Advocate (8+)** to know your rights, **Streetwise (9+)** to detect if this is legitimate or a scam.

## Urban & Social Scenes

4. The travellers sense they're being followed through the city streets. **Recon (8+)** to spot the tail, **Stealth (9+)** to lose them, **Streetwise (8+)** to determine their affiliation.

5. A street vendor insists your Credits are counterfeit and threatens to call security unless you pay a "verification fee." **Broker (7+)** to confirm your Credits are genuine, **Intimidate (8+)** to shut down the scam.

6. The local news reports a terrorist bombing at a facility you visited yesterday, and security footage shows someone who looks remarkably like one of the travellers. **Deception (9+)** to establish an alibi, **Investigate (10+)** to uncover who's framing you.

7. Your hotel room has been searched while you were out, but nothing appears to be missing. **Investigate (8+)** to determine what they were looking for, **Electronics (9+)** to find hidden surveillance devices.

8. A drunk patron at the local bar loudly accuses the travellers of being Imperial spies and challenges them to prove otherwise. **Carouse (7+)** to defuse with drinks, **Persuade (8+)** to talk him down, **Melee (8+)** if it comes to blows.

## Corporate & Industrial Scenes

9. The corporate facility you're visiting has a sudden "security drill" that locks down all exits for an hour with no explanation. **Electronics (9+)** to access security feeds, **Engineer (Mechanical) (8+)** to override door locks.

10. A Makhidkarun executive offers you a lucrative contract, but the job description is suspiciously vague and requires you to sign an extensive NDA first. **Advocate (8+)** to spot problematic clauses, **Streetwise (9+)** to sense hidden agendas.

12. Your corporate contact misses a scheduled meeting, and their assistant claims they've been "reassigned to another project" with no forwarding information. **Admin (8+)** to access personnel records, **Investigate (9+)** to trace their real whereabouts.
