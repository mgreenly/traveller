# Starport Customs Encounter

**Referee's Note:** This is a modular encounter for a standard Imperial customs checkpoint. It can be used whenever the players are entering a new system and need to be processed by local authorities. The outcome is designed to be a potential complication and time-sink, not a campaign-ender.

> **Read Aloud:** The customs hall is a vast, sterile space that echoes with the quiet hum of security scanners and the murmur of a hundred conversations. The air smells of ozone and recycled air. A long queue of travellers snakes towards a row of armored booths. The process is slow, methodical, and intimidating. Finally, it's your turn. You step forward to a booth, placing your gear on the scanner belt and your travel documents on the counter.

### The Agent: Officer Valerik

> **Read Aloud:** The man in the booth is in his mid-40s, with tired eyes and a face that has seen too many long shifts. His uniform is neat but shows signs of wear at the collar and cuffs. He doesn't look like a zealot, just a man ground down by bureaucracy. He scans your documents with a practiced, weary motion, his expression unreadable. "Purpose of your visit?" he asks, his voice flat and devoid of emotion.

---

### Task Chain: Clearing Customs

#### Stage 1: The First Impression (TN 8+)
The initial interaction sets the tone. Valerik is looking for any reason to be suspicious.
*   **The Check:** A **Persuade (Etiquette)** or **Deception** check.
    *   **Persuade (Etiquette):** Be polite, professional, and have your documents in order.
    *   **Deception:** Have a convincing and well-rehearsed cover story.
*   **Success (8+):** Valerik nods, satisfied. He sees dozens of people like you every day. This grants a **+1 bonus** to any checks made in Stage 3.
*   **Failure (7-):** You seem nervous, your story has a small hole, or your documents are out of order. Valerik's eyes narrow slightly. He decides to take a closer look. This imposes a **-1 penalty** to any checks made in Stage 3.

#### Stage 2: The Complication
Regardless of the first impression, the baggage scanner pings with an ambiguous alert.
> **Read Aloud:** The baggage scanner chimes, flashing a yellow icon on Valerik's screen. He leans in, his expression souring. "Well, what do we have here?" he says, pointing to an anomaly in your luggage. "Scanner's flagging an object with a complex energy signature and high-density metallic components. Looks like more than standard personal gear. I'm going to need a better explanation."

This is the critical point where the players must decide their approach.

#### Stage 3: The Resolution
The players have three main paths: talk their way out, bribe their way out, or bluff their way out.

---
#### **Path A: The Honest Approach (TN 8+)**
*For when the item is legal, but looks suspicious.*
*   **The Check:** An **Advocate**, **Persuade**, or **Profession (relevant skill)** check (modified by Stage 1).
    *   **Advocate:** Argue the legality of the item based on Imperial commercial code.
    *   **Persuade:** Convince him it's a misunderstanding or a vital piece of equipment for your legitimate business.
    *   **Profession:** Explain the technical reasons why the item is harmless and necessary for your work.
*   **Outcome:**
    *   **Success (8+):** Valerik sighs, clearly not wanting the extra paperwork. "Fine. Just make sure it's properly registered next time." He stamps your documents and waves you through.
    *   **Failure (7-):** "Your explanation is not sufficient." **Proceed to the Detainment outcome.**

---
#### **Path B: The Bribe**
*For when you want to make a problem go away quickly.*
**Step 1: Reading the Room (TN 8+)**
*   **The Check:** A **Streetwise** or **Investigate** check.
*   **Success (8+):** You're confident he's open to a "discretionary fee." **Proceed to Step 2.**
*   **Failure (7-):** You can't get a read on him. Attempting the bribe is now a pure gamble.

**Step 2: Making the Offer (TN 8+)**
*   **The Check:** A **Broker** check. The check is made with a **-2 penalty** if you failed Step 1.
*   **Outcome:**
    *   **Success (8+):** You slide a cred-chip over with your documents. Valerik glances at it, then back at his screen. "Ah, I see you've paid the local 'equipment tariff.' My mistake." He pockets the chip and stamps your papers. The bribe costs a few hundred Credits.
    *   **Failure (7-):** Your offer was clumsy or insulting. His face hardens. "Are you attempting to bribe an Imperial customs officer?" **Proceed to the Detainment outcome with an Escalation.**

---
#### **Path C: The Smuggler's Gambit (TN 9+)**
*For when the item is actively illegal and concealed.*
*   **The Check:** A high-stakes **Deception** or **Streetwise** check (modified by Stage 1). This assumes the players have already taken precautions (hidden compartments, scanner spoofing, etc.).
    *   **Deception:** Weave a convincing lie about the object. "That's a calibrated diagnostic tool for high-frequency atmospheric sensors. We have a contract with the local survey office."
    *   **Streetwise:** Use your knowledge of official procedures to bluff. "That's a Class-4 industrial component. It's exempt under the Interstellar Commerce Mandate 34-Gamma. Do you need me to show you the reg?"
*   **Outcome:**
    *   **Success (9+):** Valerik is suspicious, but your explanation is just plausible enough that challenging it would create a mountain of paperwork he doesn't want to deal with. He scowls, stamps your documents, and waves you through with a warning to "get your gear properly cataloged."
    *   **Failure (8-):** "That doesn't match the energy profile. Open the bag. Now." The illegal item is discovered. **Proceed to the Detainment outcome with a Severe Escalation.**

---
### Final Outcomes

#### Detainment
> **Read Aloud:** Valerik presses a button on his console. "I'm going to need you to step into the secondary inspection office. My supervisor will want to have a word with you." Two armed security officers arrive to escort you to a small, grey room with a metal table and nothing else.

*   **Standard Detainment:** You are left to cool your heels for 1D3 hours. Eventually, a new, higher-ranking officer arrives, reviews your case, gives you a stern lecture about wasting Imperial time, and lets you go with a warning. You've lost time and are now in the local security database.
*   **Detainment with Escalation (from a failed bribe):** The new officer is much less friendly. The *suspicious* item is confiscated, and you are hit with a hefty fine (1D6 x 1,000 Credits) on top of the time lost. Getting your gear back will require a lawyer and more time.
*   **Detainment with Severe Escalation (from failed smuggling):** The discovery of *illegal* goods is a serious crime. The items are permanently confiscated. You are fined (2D6 x 1,000 Credits) and held for 2D6 hours. You are now flagged in the subsector-wide security database as a potential smuggler, which may lead to future complications.
