# The Third Frontier War (979-986)

## Overview
The Third Frontier War was the longest and most devastating of the early Frontier Wars, fought between the Third Imperium and the Zhodani Consulate from 979 to 986. This seven-year conflict ended nearly 350 years of uneasy peace and resulted in the only clear Zhodani military victory over the Imperium. Taking place approximately 126 years before the classic Traveller setting year of 1105, this war had profound political consequences including the forced abdication of Emperor Styryx.

## Timeline
- **Duration:** 979-986 (7 years)
- **Time from Present:** 119-126 years before the year 1105 (classic Traveller era)
- **Political Aftermath:** Emperor Styryx's abdication in 989

## Initial Zhodani Strategy
The Zhodani launched simultaneous attacks in multiple subsectors:
- **Primary Thrust:** Attempted to sidestep Jewell by attacking Boughene, Pixie, and Efate
- **Secondary Drive:** Advanced through Lanth Subsector to establish a strategic base

## The Porozlo-Rhylanor Campaign (980-981)

### Capture of Porozlo (980)
- Zhodani forces captured Porozlo for its naval base and gas giant refueling capabilities
- A single large Zhodani task force appeared without warning, proceeding to the gas giants
- Local naval forces engaged but were preoccupied as a second task force drove for Porozlo itself
- The world organization declared Porozlo an "open world" to avoid planetary bombardment
- This gave the Zhodani a critical forward base deep in Imperial territory

### Siege of Rhylanor (980-981)
- Using Porozlo as a support base, Zhodani forces laid siege to Rhylanor
- Rhylanor had a strong naval base but no gas giant for refueling
- Elements from Regina subsector created a long stalemate
- Reinforcements from Sabine Subsector were decisive in breaking the siege in 981
- The siege demonstrated the Zhodani's ability to penetrate deep into Imperial territory

## Military Units of Note
- **154th Squadron:** Deployed new Quiquilat-class Battle Riders, saw action around Lysen and Utloand
- **4518th Lift Infantry Regiment:** Served on Foelen and Farreach

## War Outcome and Armistice Terms (986)

### Territorial Losses
The armistice of 986 resulted in significant Imperial losses:
- Over twenty worlds ceded to the Zhodani or declared neutral
- Quar and Retinae became independent worlds
- Several systems in Querion and Jewell subsectors ceded to the Zhodani
- Creation of a demilitarized zone along the Imperial border in Vilis Subsector

### Strategic Impact
- Marked the only clear Zhodani military victory over the Imperium
- Demonstrated Imperial deficiencies in response to surprise deep strikes
- Showed the vulnerability of worlds without gas giants for naval defense

## Political Consequences: The Abdication Crisis (989)

### Public Outcry
- Widespread dissatisfaction with the war's handling and outcome
- The peace settlement was viewed as a humiliating defeat
- Military and civilian criticism of Emperor Styryx's leadership

### The Palace Coup (989)
- **Leaders:** General Dienne and the Imperial Guard
- **Support:** Imperial General Staff and most of the Imperial armed forces
- **Opposition:** The IISS bodyguard was killed in a brief firefight with the Aslan Guard regiment
- **Outcome:** Emperor Styryx was found "cowering in his personal fresher" and forced to abdicate at gunpoint
- **Succession:** Styryx abdicated in favor of his eldest son, Gavin

## Historical Significance
- Ended 350 years of relative peace between the Imperium and Zhodani
- Demonstrated the effectiveness of deep strike tactics using captured forward bases
- Led to significant political instability within the Imperium
- The territorial losses and subsequent abdication crisis weakened Imperial prestige
- Set precedents for military involvement in Imperial succession
- The demilitarized zones created would remain points of tension leading to future conflicts

## Legacy
The Third Frontier War represented a low point in Imperial military history, with the Zhodani successfully executing a complex multi-pronged offensive that penetrated deep into Imperial space. The war's unpopular conclusion directly caused the first forced abdication of an Emperor in the Third Imperium's history, establishing that military failure could have dire political consequences even for the Emperor himself.
