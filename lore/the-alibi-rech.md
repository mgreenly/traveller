# The Alibi (Rech Location)

**Location:** Port District, Rech  
**Type:** Dive bar / Spacer hangout

## Description

The name "Alibi" was brazenly stolen from upscale corporate bars found elsewhere in the sector—a bit of dark humor from the proprietor who figured no corporate lawyers would ever set foot on Rech to complain. Located deep in the port district, it's a favorite watering hole for independent ship captains, including [Captain Kaelen](../characters/the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md) of the *Void Gambit*.

> **Read Aloud:** The Alibi's entrance is marked by a flickering holo-sign—a knockoff of the corporate chain's elegant glyph, rendered in the cheapest local tech with half the letters burnt out. The door squeals on rusty hinges, opening into a dimly lit space where ancient air filters struggle against Rech's industrial smog, leaving everything with a hazy quality and the mixed smell of cheap alcohol, engine grease, and chemical fumes from the port. Mismatched chairs surround scarred metal tables bolted to the floor. The bar itself is a repurposed section of starship hull plating, dented and stained from years of spilled drinks and bar fights. Behind it, bottles of questionable origin promise oblivion at reasonable prices. This is where ship crews come to forget their troubles, not to make deals—though both happen regularly.

## Function

The Alibi is where spacers go to drink away their sorrows, celebrate successful runs, or find their next crew. While technically "neutral ground," fights break out regularly—they just get taken outside (eventually). The Starport Authority ignores it as long as the violence doesn't spill into the main thoroughfares.

## Clientele

- **Independent Ship Captains:** Like [Captain Kaelen](../characters/the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md), who can often be found at her regular corner table
- **Down-on-their-luck Spacers:** Looking for their next berth or drinking away their last pay
- **Small-time Smugglers:** Bragging about runs that probably didn't happen  
- **Port Workers:** Coming off shift for the cheapest drinks in the district
- **Drifters and Mercs:** Between jobs and looking for trouble

## Security

- **Weapons Check:** There's a sign that says "Check Weapons" but the scanner's been broken for years
- **House Rules:** "Take it outside" - usually enforced after the first thrown punch
- **Enforcement:** One tired bouncer named Krogg who mostly just breaks up fights when they block the bar
- **Unwritten Rule:** Don't mess with the regulars, especially Captain Kaelen's table

## Services

- **Cheap Drinks:** Watered-down but affordable, with questionable origins
- **Ship Crew Recruitment:** Post notices on the grease-stained board by the door
- **Gossip and Rumors:** The bartender hears everything but remembers selectively
- **Captain Kaelen's Table:** She holds informal "office hours" for potential clients

## NPCs

### The Proprietor: "Chance"
A grizzled ex-merchant marine who won the bar in a card game (or so he claims). He appropriated the "Alibi" name as a joke—"Everyone needs an alibi on Rech," he's fond of saying while pouring another questionable drink. He keeps the peace through a combination of free drinks for regulars and a shotgun under the bar.

### The Bartender: Mila
A wiry woman in her 40s with chemical burn scars along her left arm from a cargo handling accident years ago. She wears her gray hair in a practical bun and has the uncanny ability to remember exactly what everyone owes, down to the credit. Never smiles, never frowns, just pours drinks with mechanical precision while listening to everything. She has a habit of cleaning the same glass over and over when she's eavesdropping on interesting conversations. Despite her stern demeanor, she'll slip extra shots to regulars having a particularly bad day.

### The Bouncer: Krogg
A massive human, Krogg stands nearly seven feet tall with the build of someone who spent years in high-gravity environments. His nose has been broken so many times it's practically horizontal, and his knuckles are a map of scar tissue. He wears a faded military surplus jacket with the insignia torn off and spends most of his time slumped on a reinforced stool by the door, looking half-asleep. Don't let this fool you; he can cross the bar in two seconds when trouble starts. His preferred method of breaking up fights is to grab combatants by the collar and literally throw them out the door. He has a soft spot for underdogs and has been known to "accidentally" not see when someone slips out the back to avoid trouble.

### The Regulars

#### [Captain Kaelen](../characters/the-void-gambit/captain-kaelen.md)
Has her own corner table where she conducts business, rolling her Imperial Scout challenge coin across her knuckles while negotiating rates.

#### "Three-Finger" Tam
A garrulous old spacer missing two fingers on his right hand. The story changes each time—mining accident, Vargr bite, plasma leak, angry ex-spouse. Wears multiple layers of mismatched ship patches from vessels he claims to have served on. Actually a decent astrogator when sober, which is rare. Has an encyclopedic knowledge of jump routes through disputed space and will share them for drinks.

#### Old Vijay
A small, weathered man of indeterminate age who claims to have "been everywhere worth going and a few places that weren't." Dresses in an ancient Imperial Navy dress uniform that's two sizes too large. In reality, he's never left Rech but has spent 40 years in the port listening to stories, which he retells as his own adventures. Surprisingly, his secondhand knowledge is often accurate and useful. Always nursing the same warm beer.

#### The Twins (Petra and Pavel)
Not actually related but might as well be. Both are lanky, grease-stained engineers in their 30s who finish each other's sentences and arguments. They run a semi-legal repair shop and are always looking for "spare parts" (don't ask where they come from). Petra has a cybernetic left eye that whirs when she's calculating repair costs. Pavel is missing his right ear from "a disagreement with a fusion cutter." They'll fix anything for the right price and guarantee it'll work for at least one jump.

#### "Duchess" Scarlett Voss
Nobody knows why she's called Duchess, and nobody's brave enough to ask. A severe-looking woman who always sits alone at the bar, drinking imported whiskey she brings herself (she doesn't trust Chance's stock). Wears an expensive but outdated business suit and speaks with a Core Worlds accent. Rumors say she's exiled nobility, a corporate spy, or a bounty hunter. She occasionally buys rounds for the entire bar when she's in a good mood, which creates an nervous atmosphere as everyone wonders why.

#### Jinx the Rigger
A hyperactive young woman covered in tattoos of ship schematics. She claims each tattoo is from a ship she's personally sabotaged or saved, depending on who was paying. Always fidgeting with some piece of equipment—rewiring comms units, modifying tools, or building tiny robots from spare parts. Talks rapidly about technical specifications that go over most people's heads. Will eagerly offer to "improve" anyone's equipment for a small fee, though her modifications are as likely to explode as work correctly.

## Adventure Hooks

- Captain Kaelen can be found here when looking for transport off Rech
- Overhear useful gossip about ship movements and cargo runs
- Recruit desperate crew members with questionable pasts
- Bar fights can lead to unexpected alliances or enemies
- Chance knows everyone and might share information for the right price (or drink)

## Notes

This dive bar is Captain Kaelen's preferred meeting spot for potential clients. She likes it because it's unpretentious, the drinks are cheap, and troublemakers usually can't afford her rates anyway. The atmosphere is rough but honest—a reflection of Rech itself. If you need discrete transport and don't mind a captain who checks her Imperial Scout challenge coin before every decision, this is where you'll find her.
