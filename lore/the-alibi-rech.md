# The Alibi (Rech Location)

**Location:** Port District, Rech  
**Type:** Neutral ground establishment / Black market hub

## Description

The name "Alibi" was brazenly stolen from upscale corporate bars found elsewhere in the sector—a bit of dark humor from the proprietor who figured no corporate lawyers would ever set foot on Rech to complain. Located in the port district just inside the Starport Authority's security perimeter, it represents the closest thing to an upscale establishment on this lawless world.

> **Read Aloud:** The Alibi's entrance is marked by a flickering holo-sign—a knockoff of the corporate chain's elegant glyph, rendered in cheaper local tech. The reinforced door scanner checks for weapons before admitting you to a space that tries hard to maintain respectability. The air filtration actually works, keeping out most of Rech's tainted atmosphere. Mismatched furniture—some genuine imported pieces, others clever local copies—fills a room lit by emergency strips and a few working holo-panels. The bar itself is solid metal, scarred but clean, and the bottles behind it represent the best selection you'll find outside of faction territory.

## Function

The Alibi serves as neutral ground where faction representatives, successful smugglers, and ships' captains can conduct business without the constant threat of violence. The Starport Authority tolerates its existence because it provides a valve for the pressure that builds when rival factions need to negotiate.

## Clientele

- **Ship Captains:** Those successful enough to afford the marked-up drinks
- **Faction Representatives:** Mid-level operatives conducting deniable negotiations  
- **Black Market Dealers:** The more successful ones who can afford the table fees
- **Information Brokers:** Trading in the most valuable currency on Rech
- **Skilled Criminals:** Forgers, slicers, and specialists offering discrete services

## Security

- **Weapons Check:** Mandatory at the door, though everyone knows about the holdout pieces
- **House Rules:** No violence on premises, violators banned for life (often a short sentence on Rech)
- **Discrete Enforcement:** Two heavily augmented bouncers and automated stun systems
- **Unwritten Rule:** What happens at the Alibi stays at the Alibi

## Services

- **Information Brokerage:** Bartender knows who's in port and what they're looking for
- **Neutral Meeting Space:** Private booths for sensitive negotiations (Cr100/hour)
- **Black Market Connections:** Can arrange introductions for a finder's fee
- **Discrete Services:** Forgers and specialists often work the back tables

## NPCs

### The Proprietor: "Chance"
A former corporate security executive who claims to have worked for an upscale bar chain before Rech's collapse. He appropriated the "Alibi" name as a joke—"Everyone needs an alibi on Rech," he's fond of saying. He maintains the establishment with an iron hand and a diplomat's smile, taking a percentage of every deal made under his roof and ensuring everyone follows the rules.

### Regular Operators
The back tables usually host 2-3 specialists at any given time:
- Forgers creating false identities
- Slicers offering data services
- Fixers arranging discrete transportation
- Information brokers trading in rumors and facts

## Adventure Hooks

- The party needs to find a specific person or crew on Rech
- A faction representative wants to hire deniable assets
- Black market goods are available here that can't be found elsewhere
- Information about faction politics or hidden routes through the lawless zone
- Someone is offering bounties on specific targets

## Notes

This establishment represents Rech's attempt at civilization within chaos. It's not truly safe—nothing on Rech is—but it's safer than the alternatives. The mix of desperate criminals, ambitious smugglers, and faction operatives creates a powder keg atmosphere where fortunes can be made or lives can end with a single wrong word.
