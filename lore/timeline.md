# Timeline and Dating Systems

## Imperial Calendar

The Third Imperium uses the Imperial Calendar (IC), established at the founding of the Imperium as a universal dating system. Years are counted from the founding of the Third Imperium (Year 0), which corresponds to 4523 A.D. in Earth reckoning.

### Date Format
- Years marked with IC (Imperial Calendar), IY (Imperial Year), or TI (Third Imperium)
- Days use Julian numbering: 001-365 consecutively
- Standard format: Day-Year (e.g., "206-1107" = 206th day of Imperial Year 1107)

### Conversion to Earth Calendar
**Imperial Year + 4523 = Earth A.D.**
- 0 Imperial = 4523 A.D. (Founding of Third Imperium)
- 1105 Imperial = 5628 A.D. (Classic campaign setting)
- 1107 Imperial = 5630 A.D. (Fifth Frontier War begins)

## Major Historical Periods

### The Classic Golden Age (1100-1116 Imperial)
The default era for most Traveller campaigns. A period of relative peace and prosperity across the Imperium, though punctuated by the Fifth Frontier War in the Spinward Marches. Most published adventures assume this timeframe.

**Key characteristics:**
- Stable Imperial government
- Active exploration and trade
- Growing tensions with Zhodani Consulate
- Technology plateauing at TL15 in core worlds

## The Frontier Wars

### Fifth Frontier War (1107-1110 Imperial)
The most recent major conflict between the Third Imperium and the Zhodani Consulate in the Spinward Marches.

**Timeline:**
- **206-1107:** IISS recalls all scout vessels and retired personnel
- **1107-1109:** Active warfare across multiple subsectors
- **120-1110:** Armistice declared between Zhodani Consulate and Imperial Admiralty
- **1110-1116:** Uneasy peace and reconstruction

**Campaign Impact:**
- Campaigns set before 1107: Building tensions, espionage, preparations
- Campaigns during 1107-1110: Active war zone, military operations
- Campaigns after 1110: War veterans, reconstruction, unresolved tensions

### Previous Frontier Wars
- **First Frontier War:** 589-604 Imperial
- **Second Frontier War:** 615-620 Imperial
- **Third Frontier War:** 979-986 Imperial
- **Fourth Frontier War:** 1082-1084 Imperial

Each war has seen escalating involvement and technology, with longer periods of peace between later conflicts.

## Campaign Start Dates

### Common Campaign Years

**1105 Imperial (Classic Default)**
- Most published materials assume this date
- Pre-war tensions building
- Maximum freedom for player actions
- All published NPCs at their "standard" positions

**1107 Imperial (War Beginning)**
- Dramatic events unfolding
- Military campaign opportunities

- War veterans as PCs
- Reconstruction efforts

## Other Dating Systems

### Vilani Calendar
Still used in some old Vilani sectors, counting from the founding of the First Imperium (approximately -4045 Imperial).

### Solomani Calendar  
Earth's A.D. system, still used in the Solomani Sphere. Add 4523 to Imperial dates for A.D. equivalent.

### Zhodani Olympiad
The Zhodani count time in Olympiads (three Terran years each), currently in their 35th century of recorded history.

### Aslan Calendar
Based on the orbit of Kusyu (Kilrai'), approximately 320 standard days. Each Aslan clan may also maintain their own dating from significant events.

## Useful Date References

### Standard Measurements
- **Standard Day:** 24 standard hours
- **Standard Week:** 7 standard days  
- **Standard Year:** 365 standard days
- **Jump Duration:** 148 + 6D hours (roughly one week)

### Key Dates for Campaigns
- **001 each year:** Holiday Day (New Year) - Imperial holiday
- **126 each year:** Founding Day - Celebrates Third Imperium establishment
- **245 each year:** Arbellatra Day - Honors the first Alkhalikoi Emperor

## Referee Notes

### Using the Timeline
- The 1105-1116 period offers maximum flexibility
- Earlier periods require adjusting technology and political situations
- Later periods must account for canonical events
- Alternative timelines can diverge at any point

### Campaign Continuity
- Keep a campaign calendar tracking the current date
- Account for jump travel time (roughly 1 week per jump)
- Track major events that might affect the PCs
- Use news lag (information travels at jump speed) for dramatic effect

### Historical Events as Adventures
- Previous Frontier Wars offer different campaign styles
- Peacetime between wars has espionage and trade opportunities  
- Major historical events can be reimagined with PC involvement
- "What if" scenarios can explore alternate timelines
