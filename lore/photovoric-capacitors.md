# Photovoric Capacitors

## Classification
**Scientific Name:** *Crystallus photovoricus*  
**Common Names:** Djinn Sparks, Energy Leeches, Niflheim Crystals  
**Discoverer:** [Altaira Aros](../characters/altaira.md), xenobiologist  
**Discovery Date:** Imperial Year 1098 (7 years before present)  
**Native System:** [Djinni](../systems/djinni.md), specifically planet Niflheim  
**Status:** Extreme hazard - containment protocols mandatory  

## Physical Description
Photovoric Capacitors appear as crystalline entities approximately the size of a clenched human fist. Their structure exhibits:
- Semi-translucent crystalline matrix with internal fractal geometries
- Faint bioluminescence that pulses in response to ambient electromagnetic fields
- Color shifts from pale blue to deep violet depending on energy saturation
- Mass of approximately 0.3kg despite appearing to have hollow internal chambers
- Surface temperature remains at exactly -40°C regardless of environment

## Biological Properties
Despite their crystalline appearance, Photovoric Capacitors are classified as living organisms:
- Silicon-based biochemistry with traces of exotic elements not found naturally
- No apparent metabolism in traditional sense - sustenance derived from electromagnetic radiation
- Reproduction method unknown - no specimens observed dividing or spawning
- Lifespan estimated at 10,000+ standard years based on isotopic dating
- Display rudimentary awareness of energy sources, orienting toward them

## Energy Anomaly
The defining characteristic that earned them their name:
- Absorb electromagnetic radiation across entire spectrum (hence "photovoric")
- Store energy in quantum-locked crystalline lattice structure
- **Critical Anomaly:** Output energy exceeds input by factor of 3-5x, violating thermodynamic laws
- Discharge occurs as omnidirectional electromagnetic pulse when saturation threshold reached
- Effective range of discharge: 500-meter radius for unshielded electronics

## Ancient Engineering Evidence

### Artificial Origin Indicators
Dr. Altaira Aros's final research notes contained compelling evidence of engineered origin:
- Crystalline structure contains microscopic channels arranged in perfect mathematical spirals
- Genetic markers (if they can be called that) show signs of deliberate sequencing
- Internal quantum resonance chambers match Ancient artifact energy signatures
- Specimens found only near dormant Ancient installations on Niflheim

### Theoretical Purpose
Several hypotheses exist for their original function:
1. **Power Sources:** Living batteries for Ancient technology, explaining energy amplification
2. **Defensive Organisms:** Biological EMP weapons to protect Ancient sites
3. **Sensor Network:** Distributed consciousness nodes for monitoring energy patterns
4. **Dimensional Anchors:** Stabilizers for Ancient experiments with parallel dimensions

### The Consciousness Connection
Most disturbing discovery from recovered data:
- Photovoric Capacitors respond to neural energy patterns
- Can form resonance bonds with complex nervous systems
- Prolonged exposure causes "neural crystallization" - consciousness patterns begin mirroring crystalline structure
- This may explain Ancient consciousness preservation technology

## The Altaira Incident

### Discovery
Altaira Aros discovered the first specimens during an expedition to Djinni's second planet, Niflheim, while investigating Ancient ruins. Her initial reports described them as "the most beautiful and terrifying organisms I've ever encountered."

### Fatal Encounter
According to reconstructed logs:
1. Altaira approached cluster of Photovoric Capacitors near Ancient defense node
2. Her presence triggered dormant Ancient systems
3. Defense systems used Capacitors as amplifiers, creating focused neural disruption field
4. Altaira suffered catastrophic neural trauma from the targeted discharge
5. She survived initial exposure but succumbed to "neural crystallization" three days later

### Final Words
From Dr. Aros's personal logs, Altaira's last coherent statement:
> "They're not just organisms, father. They're components. Living circuits in a machine we can't comprehend. They see through dimensions we can't perceive. I can feel them rewriting my thoughts into their patterns. Don't let them spread. The Ancients created them for something terrible."

## Containment Protocols

### Standard Containment
- Minimum 5cm electromagnetic shielding (gold mesh or equivalent)
- Maintain specimen at -40°C or below
- Limit exposure to energy sources - emergency lighting only
- Never house more than one specimen per facility
- Automatic ejection systems mandatory for containment breach

### Emergency Procedures
1. **Saturation Event:** Evacuate 500m radius, activate EM hardening
2. **Containment Breach:** Initiate facility-wide EMP to drain specimen
3. **Neural Exposure:** Immediate cryogenic suspension of affected personnel
4. **Multiple Specimens:** Evacuate facility - cascade resonance unstoppable
