# Fringe Asset Recovery, LLC

## Overview
**Public Face:** A small, legally registered company specializing in high-risk asset recovery and salvage operations in frontier space. Their public records show a handful of successful, legitimate salvage jobs, from recovering lost mining equipment to towing derelict ships. They are known for their efficiency and for taking on jobs that larger corporations deem too small or too dangerous.

**The Reality:** Fringe Asset Recovery, LLC is a wholly-owned, deniable shell corporation created and controlled by **[Makhidkarun](./makhidkarun.md)**. Its true purpose is to carry out black-ops acquisitions that cannot be traced back to the parent megacorporation.

## Operations and Methods
*   **Plausible Deniability:** The company exists to give Makhidkarun a legal and logistical buffer. If one of their operations goes wrong, the trail ends at a disposable LLC, not a multi-trillion credit Imperial entity.
*   **Hiring Cut-Outs:** Fringe Asset Recovery does not have its own field agents. Instead, it hires independent contractors, mercenary outfits, and pirate crews—like the "Scrap Vultures" aboard the [*Scrap Vulture*](../ships/scrap-vulture.md)—to do the dirty work. This provides another layer of separation. The contractors are paid handsomely but are considered expendable.
*   **Targeted Acquisitions:** Their missions are not random. They are tasked with acquiring specific ships, technologies, research data, or personnel that are of strategic interest to Makhidkarun's long-term goals. The acquisition of the [*Stardust Drifter*](../ships/stardust-drifter.md) is a prime example of their typical operation.

## Adventure Hooks
*   **Following the Paper Trail:** After discovering the contract on the *Scrap Vulture*, the players can investigate Fringe Asset Recovery. This will lead them down a rabbit hole of shell corporations, dummy addresses, and falsified records, giving them a glimpse into the scale of Makhidkarun's clandestine operations.
*   **A Rival Offer:** An agent from Fringe Asset Recovery might approach the players directly, unaware of their previous conflict with the Scrap Vultures. They could offer the players a lucrative contract to "recover" another asset, putting the players in the position of knowingly working for the enemy.
*   **Cleaning House:** If the players become too much of a nuisance, Makhidkarun might use Fringe Asset Recovery to post a bounty on their heads, hiring other mercenary groups to eliminate the threat.
