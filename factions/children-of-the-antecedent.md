# The Children of the Antecedent

**Concept:** A syncretic religion that blends mysticism, fringe science, and ancestor worship, all centered on the belief that the "Ancients" were not just a precursor race, but the literal architects of life in the subsector. They call them the **Antecedents**. Their faith is built around artifacts they cannot understand, interpreting their function as a form of prayer.

---

### Core Beliefs

*   **The Architects of Life:** The Children believe the Antecedents seeded the galaxy with life and that all sentient species are their "descendants," whether biologically or spiritually. They believe the Antecedents did not die out but instead "ascended," transcending the physical universe to exist as pure energy or consciousness.
*   **The Celestial Song:** They hold that the universe is filled with the faint, residual energy of the Antecedents' ascension—a cosmic vibration they call the "Celestial Song." They believe that by "listening" properly, one can achieve a state of enlightenment and commune with the ascended.
*   **Artifacts as Prayer:** The Children see Ancient artifacts not as tools, but as devotional objects. They believe these devices are "Resonators," designed to amplify and broadcast the Celestial Song. They don't understand the original function of these artifacts; a device that was once a power source might be interpreted as a "Hymnal," while a data-slate could be a "Book of Psalms." Their rituals involve activating these artifacts and meditating on the energies or data they produce, which they interpret as divine scripture.

---

### Goals and Motivations

*   **Primary Goal: The Great Awakening.** Their ultimate objective is to find and gather enough "Resonators" to create a "Grand Choir." They believe that if they can activate enough artifacts in a specific cosmic alignment, they can amplify the Celestial Song to a point where it reaches the ascended Antecedents, calling them back to the physical universe and ushering in a golden age of peace and enlightenment.
*   **Secondary Goal: Liberation of Artifacts.** They see any Antecedent artifact held in a museum, private collection, or corporate lab as being "imprisoned" and "silenced." They are driven to "liberate" these artifacts so they can be used in their rituals. This can make them appear as anything from simple thieves to dangerous terrorists, depending on their methods.
*   **Tertiary Goal: Conversion.** They seek to convert others to their faith, not through force, but by demonstrating the "truth" of the Celestial Song. They often establish quiet monasteries or communes on worlds with known Ancient sites, like Regina, inviting others to join their meditations.

---

### Impact and Adventure Hooks

*   **As Rivals:** The players might be hired by a corporation or a university to retrieve an Ancient artifact for scientific study or profit. They would find themselves in direct competition with a cell of the Children of the Antecedent who want to "liberate" the same object for their own religious purposes. The Children would be less interested in a firefight and more inclined to use stealth, misdirection, and sabotage to get what they want.
*   **As Patrons:** A wealthy, high-ranking member of the Children could hire the players for a "liberation" mission. They might offer a massive sum of money for the players to acquire an artifact from a rival collector or a secure museum. This would put the players in a morally grey area, as they would essentially be art thieves in the service of a religious cult.
*   **As a Source of Mystery:** The players might discover an artifact that has a clear, scientific function (e.g., it's a weapon, a power source, or a star chart). The Children of the Antecedent might be completely unaware of its true purpose, and their attempts to use it as a "Resonator" could have catastrophic consequences (e.g., activating the weapon, overloading the power grid, or revealing a dangerous secret). The players would then have to decide whether to stop them, educate them, or exploit their ignorance.
