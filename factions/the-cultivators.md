# The Cultivators

## Overview
The Cultivators are one of two warring factions of a hyper-advanced, physically secluded alien species. They believe the most efficient path to their ultimate goal—transforming the universe into a vast computational substrate—is to act as unseen gardeners, nurturing the natural chaos and creativity of existing civilizations.

They view the Imperium and other races not as equals or even as sentient beings in a way they respect, but as magnificent, self-organizing "algorithms" that generate the novel complexity they require. To them, a thriving, interconnected, and ever-more-complex galaxy is the most fertile ground for their work.

## Methodology
The Cultivators never act directly and their physical forms are never seen. Their influence is subtle, patient, and spread across millennia.

*   **Remote & Hidden:** All operations are conducted from their secluded habitats, using technology that is indistinguishable from magic.
*   **Agents & Proxies:** They work through countless layers of agents and cutouts. A Cultivator agent is often a respected and loyal Imperial citizen who has no idea who they truly work for. They might believe they are being guided by a secret society, a corporate benefactor, or even their own brilliant intuition.
*   **Subtle Influence:** Their actions are designed to foster growth and complexity. They might anonymously leak a medical breakthrough to a university, subtly alter the sensor readings of a scout ship to guide it towards a habitable world, or provide a flash of inspiration to an artist or engineer. They prevent galaxy-ending catastrophes but see smaller-scale conflicts and rivalries as useful engines of innovation.

## Adventure Hooks
*   **The "Beneficial" Mission:** The players are hired by a mysterious benefactor for a seemingly noble cause: delivering a vaccine to a frontier world (which secretly contains a gene-mod to enhance creativity), rescuing a brilliant scientist from pirates, or charting a new, lucrative trade route. They are unknowingly acting as agents for the Cultivators.
*   **Containing a Mistake:** A Cultivator "gift" of technology to a minor race proves too advanced, causing social collapse. The players are hired by a third party to clean up the mess, bringing them into conflict with the disastrous results of the Cultivators' hubris.
*   **Thwarting the Engineers:** The players are hired to stop a terrorist plot or prevent a corporate collapse, only to discover they are on the front line of a shadow war, acting as the Cultivators' unwitting pawns against a plot by The Engineers.
