# Section 86 (Imperial Intelligence Service)

## Overview
Section 86 is the common, clandestine name for the Imperial Intelligence Service (IIS). Officially, the organization **does not exist**. Its budget is buried within the accounts of other ministries, its agents carry no official identification, and the Imperial government will deny any knowledge of its operations if they are ever exposed.

## Chain of Command
Section 86 answers directly and only to the Emperor of the Third Imperium. It operates completely outside the standard military and political chains of command, giving it unparalleled autonomy and secrecy.

## Mission
Section 86 is tasked with protecting the Imperium from the most severe and sensitive threats, both internal and external. Its mandate is absolute, allowing it to use any means necessary to achieve its objectives.

Core responsibilities include:
*   **High-Level Espionage:** Countering and conducting espionage against rival star nations, powerful megacorporations, and internal political threats.
*   **Black Operations:** Carrying out covert operations that require complete deniability, such as assassinations, sabotage, and political destabilization.
*   **Threat Neutralization:** Infiltrating and dismantling revolutionary movements, powerful criminal syndicates, and secessionist plots before they can threaten Imperial stability.
*   **Forbidden Knowledge:** Investigating and containing existential threats, such as forbidden technologies (e.g., illegal AI, psionic weapons) and dangerous artifacts of the Ancients.
*   **Alien Affairs:** Monitoring and manipulating non-human governments and societies that pose a potential long-term threat to the Imperium.

## Relationship with Other Agencies
While the Imperial Navy has its own Naval Intelligence and the Scout Service gathers vast amounts of information, these are considered conventional intelligence bodies. Section 86 is the "deep state," the true black-ops organization. When a situation requires a level of secrecy and ruthlessness that is politically untenable for the public-facing military or government, Section 86 is activated. They are the scalpel (or sledgehammer) used in the dark to ensure the Imperium's survival.

### Plot Hook: The Makhidkarun Investigation
Section 86 has long held deep suspicions about the megacorporation **[Makhidkarun](./makhidkarun.md)**. They believe the corporation's insular nature and surprisingly advanced R&D may be a result of clandestine ties to the Zhodani Consulate. Section 86 is actively running a long-term, highly classified investigation to prove this connection. This could involve hiring outside assets (like the players) for deniable operations, such as infiltrating a Makhidkarun facility or tracking a suspected corporate spy.
