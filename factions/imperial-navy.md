# The Imperial Navy (0th Fleet)

## Overview
The Imperial Navy is the sword and shield of the Third Imperium in the Spinward Marches. The primary force assigned to this sector is the **0th Fleet**, also known as the Spinward Fleet. This unique, historical designation is a point of pride, dating back to the fleet's origins as an unnumbered expeditionary force that was officially chartered after pacifying the sector.

**Setting Year: 1105.** The Spinward Marches is a powder keg. The "cold war" with the Zhodani Consulate is rapidly heating up, and the sector is just two years away from the outbreak of the Fifth Frontier War. The 0th Fleet is on a razor's edge, and tensions are at an all-time high.

## Command Structure
*   **Fleet HQ:** The 0th Fleet is headquartered at the naval base in the [Regina](./systems/regina.md) system, the sector capital.
*   **Fleet Commander:** **Sector Admiral Arlian G. Vatz**. The canonical commander of the Spinward Marches Sector in 1105. Vatz is a seasoned, politically savvy officer who has spent years managing the delicate balance of power on the Zhodani front. He is under immense pressure from both the Imperial core and local nobles as the political situation deteriorates.
*   **Subsector Command:** The fleet is broken down into smaller squadrons assigned to each subsector. The **Regina Subsector Command** and **Lanth Subsector Command** are responsible for the systems in this adventure. These squadrons are typically led by a Rear Admiral and are composed of cruisers, destroyers, and patrol ships like the *Vigilant*-class corvette.

## Fleet Composition and Disposition
The full strength of the 0th Fleet numbers in the thousands of vessels. While a precise order of battle is a closely guarded secret, its primary combat strength is drawn from:
*   **Battleships & Carriers:** The core of the fleet's power, concentrated at Regina and key border systems.
*   **Cruisers & Destroyers:** The workhorses of the fleet, forming the bulk of the patrol squadrons that move along the Spinward Main.
*   **Corvettes & Patrol Ships:** Smaller vessels like the [INV *Steadfast*](./ships/inv-steadfast.md), tasked with customs enforcement, anti-piracy sweeps, and quarantine duties.

In 1105, the fleet is in a state of high alert. Most front-line units are deployed along the Zhodani border, conducting readiness exercises and aggressive patrols.

## Primary Missions
1.  **Deter Zhodani Aggression:** The fleet's overriding priority is to prepare for the imminent war with the Zhodani Consulate. This involves constant patrols, intelligence gathering, and maintaining a high operational tempo.
2.  **Secure the Spinward Main:** With war looming, protecting the vital Spinward Main trade route is more critical than ever. The Navy is cracking down hard on any hint of piracy or disruption to commerce.
3.  **Enforce Imperial Law:** In this high-tension environment, there is zero tolerance for unauthorized activity. Customs inspections are more thorough, and quarantine enforcement on Red Zones like [Djinni](./systems/djinni.md) is brutally efficient. Any unauthorized jump dimming is treated as a potential Zhodani incursion until proven otherwise.

## Adventure Hooks
*   **The Law:** The most direct way for players to interact with the Navy is by breaking the law. In the current climate, a patrol ship like the *Steadfast* is more likely to shoot first and ask questions later.
*   **As Patrons:** Naval Intelligence is desperate for deniable assets. They might hire the players to conduct reconnaissance in the Zhodani Consulate, sabotage a Zhodani listening post, or investigate suspected Zhodani collaborators, like [Makhidkarun](./makhidkarun.md).
*   **Caught in the Middle:** The players could be on a world when a fleet-wide alert is declared, locking the system down and turning it into an armed camp overnight.
