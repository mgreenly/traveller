# Imperial Navy Forces in Lanth Subsector

## The 18th Fleet

The **18th Fleet** is the Imperial Navy fleet deployed to the Lanth subsector of the Spinward Marches. This numbered fleet serves as a critical defensive force in one of the Imperium's most strategically important frontier regions.

## Naval Bases

According to Behind the Claw (page 69), the main naval bases for the 18th Fleet and elements of the Sector Fleet deployed to Lanth are located in the Abyss Rift or along its edge at:

- **Ivendo** - Naval base
- **D'Ganzio** - Naval base  
- **Lanth** - Naval base and subsector capital
- **Ghandi** - Naval base

## Strategic Importance

The Lanth subsector occupies a critical position in Imperial defense strategy:

- Located near both the **Sword Worlds Confederation** (rimward) and the **Zhodani Consulate** (coreward)
- Controls portions of the Spinward Main trade route
- The subsector branches at Tureded, with traffic splitting between routes to Rhylanor and Regina/Jewell/Vilis

## Fleet History

The 18th Fleet was originally stationed in Dunmag/Deneb before being sent to the Spinward Marches as reinforcements during the Fifth Frontier War. After the post-war fleet reorganization, it remained permanently stationed in Lanth subsector.

During the Fifth Frontier War (1107-1110):
- Lanth was subject to a surprise attack by the Sword Worlds beginning on 190-1107
- The system saw fierce fighting between Imperial forces and the Sword Worlds Gram Fleet
- Imperial victory was achieved on 094-1109, though planetary fighting continued until 241-1109

## Current Deployment

The 18th Fleet maintains:
- Seven Naval bases throughout the subsector
- Five Scout bases for reconnaissance and communication
- Regular patrols of the sparse "Abyss Rift" region in the spinward and rimward portions of the subsector

The fleet's positioning allows it to respond to threats from multiple directions while maintaining the security of this vital frontier region.
