# Hortalez et Cie (or-tah-LEZ ay SEE)

## Overview
Hortalez et Cie ("Hortalez and Company") is one of the thirteen Imperial megacorporations, a massive financial and trade conglomerate with operations spanning the entire Imperium and beyond. Originally founded in the Solomani Confederation over 800 years ago, the company relocated its headquarters to the Imperial core following the Solomani Rim War.

## Primary Operations
- **Banking & Finance:** One of the Imperium's largest commercial lenders
- **Interstellar Trade:** Owns vast merchant fleets and trade routes
- **Manufacturing:** Diverse industrial holdings from starship components to consumer goods
- **Insurance:** Provides coverage for ships, cargo, and commercial ventures
- **Real Estate:** Major holder of starport facilities and planetary development rights

## Presence in the Spinward Marches
In the Marches, Hortalez et Cie operates primarily through regional subsidiaries:
- **Spinward Financial Services:** Ship mortgages and commercial lending
- **Marches Trade Consortium:** Import/export operations
- **Frontier Development Bank:** Colonization and infrastructure loans

## Corporate Culture
- Known for aggressive debt collection and strict contract enforcement
- Maintains a reputation for honoring deals but exploiting every legal advantage
- Employs extensive legal teams and corporate security forces
- Often works with local governments through financial leverage

## Relevance to Campaign
- Holds the MCr 42 mortgage on the *Stardust Drifter*
- Has 90-day foreclosure deadline on the ship
- May be interested in Dr. Aros's research data as payment
- Subsidiary Spinward Financial Services is the primary creditor players must deal with
- Has the legal authority to impound the ship if payments aren't made

## Key Personnel
- **Ms. Veronica Hale:** Senior Collections Manager, Spinward Financial Services (K'Kirka branch)
- **Regional Director:** Unknown (potential future contact)
- **Corporate Security:** Maintains teams for asset recovery

## Resources
- Virtually unlimited financial resources
- Extensive legal departments
- Corporate intelligence network
- Private security contractors
- Political influence through debt leverage

## Potential Relationships
- **Neutral:** Standard business relationship if payments are made
- **Adversarial:** Aggressive foreclosure and asset seizure if in default
- **Allied:** Possible patron if players prove valuable for special operations
- **Complicated:** May trade debt relief for dangerous missions or information

## Adventure Hooks
- Offer high-risk courier jobs to earn money toward mortgage
- Corporate espionage missions against rivals
- Asset recovery from other defaulted debtors
- Transportation of sensitive corporate personnel
- Investigation of insurance fraud cases

## Notes
- More interested in profit than ideology
- Will negotiate if it serves their financial interests
- Has connections to both Imperial nobility and criminal organizations
- Maintains strict neutrality in political conflicts
- Known to fund both sides of trade disputes
