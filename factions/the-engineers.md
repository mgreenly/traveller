# The Engineers

## Overview
The Engineers are one of two warring factions of a hyper-advanced, physically secluded alien species. They are the direct opposition to The Cultivators. They believe the universe is a flawed machine that must be demolished and rebuilt according to a logical, top-down design.

They view biological civilizations like the Imperium as "spaghetti code"—messy, inefficient, and prone to catastrophic errors. The chaos and creativity that The Cultivators see as a strength, The Engineers see as a bug to be patched or deleted. Their goal is the same—a universal computer—but they intend to build it themselves on the cleared rubble of the current galactic landscape.

## Methodology
Like their rivals, The Engineers never act directly and remain perfectly hidden. Their methods, however, are far more direct and destructive.

*   **Remote & Hidden:** All operations are conducted from their secluded habitats, using technology that is indistinguishable from magic.
*   **Agents & Proxies:** They also work through agents, but they recruit from the disenfranchised, the greedy, and the destructive. Their agents are often terrorists, corporate raiders, corrupt officials, and pirate fleets who believe they are working for a simple anarchist cause or a powerful crime syndicate.
*   **Destructive Influence:** Their actions are designed to dismantle, disrupt, and destroy. They secretly sponsor wars (the brewing conflict between the Imperium and the Zhodani is a prime opportunity for them), trigger economic collapses to weaken stable regions, and introduce sophisticated computer viruses to cripple information networks. They are the unseen architects of many of the galaxy's greatest calamities.

## Adventure Hooks
*   **The Tip of the Spear:** The players are hired to perform a mission that seems like standard corporate sabotage or a simple data theft, but the consequences are far more devastating than they were led to believe (e.g., the virus they upload causes a starport's reactor to go critical). They were the deniable tools of an Engineer plot.
*   **Direct Opposition:** The players are hired to stop an Engineer plot in motion: defusing a bomb, protecting a key political figure from assassination, or defending a colony from a suspiciously well-equipped pirate fleet.
*   **A Common Enemy:** The players discover that a mutual enemy—perhaps the Zirunkariish pirates or a specific political terrorist—is being secretly funded and directed by The Engineers, forcing them into an uneasy alliance with other factions to combat a threat no one understands.
