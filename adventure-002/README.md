## Key Themes

### Financial Pressure
The crew must generate income to service their debt to Silas Kane while maintaining and operating their newly acquired research vessel.

### Corporate Warfare
Makhidkarun's interest in recovering their "lost" research creates ongoing tension and potential conflicts.

### Scientific Discovery
Dr. Aros's work with Photovoric Capacitors may have implications beyond simple energy storage, attracting attention from various parties.

### Moral Choices
The crew must decide how to use their leverage over Kane and whether to exploit or protect the alien research.

## Current Status

**Location:** K'Kirka system

**Financial Status:** Debt to Silas Kane (terms varying by negotiation outcome)

## Notes for Referees

This adventure picks up immediately after Adventure 001's conclusion. The crew's choices in the previous adventure will significantly impact the opening scenarios and available options.

Key variables to track:
- Loan terms negotiated with Silas Kane
- Relationship status with various factions
- Condition of the *Stardust Drifter* and its systems
- Knowledge gained about Photovoric Capacitors and their applications
