# Scene 4: The Mind Market - Psionic Black Market

**[See specialist-discovery-guide.md for standardized discovery mechanics and pricing]**

## Introduction Section

### Finding the Mind Market
**Primary Discovery Method:** Altaira file shows "Zhodani psi-lock" requiring psionic assistance

**WARNING:** This is the hardest specialist to locate - psionics are highly illegal in Imperial space.

Players might learn about it through:
- **Streetwise (TN 12) asking very carefully:** "Three-tap signal in the Lower Wards. But I never said that."
- **Investigate (TN 11) + Stealth (TN 9):** Follow Zhodani graffiti symbols forming trail to warehouse district
- **Deception (TN 10) posing as sympathizer:** Refugee whispers: "Third warehouse, green door. Say 'the mind seeks its own level.'"
- **Carouse (TN 13) buying many drinks:** Drunk refugee mentions "warehouse 47-C helps our kind"

### What Others CAREFULLY Say
- **Black market dealer:** "I don't deal in that. No one does. But hypothetically, if someone did, they'd probably operate out of the Lower Wards."
- **Zhodani refugee (whispered):** "There are those who help people like me. They call it a market but it's more like... a sanctuary. Be very careful."
- **Corrupt cop:** "Every few months we're told to raid warehouse 47-C. Every time it's empty. Draw your own conclusions."
- **Previous customer (if found):** "They saved my daughter. She was dying, needed a psionic healer. Cost everything I had but... just be respectful. They're dangerous."

### Warning Signs
- Multiple surveillance cameras seem to malfunction near the warehouse
- Local criminals avoid that entire block
- People who ask too many questions about psionics sometimes disappear

**Location:** K'Kirka Lower Wards, Warehouse District, Building 47-C (Third warehouse from corner, green door)

> **Read Aloud:** Following cryptic directions through K'Kirka's industrial underbelly, you arrive at what appears to be an abandoned warehouse. But the homeless person at the corner taps their temple three times as you pass - the signal you were told to watch for. Inside, the warehouse has been converted into a maze of sensor-dampening curtains and white noise generators. The air tastes of copper and ozone. You're guided to a central chamber where a figure in a hooded environment suit waits, their face hidden behind a reflective visor. "Welcome to the Mind Market," they say, their voice artificially distorted. "You seek forbidden knowledge. Everything has a price here, especially secrets that could burn worlds."

## The Mind Market Operator

The operator goes by "Synaptic" - nobody knows their real identity.

### Gaining Entry (Before meeting Synaptic)

**How players might find this place:**

**Streetwise (TN 12):** Extremely careful inquiries in the underworld
- Critical Success (Effect 6+): Get introduction from trusted source (+2 to negotiations)
- Success: Get directions but marked as "unknowns" (-1 to negotiations)
- Failure: Attract unwanted attention from law enforcement

**Investigate (TN 11) + Stealth (TN 9):** Follow psionic institute graffiti tags
- Must succeed both checks
- Failure on Investigate: Dead ends and false leads
- Failure on Stealth: Imperial Security notices investigation

**Deception (TN 10) + Acting:** Pose as Zhodani sympathizers at refugee safehouse
- Risky: Failure means tested by actual psionic (must have good cover story)
- Success: Given emergency contact protocols

### Initial Security Check

Upon arrival, players are scanned by hidden psionics:

**Roll 2D6 for each character:**
- 2-4: Detected as potential threat (asked to leave one person outside as "insurance")
- 5-8: Cleared but watched carefully
- 9-11: Deemed trustworthy customers
- 12: Recognized as having latent psionic potential (Synaptic very interested)

### Negotiating with Synaptic

#### Understanding What You Need

**Science (TN 11) or Investigate (TN 10):** Explain the Zhodani psionic locks
- Success: Synaptic nods. "Altaira Protocols. Yes, I know of these. Dr. Aros bought equipment here."
- Failure: Must show data directly (risky - Synaptic now knows what you have)

#### The Offer

Synaptic provides three options:

**Option 1: Black Market Psi-Interface**
- Cost: Cr200,000
- Availability: In stock
- Risk: 15% chance of Imperial Intelligence tracking
- Includes basic operation manual

**Option 2: Psionic Specialist Service**
- Cost: Cr50,000
- A telepath will unlock the files directly
- Risk: Telepath will know contents (cannot prevent)
- Work must be done on-site

**Option 3: The Alternative Method**
- Cost: Information trade
- Synaptic reveals: "One of your crew has latent abilities. With training and the right equipment, they could unlock it themselves."
- Requires: 1 week training, Cr25,000 in amplification drugs
- Risk: Awakening psionics is illegal and dangerous

#### Negotiation Checks

**Broker (TN 10):** Reduce prices by 20%
- Effect 4+: Reduce by 35% and get warranty

**Streetwise (TN 9):** Leverage underworld reputation
- Success: 10% discount and future contact privileges

**Intimidate (TN 13):** Threaten to expose operation
- Success: 50% discount but marked as enemy
- Failure: Immediately ejected, possibly attacked

**Trade Offer:** Information about Dr. Aros or Ancient technology
- Highly valuable: Synaptic very interested in consciousness transfer research
- Worth up to Cr100,000 credit depending on information

### If Using the Telepath (Option 2)

> **Read Aloud:** A young woman enters, her eyes unfocused, temples bearing the ritual scars of Zhodani psionic training. She sits across from your data core, hands hovering over the interface. "This will take time," she says in accented Galanglic. "And I must warn you - I cannot choose what I see. Your secrets will be mine as well."

**The Process:**
1. Takes 4 hours of intense concentration
2. Telepath (Yeva) makes Telepathy check (2D6+4)
   - 10+: Unlocks Altaira Protocols successfully
   - 12+: Also finds and unlocks related hidden files
   - 14+: Discovers Dr. Aros left a psionic message in the data
   - 8-9: Partial success, some corruption
   - 7 or less: Triggers security, data scrambled

**What Yeva Learns (and might share for a price):**
- The location of Clone Seven
- Dr. Aros's final thoughts (recorded psionically)
- The true identity of "Sparrow"
- Hidden Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) facilities doing psionic research

### Additional Services Available

**Psionic Training:** Cr10,000 per month (highly illegal)
- Basic telepathy or telekinesis
- Includes drugs to accelerate development
- 30% chance of detection by authorities per month

**Memory Extraction:** Cr75,000
- Recover deleted or suppressed memories
- Can unlock trauma-locked information
- Risk of psychological damage

**Consciousness Transfer Research:** Cr500,000
- Synaptic has other buyers interested
- Connects to the Altaira Protocols
- Involves Zhodani and stolen Imperial research

**Psionic Shielding:** Cr5,000 per person
- Temporary (1 month) protection from telepathic intrusion
- Useful if expecting to encounter psionics

### Synaptic's Hidden Agenda

**The Truth (Learn only through exceptional roleplay or investigation):**
- Synaptic is three people sharing one identity
- One is an Imperial Intelligence operative (maintaining cover)
- One is an actual Zhodani refugee (true believer)
- One is a rogue telepath (only cares about profit)
- They don't know about each other
- Which one the players meet is random

### Leaving the Mind Market

**Exit Protocols:**
- Memory fog dispersed at exit (forget exact location)
- Given encrypted commlink for future contact
- Warned that betrayal means death
- If deal made: "The mind has many doors. We've opened one for you. Be careful what walks through."

### Future Consequences

**If players use Mind Market services:**
- 25% chance per month of Imperial Intelligence investigation
- Synaptic becomes a contact for psionic-related needs
- Other psionics in sector learn of players (good or bad)
- May trigger Zhodani interest in the party

**If players refuse or betray Mind Market:**
- Marked as enemies of psionic underground
- Difficulty finding alternative help with Zhodani tech
- But gain Standing with Imperial authorities if reported

### Key NPCs

**"Synaptic" (Variable Identity)**
- Stats depend on which personality
- Always has Deception-3, Broker-2, Streetwise-3
- May have Telepathy-2 if the telepath persona

**Yeva (Zhodani Telepath)**
- STR 5, DEX 7, END 6, INT 10, EDU 9, SOC 6, PSI 10
- Skills: Telepathy-3, Deception-2, Science-1, Computers-1
- Equipment: Psi amplifier, mental shields
- Motivation: Survive, find other Zhodani refugees, understand the Ancients
