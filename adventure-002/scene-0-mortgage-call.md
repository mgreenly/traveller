# Scene 0: The Mortgage Call

**Location:** K'Kirka Starport, *Stardust Drifter* Bridge
**Timing:** First morning after claiming the ship

> **Read Aloud:** You're reviewing the ship's systems after your first night as official owners when the comm system chimes with an incoming tight-beam transmission. The display shows the corporate logo of Spinward Financial Services - a subsidiary of Hortalez et Cie (or-tah-LEZ ay SEE). The screen resolves to show a middle-aged woman in a crisp business suit, her expression professionally neutral but her eyes sharp as monofilament.
>
> "Good morning. I am Ms. Veronica Hale, Senior Collections Manager for Spinward Financial Services. Our records show you've just filed salvage claim on the *Stardust Drifter*, Imperial Registry SD-4471. Congratulations on your successful recovery. However, this vessel has an outstanding mortgage that must be addressed."

## The Financial Reality

Ms. Hale presents the facts with ruthless efficiency:

### The Mortgage Details
- **Original Ship Value:** MCr 140 (heavily modified 400-ton lab ship)
- **Monthly Payment:** Cr 583,333 (MCr 140 ÷ 240 months per Traveller rules)
- **Months Delinquent:** 12 months
- **Past Due Amount:** Cr 6,999,996 (12 × Cr 583,333)
- **Late Fees:** Cr 700,000 (10% of past due)
- **Total to Bring Current:** Cr 7,699,996 (rounded to Cr 7.7 million)

**How Mortgage Payments Are Calculated (Per Traveller Core Rules p.149):**
*"The monthly repayments on a ship mortgage are easy to calculate. Start with the total purchase price of the ship being bought, then divide this amount by 240."*

**The Math:**
- Ship purchase price: MCr 140 (140,000,000 credits)
- Mortgage term: 240 months (20 years)
- Monthly payment: 140,000,000 ÷ 240 = Cr 583,333
- 12 months past due: 12 × 583,333 = Cr 6,999,996
- Late fees (10%): 699,999.6 (rounds to Cr 700,000)
- Total owed: Cr 7,699,996

### Current Ownership Breakdown
Ms. Hale explains the ownership structure:

**Current Ownership Stakes:**
- Ash: MCr 70 (50% of ship value)
- Each other traveller: MCr 1 each (4 travelers = MCr 4 total)
- Spinward Financial Services: MCr 66 (47.1% ownership)
- Total ship value: MCr 140

**History:** "Dr. Aros had MCr 74 in equity which he transferred as a gift - MCr 70 to Ash and MCr 1 to each of the other four travelers."

**The Mortgage Situation:**
- Spinward Financial owns MCr 66 worth of the ship (47.1%)
- You must pay Cr 7.7 million to bring the mortgage current
- Failure to pay means foreclosure and loss of all equity

### The Ultimatum

> **Read Aloud:** Ms. Hale's expression doesn't change as she delivers the blow. "Under Imperial Salvage Law, Section 247, Subsection 3, you have 90 days from your claim filing to bring the mortgage current. That's Cr 7,699,996 - let's call it 7.7 million credits - due by..." she checks her screen, "...day 287 of this year. That gives you precisely 89 days from tomorrow."
>
> "If you cannot bring the loan current, we will exercise our right to foreclose. The ship will be impounded and sold at auction. Your salvage claim will be nullified, though you may receive a small percentage of any auction proceeds exceeding our lien."
>
> "Alternatively, you may attempt to negotiate a loan modification, though given Dr. Aros's payment history, our terms would be... stringent."

### Negotiation Options

Players can attempt to negotiate:

#### Request Payment Plan
**Persuade or Broker (TN 10):**
- Success: Can pay Cr 500,000 now and Cr 500,000 monthly for 6 months
- Exceptional (Effect 4+): Cr 250,000 now and Cr 400,000 monthly for 8 months
- Failure: Full amount due in 90 days, no extensions

#### Offer Partial Payment
**If they offer at least Cr 2,000,000 immediately:**
- Extends deadline by 30 days
- Reduces late fees by 50%
- Monthly payments resume at Cr 583,333

#### Trade Information
**If they mention valuable data or research:**
- Ms. Hale becomes interested but cautious
- "Our parent company, Hortalez et Cie (or-tah-LEZ ay SEE), has... diverse interests. Perhaps we could arrange a meeting."
- Potential to trade some datacore contents for debt relief

### The Silas Kane Complication

Ms. Hale adds one final note:

> "I should also inform you that we've received notice of a competing lien from a Mr. Silas Kane of Regina. He claims you owe him MCr 12.5 which was used to fund this salvage operation. While his claim is junior to ours, it... complicates matters. The courts will need to sort out priority if this goes to foreclosure."

**The Real Numbers:**
- Kane's original loan: MCr 12.5 at 40% monthly 
- After 1 month: MCr 17.5
- After 2 months (60 day deadline): MCr 24.5 DUE IN FULL
- If not paid, enforcement actions begin

### Call Conclusion

> **Read Aloud:** "You have 89 days. I suggest you use them wisely. We'll be monitoring the ship's transponder. Any attempt to leave the system without satisfying the lien will result in an Imperial warrant and automatic forfeiture of all salvage rights. Good day."
>
> The screen goes dark. Your celebration of ownership just became a desperate race against foreclosure.
