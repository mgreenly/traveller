# Specialist Discovery Guide

## Standard Discovery Process

Each specialist requires a three-phase approach:
1. **Learning They Exist** - Hearing about them through various sources
2. **Finding Their Location** - Tracking down where they work
3. **Gaining Their Trust** - Convincing them to help

## Detailed Discovery Paths

### Binary Tessa - The Cryptographer

#### Phase 1: Learning About Her
**Automatic Hints:**
- Ship service bill includes flyer: "Data Resurrection Services - Military Grade Encryption"
- Examining datacore shows "Q7 encryption" label

**Active Search (pick one):**
- **Computers (TN 8):** Search crypto forums → Find "BinaryT" with stellar reviews
- **Streetwise (TN 9):** Ask about encryption experts → "Binary Tessa in tech district"
- **Investigate (TN 10):** Ask at starport shops → Get specific address
- **Admin (TN 11):** Check business registries → Find "Data Resurrection Services"

#### Phase 2: Finding Her Location
- **Automatic:** If have flyer → Address listed: Tech District, Level -3, Shop 47B
- **Navigate (TN 7):** Find tech district → Level -3 is clearly marked
- **Streetwise (TN 8):** Ask locals → "Between the cyber clinic and robot shop"

#### Phase 3: Gaining Trust
**Initial Reaction:** 2D6 + highest SOC modifier
- 2-5: Hostile ("Shop's closed permanently")
- 6-8: Suspicious ("What do you really want?")
- 9-11: Professional ("State your business")
- 12+: Intrigued ("Is that military encryption?")

**Trust Building (need one):**
- Show encrypted data (automatic success)
- **Persuade (TN 9):** Explain honestly
- **Computers (TN 7):** Demonstrate knowledge
- **Broker (TN 10):** Offer good payment

### Professor Vault - The Xenoarchaeologist

#### Phase 1: Learning About Him
**Automatic Hints:**
- Omicron Particle preview shows "Active Ancient Code"
- University public directory lists all researchers

**Active Search (pick one):**
- **Education (TN 11):** Recall academic knowledge → "Vault was the Ancient expert"
- **Investigate (TN 9):** Search university → Find him in storage area
- **Library Data (TN 8):** Research Ancient experts → Vault dominates the field
- **Science (TN 10):** Ask academics → "The crackpot in the basement"

#### Phase 2: Finding His Location
- **Automatic:** University directory → "Visiting Researcher, Sub-basement B-7"
- **Admin (TN 7):** Ask reception → Given directions reluctantly
- **Persuade (TN 9):** Convince staff → "Down the service elevator, last door"

#### Phase 3: Gaining Trust
**Initial Reaction:** 2D6 + INT modifier (he respects intelligence)
- 2-5: Paranoid ("The review board sent you!")
- 6-8: Dismissive ("Another treasure hunter")
- 9-11: Curious ("What have you found?")
- 12+: Excited ("Is that active Ancient code?!")

**Trust Building (need one):**
- Mention Omicron Particle (automatic success)
- **Science (TN 10):** Discuss Ancient theories
- **Education (TN 9):** Show academic knowledge
- **Persuade (TN 9):** Appeal to his vindication desire

### Mind Market - The Psionic Underground

#### Phase 1: Learning About Them
**Automatic Hints:**
- Altaira file shows "Zhodani psi-lock"
- Refugees whisper about "help for our kind"

**Active Search (pick highest skill):**
- **Streetwise (TN 12):** Very carefully ask → "Three-tap signal, Lower Wards"
- **Investigate (TN 11):** Follow graffiti → Zhodani symbols form trail
- **Deception (TN 10):** Pose as sympathizer → "Green door, third warehouse"
- **Carouse (TN 13):** Buy many drinks → Drunk refugee mentions location

#### Phase 2: Finding Their Location
- **Investigate + Stealth (TN 9 both):** Follow graffiti without being seen
- **Streetwise (TN 11):** Find three-tap signal location
- **Navigate (TN 10):** Search warehouse district → Eventually find green door
- **Bribery:** Cr5,000 to corrupt cop for exact address

#### Phase 3: Gaining Trust
**Initial Scan:** Automatically scanned by hidden psionics
- Roll 2D6 + lowest characteristic modifier
- 2-5: Rejected ("Leave. Now.")
- 6-8: Suspicious extensive questioning
- 9-11: Cautious acceptance
- 12: Recognized as having potential

**Trust Building (need one):**
- Mention Altaira Protocols (automatic but risky)
- **Persuade (TN 11):** Convince of genuine need
- **Deception (TN 13):** Perfect cover story
- Pay Cr10,000 "earnest money"

#### Phase 1: Learning About Her
**Automatic Hints:**
- Prometheus file shows "molecular manipulation required"
- Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) files trigger "She strips their ships"

**Active Search (pick one):**
- **Computers (TN 10):** Search tech forums → "VoidWrench" hardware posts
- **Mechanic (TN 8):** Ask about molecular tools → "Bay 47 at the yards"
- **Streetwise (TN 9):** Ask about ex-Makhidkarun → "Kass Chen, stays hidden"
- **Investigate (TN 11):** Research Makhidkarun defectors → Find her story

#### Phase 2: Finding Her Location
- **Navigate (TN 8):** Find Reclamation Yards → Bay 47 is marked "Hazardous"
- **Streetwise (TN 9):** Ask yard workers → "Knock three times, wait, knock twice"
- **Mechanic (TN 7):** Follow the Makhidkarun ship parts → Lead to Bay 47

#### Phase 3: Gaining Trust
**Door Protocol:** Must knock correctly or no answer
- Wrong pattern: No response ever
- Correct pattern: Opens cautiously

**Initial Reaction:** 2D6 - 2 (very paranoid)
- 2-3: Hostile ("Corporate spies!")
- 4-7: Suspicious interrogation
- 8-10: Wary but listening
- 11-12: Recognizes kindred spirits

**Trust Building (need one):**
- Show Makhidkarun data (automatic success but she copies it)
- **Mechanic (TN 8):** Appreciate her work
- **Persuade (TN 10):** Share your Makhidkarun troubles
- Have Binary Tessa vouch for you

## Standard Pricing Structure

### Base Services
Each specialist has three tiers of service:

#### Tier 1: Basic Help
- **Cost:** Cr10,000-15,000 OR information trade
- **Time:** 1-2 days
- **Success:** 85% chance
- They solve one specific file

#### Tier 2: Full Service  
- **Cost:** Cr20,000-30,000 OR major favor
- **Time:** 3-5 days
- **Success:** 95% chance
- They solve their specialty files completely

#### Tier 3: Partnership
- **Cost:** Revenue sharing OR bringing them on mission
- **Time:** Ongoing
- **Success:** Automatic for their specialties
- They become crew allies

### Negotiation Modifiers

**Broker Skill Check (TN 10):**
- Critical Failure: Price doubles
- Failure: Full price + complications
- Success: 20% discount
- Effect 4+: 40% discount
- Effect 6+: Will work for favors/information

**Modifiers to Broker:**
- Showed genuine need: +1
- Time pressure mentioned: -2
- Offered information trade: +2
- Previously helped them: +3
- Threatened them: -5
- Corporate connections: -3

## Complications If Discovery Fails

### Taking Too Long (7+ days searching)
Roll 2D6:
- 2-3: Specialist leaves K'Kirka
- 4-5: Price doubles due to demand
- 6-8: Another group gets there first
- 9-10: Specialist suspicious of stalking
- 11-12: Actually easier to find than expected

### Asking Wrong People
Roll 2D6:
- 2-3: Reported to authorities
- 4-5: Given false location (ambush)
- 6-8: Word spreads you're looking
- 9-10: Specialist alerted, goes into hiding
- 11-12: Actually helpful, gives correct info

### Failed Trust Building
- Binary Tessa: Calls security, banned from shop
- Professor Vault: Reports to university security
- Mind Market: Memory wiped of location
- VoidWrench: Disappears for 2 weeks
- University: Blacklisted from resources

## Quick GM Reference

**When players are stuck:**
1. Have an NPC mention specialists during other scenes
2. Drop business cards/flyers in routine activities  
3. Let failed Computer checks show forum posts
4. Ship service tech offers to make introductions (for a fee)
5. Another specialist mentions them

**Making it easier:**
- Lower TNs by 2 for desperate players
- Add more automatic hints
- Have specialists reach out to them
- Let any reasonable approach work

**Making it harder:**
- Specialists are paranoid after corporate raids
- Require multiple successful checks
- Add rival groups seeking same help
- Time pressure before specialist leaves

## Alternative: The Fixer

- **Location:** Starport bar, booth 7
- **Cost:** Cr2,000 per introduction
- **Knows:** All specialist locations and protocols
- **Catch:** Takes 1d3 days to arrange meetings
