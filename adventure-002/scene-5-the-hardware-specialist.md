## Meeting VoidWrench

**Real Name:** Kassandra "Kass" Chen (only revealed if trust gained)

### Initial Approach

**How players know to come here:**
- Found username on underground tech forums
- Ex-Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) employee registry (requires research)
- Referral from Binary Tessa (if she trusts them)

### First Impressions

**Mechanic (TN 8):** Recognize the quality of their work
- Success: "Finally, someone who appreciates precision." (+2 to negotiations)
- Failure: "Tourists. What do you really want?"

**Streetwise (TN 9):** Use underworld protocols correctly
- Success: "You know the rules. Good." (+1 to negotiations)
- Failure: Suspicion increases (-1 to negotiations)

**Deception (TN 11):** Pretend to be corporate buyers
- Success: Gets information but will be angry when truth revealed
- Failure: "Corporate spies! Get out!" (May attack)

### Trust Building Phase

VoidWrench needs to know they're not corporate agents:

#### Proving Legitimacy

**Required: One of the following:**
- Show them data proving Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) crimes (Kane Contingency)
- Reveal you're also on the run from Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run)
- Have Binary Tessa vouch for you (if good relationship)
- Demonstrate detailed knowledge of Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) hardware

**Persuade (TN 9):** Explain your situation honestly
- Success: "Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) screwed us both. Maybe we can help each other."
- Effect 4+: "Dr. Aros? I knew him. Brilliant but reckless."

- Cost: Cr20,000 or Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) intel

**Service 5: Remove Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) Signatures**

Instead of credits, VoidWrench will trade for:
- Location of active Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) facilities

- Promise to sabotage Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) operation
- The corpse of a Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) executive (serious)

### The Work Session

**Note:** The description below assumes VoidWrench is working on the Prometheus partitions. Adjust for other services.

#### Hour 1-2: Initial Analysis

> **Read Aloud:** Kass works with molecular manipulators so precise they could split atoms. "Your Dr. Aros modified this himself. See these micro-fractures? Amateur work but clever. He was hiding something from his employers. These Prometheus files... scattered across 17 hidden partitions. Someone really didn't want this found. Wait... there's a deadman switch. If I don't do this right, it'll fry everything."

**Kass's Mechanic Check (2D6+4):**
- 10+: Safely disarms deadman switch, accesses all partitions
- 12+: Finds bonus hidden partition with subject names
- 8-9: Partial success, minor data loss (missing 3 partitions)
- 7 or less: Triggers security, must start over

#### Hour 3-4: The Discovery

> **Read Aloud:** "Got it!" The molecular locks disengage with an almost inaudible click. Inside the hidden partition, additional data streams begin flowing. Kass whistles. "This isn't just research data. This is evidence. Aros was documenting everything - illegal experiments, corporate assassination orders, bribery records. He knew they'd eventually kill him."

**Hidden Partition Contents:**
1. **Project Prometheus** - Full uncorrupted files
2. **Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) Operations:**
   - List of 17 illegal research facilities
   - Names of corrupted Imperial officials
   - Corporate assassination list (players might be on it)
3. **The Breeding Program:**
   - Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) has been genetically modifying colonists
   - Creating humans adapted for specific worlds
   - Some subjects developed unexpected abilities
4. **Dr. Aros's Insurance:**
   - Video confession about all projects
   - Location of physical evidence caches
   - Dead-drop protocols for data release
