## Introduction Section

### Finding Professor Vault
**Primary Discovery Method:** Omicron Particle preview shows "Active Ancient Code"

Players might learn about Vault through:
- **Education (TN 11):** "Professor Vault? He was the leading Ancient technology expert until that transcendence paper buried his career."
- **Investigate (TN 9) at university:** Find him listed as "Visiting Researcher - Unpaid" in storage area B-7
- **Library Data (TN 8) on Ancient experts:** His papers dominate the field for two decades, then stop 3 years ago
- **Automatic if asking about Omicron preview:** "Active Ancient code? Only one person on K'Kirka could read that - the crackpot in basement B-7."

### What Others Say
- **University administrator:** "Professor Vault is... on sabbatical. Indefinitely. You'll find him in B-7. Please don't encourage his theories."
- **Graduate student:** "Brilliant man. Taught me everything. Then he started talking about Ancient consciousnesses still watching us. Career suicide."
- **Janitor:** "Nice fellow. Forgets to eat. Sometimes the artifacts in his office glow at night. I don't go in there anymore."
- **Fellow researcher:** "His math is flawless, his evidence compelling, but his conclusions? The Ancients achieving godhood? Academic heresy."

**Location:** K'Kirka Technical Institute, Sub-Basement Storage Area B-7 (Service elevator, last door)

> **Read Aloud:** The K'Kirka Technical Institute's gleaming towers speak of academic prestige, but you're directed to the forgotten sub-basements where "visiting researchers" work. Storage Area B-7 has been converted into a makeshift office. Ancient artifacts in various states of analysis cover every surface - crystalline fragments, etched metal plates, and devices of unknown purpose. The air hums with the barely audible resonance of Ancient technology. A man in his sixties with wild grey hair and a stained lab coat mutters to himself while manipulating holographic projections of Ancient script. Empty stimulant injectors and food containers suggest he hasn't left in days.

## Meeting Professor Vault

### Initial Reaction
Professor Vault is brilliant but bitter about his fall from grace. His reaction depends on approach:

**Academic Credentials (Education TN 8):**
- Success: "Ah, someone who appreciates real scholarship!" (Friendly)
- Failure: "Another treasure hunter. What do you want?" (Dismissive)

**Mentioning Ancient Technology:**
- Immediately interested but suspicious: "Who sent you? The review board? I've already been censured!"

**Offering Payment:**
- Conflicted: "I don't do black market authentication anymore... usually... how much?"

### Understanding Vault's Situation

**Investigate or Carouse (TN 9):** Learn why he lost tenure:
- Published paper claiming Ancients didn't disappear but "ascended to a higher dimensional state"
- Suggested some Ancient sites are still active and monitoring current civilizations
- Accused colleagues of suppressing evidence of ongoing Ancient presence
- Lost tenure after refusing to retract his theories

### Negotiation Phases

#### Phase 1: Gaining Trust

**Science (TN 10):** Discuss legitimate Ancient theories
- Success: "Finally! Someone who understands the implications!" (+3 to Phase 2)
- Failure: He lectures condescendingly for 20 minutes (-1 to Phase 2)

**Persuade (TN 9):** Appeal to his desire for vindication
- Success: "If this proves my theories..." (+2 to Phase 2)
- Failure: "Empty promises. I've heard them before."

**Deception (TN 11):** Pretend to be believers in his theories
- Success: He's eager to share everything (+4 to Phase 2)
- Failure: "You're mocking me!" (-3 to Phase 2, might refuse entirely)

#### Phase 2: The Deal

**Base Requirements:**
- Cr30,000 for basic Ancient interface work
- OR Cr10,000 plus sharing all Ancient-related findings
- OR Bring him along on any Ancient site expeditions

**Broker (TN 9 + modifiers):**
- Critical Failure: Demands Cr50,000 and publication rights
- Failure: Standard price plus he keeps copies
- Success: Standard price or alternative payment
- Effect 4+: He's so excited he'll do it for Cr5,000 and future collaboration

### Working with the Omicron Particle Data

#### Day 1: Initial Interface

> **Read Aloud:** Vault connects a crystalline Ancient interface to your data core. The moment it activates, every Ancient artifact in his lab begins resonating. His eyes widen. "This isn't just data about the Ancients. This IS Ancient data. Active protocols. Still running. My God, what did your Dr. Aros find?"

**Vault makes Science check (2D6+5):**
- 10+: Successfully interfaces with Omicron Particle files
- 13+: Unlocks additional hidden Ancient protocols
- 16+: Discovers active connection to Ancient network node

#### Day 2: The Revelation

> **Read Aloud:** Vault hasn't slept. His eyes are bloodshot but gleaming with fanatic intensity. "I was right! The Ancients didn't die - they transformed! This Omicron Particle, it's not just a key, it's a test! They're watching to see if younger races are ready for the next phase of evolution. But we failed. The contamination protocol... we triggered their reset mechanism!"

**Critical Discovery:** Vault determines:
1. The countdown has 1,827 days remaining (about 5 years)
2. Three possible planets based on partial coordinates:
   - Pixie (industrial world, population 8 million)
   - Boughene (mining colony, population 500,000)  
   - Lysen (agricultural world, population 2 million)
3. The auroras have already started on one world but are being dismissed as "unusual solar activity"

#### Day 3: Complications and Choices

**Roll 2D6 for Complications:**

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 2-3  | Vault's former colleagues report his "illegal research" to authorities |
| 4-5  | Ancient artifacts in lab begin broadcasting signals (attracts attention) |
| 6-7  | Vault becomes obsessed, refuses to return data without going to site |
| 8-9  | No complications - smooth work |
| 10-11| Discovers way to slow countdown (buys additional time) |
| 12   | Ancient node beneath K'Kirka responds, opening a hidden chamber |

### Additional Discoveries

**If successful, Vault also reveals:**

1. **About the Ancient Network:** "Seventeen nodes, all connected. If we could access the primary node on Regina, we might be able to abort the reset. But we'd need someone with the right genetic markers... or consciousness pattern."

2. **About the Consciousness Matrices:** "These formats... they're similar to Ancient preservation systems. Your Dr. Aros was trying to make human consciousness compatible with Ancient technology. If he succeeded..."

3. **About Clone Seven:** "The Altaira Protocols mention consciousness transfer into clones. If Clone Seven has Ancient-compatible consciousness patterns, she might be the key to stopping the reset."

4. **Hidden Warning:** "There's something else. The data shows multiple reset attempts were aborted in the past. Someone or something has been protecting us. But that protection is failing."

### Vault's Offer

After seeing the data, Vault makes a proposition:
> "This changes everything. Forget payment. Make me part of this. I'll provide all my resources, my connections in the xenoarchaeology community, everything. We need to warn people, but more importantly, we need to find a solution. The Ancients left us a test, and we're failing it. But maybe... maybe we still have time."

**If accepted as ally:**
- Provides ongoing Ancient technology support
- Can interpret Ancient sites and artifacts
- Has contacts at universities across the sector
- Knows location of three unregistered Ancient sites
- WARNING: His obsession might lead to dangerous decisions
