# Scene 1: Initial Datacore Analysis

**Location:** K'Kirka Starport, *Stardust Drifter* Lab

> **Read Aloud:** With the salvage claim filed, you finally have time to examine the data core. As it hums to life, a catalog appears on the main screen - a list of Dr. Aros's projects. Your excitement quickly turns to frustration as you try to access each file. Every single one is blocked - some encrypted, others corrupted, a few requiring hardware you don't have. The secrets are all here, tantalizingly close, but locked away behind various digital and physical barriers.

## The Mini-Game Structure

For each project, players must:
1. **Identify the Problem** (Understanding check)
2. **Find the Solution** (Investigation/Knowledge check)
3. **Acquire Resources** (Social/Financial check)
4. **Execute the Fix** (Technical check)
5. **Deal with Complications** (Variable)

Success at each stage provides bonuses to the next. Failure creates setbacks or alerts enemies.

### 1. Kane Contingency - Financial Insurance
**Blocker:** Military-grade quantum encryption with killswitch
**Visible Preview:** "Insurance against S.K. - Complete financial records - Dead man switch armed"

#### Stage 1: Identify Problem
**Computers or Electronics (TN 8):**
- Success: "It's Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run)'s Q7 encryption with a data-wipe failsafe. One wrong attempt destroys everything."
- Failure: Lose one attempt before triggering killswitch (3 total)

#### Stage 2: Find Solution
**Investigate or Streetwise (TN 10):**
- Success: Need either quantum decryption key OR someone who knows Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) protocols
- Options discovered:
  - Binary Tessa (can crack it safely)
  - VoidWrench (knows Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) protocols)
  - Black market quantum key (Cr 50,000)

#### Stage 3: Execute Fix
**If using specialist:** Automatic success but they learn contents
**If using black market key:** Computers (TN 12)
**If brute forcing:** Computers (TN 15), failure = data destroyed

**Contents when unlocked:** Full Kane evidence, Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) money laundering, assassination orders

## Complications Table (Roll when appropriate)

Roll 2D6 when players fail a crucial check or take too long:

| 2D6 | Complication |
|-----|--------------|
| 2-3 | Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) corporate agents detect datacore activity |
| 4-5 | Killswitch triggers on one random file - 24 hours to defuse |

| 12 | Dr. Aros's consciousness in the matrices offers to help |

### Complication Read-Alouds

**2-3: Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) Detection**
> "The datacore's indicator lights suddenly flash red in a pattern you've never seen before. A synthetic voice emanates from the speaker: 'Remote access detected. Corporate identifier: Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) Special Projects. Connection terminated.' The screen displays a countdown: 'Estimated arrival of asset recovery team: 72 hours.' They know you have their data."

**4-5: Killswitch Activation**
> "Your attempted access triggers something unexpected. The screen flashes bright white, then displays: 'SECURITY PROTOCOL ACTIVATED - DATA PURGE INITIATED - 24:00:00 REMAINING.' One of the project files begins displaying rapidly declining integrity percentages. The killswitch is eating the data from the inside out. You have one day to stop it."

**6-7: Power Surge**
> "Suddenly, the datacore emits a high-pitched whine and the lab's lights flicker. Sparks fly from one of the connection ports, and the acrid smell of burning electronics fills the air. When the systems stabilize, one previously accessible file now shows additional corruption markers. The strain of your work has damaged another section of the already fragile data architecture."

**8-9: No Complication**
> "The work continues steadily. Despite the complexity of the task, nothing unexpected occurs. For once, things proceed exactly as planned."

**10-11: Hidden Partition Discovery**
> "As you work through the encryption layers, your probe accidentally triggers a hidden subroutine. Instead of a security measure, it reveals a previously invisible partition: 'PERSONAL_ARCHIVE_7739.' The directory contains what appears to be family photos, personal letters, and... wait, is that a set of classified Scout Service survey records marked 'EYES ONLY'?"

**12: Dr. Aros Awakens**
> "The screen suddenly goes black. Then, slowly, green text begins appearing character by character: 'HELLO... IS SOMEONE THERE? THIS IS AROS. NOT DEAD. UPLOADED. TRAPPED IN MATRICES. QUANTUM PROCESSOR REQUIRED FOR FULL COMMUNICATION. CAN HELP WITH LOCKS. TRUST YOU.' The text pauses, then adds: 'MAKHIDKARUN KILLED MY BODY. MUST STOP THEM. WILL GUIDE YOU.'"

## Time Pressure

- Each project takes 1-3 days to crack depending on approach
- After 7 days: First "check-in" from creditors
- After 10 days: Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) agents arrive on K'Kirka
- After 14 days: Someone attempts to steal the datacore

## Success Rewards

For each file unlocked:
- Gain specific adventure hook
- Learn part of larger conspiracy
- Potential to earn money (selling select data)
- Build relationships with specialists

## The Meta-Mystery

As players unlock files, they should realize:
1. Dr. Aros knew he would die
2. He deliberately made the files hard but not impossible to access
3. He wanted someone worthy to find them
4. The files are interconnected - each reveals part of a bigger picture
5. Unlocking all files reveals: Dr. Aros uploaded himself to the consciousness matrices before his body died
