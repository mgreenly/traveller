# Scene 6: Ship Services & Supply

**Location:** K'Kirka Starport Services
**Timing:** Can occur anytime during the K'Kirka stay

> **Read Aloud:** The *Stardust Drifter* needs attention after its time drifting in the Djinni belt. K'Kirka Starport Services occupies a gleaming office complex overlooking the docking bays. The smell of fresh polymer and cleaning solutions fills the air - a stark contrast to your ship's current state. A holographic menu displays various service packages while a cheerful automated greeter asks, "How may we serve your vessel today?"
