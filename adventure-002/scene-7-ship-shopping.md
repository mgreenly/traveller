# Scene 7: Ship Shopping at K'Kirka Used Ship Emporium

**Location:** K'Kirka Starport, Bernstein & Associates Ship Brokers
**Timing:** After dealing with mortgage and datacore issues

> **Read Aloud:** The offices of Bernstein & Associates occupy prime real estate overlooking the main docking bays. Floor-to-ceiling transparisteel windows display dozens of ships in various states of repair. A well-dressed Vilani woman in her fifties greets you with practiced charm. "Welcome to Bernstein & Associates. I'm Kalinda Bernstein. I understand you're in the market for a vessel? Perhaps something more... manageable than your current situation?"

## The Sales Pitch

Ms. Bernstein has clearly done her homework on your financial situation:

> "Let's be frank. The *Stardust Drifter* is a magnificent ship, but at MCr 140 with your current debts, it's an albatross. I can offer you several alternatives that would leave you with capital to actually operate. All our ships come with... character. Full disclosure of quirks, as required by Imperial law."

## Negotiation Options

**Broker (TN 10):** Reduce price by 5%
- Effect 4+: Reduce by 10%

**Mechanic (TN 9):** Identify fixable problems
- Can negotiate another 5% off for DIY repairs

**Investigate (TN 11):** Uncover hidden issues
- Finds problems worth 10% discount but requires MCr 1 in parts

## Hidden Information

**Streetwise (TN 10) reveals:**
- "Black Diamond" - Previous owner was Section 86 operative
- "Swift Passage" - Three crew members went insane, one still in asylum
- "Phoenix Rising" - Has hidden Imperial Intelligence transponder
- "Wild Venture" - Previous owner killed the wife of a crime boss
- Bernstein gets 20% commission from Spinward Financial for debt resolutions

## Alternative: Keep the Drifter

If they insist on keeping their ship:

> "Your funeral. That's Cr 7.7 million in 90 days, MCr 12.5 to Kane in 60 days, plus Cr 583,333 every month forever. When you change your mind - and you will - I'll still be here. Though these particular ships won't be."

## Referee Notes

- All quirks should affect gameplay
- Ships with bad quirks are cheaper but create ongoing problems
- The radiation leak on Phoenix Rising could be a serious issue
- The psionic echoes on Swift Passage could lead to adventures
- Black-listed ships create travel restrictions
- Famous ships attract both good and bad attention
