# The Stardust Drifter Data Core

## Overview
The *Stardust Drifter's* data core contains terabytes of Dr. Aros's personal research files and logs. The players recovered it from the Scrap Vultures during adventure-001 and have had time to examine its contents during the journey to K'Kirka.

## Core Contents

### 1. The Kane Contingency File
**Access:** Hidden encrypted partition labeled "Insurance Policy - SKane" (Computers TN 10 to decrypt)

**Contents:**
- **Financial Records:** Detailed transactions showing Kane laundering money for Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run)'s illegal operations in the Spinward Marches
- **Recorded Conversations:** Audio files of Kane discussing "asset seizures" where borrowers met suspicious ends, including one chilling conversation about "making an example" of a defaulter  
- **Contract Database:** Proof that Kane's loan contracts contain clauses that violate Imperial usury laws and Commerce regulations
- **Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) Connection:** Documents proving Kane is not just a lender but a wholly-owned asset of Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run)'s Special Projects Division

**Usage Options:**
- **Direct Confrontation:** Present to Kane to nullify debt (Persuade TN 8 or Advocate TN 10)
- **Legal Route:** File with Imperial authorities (auto-cancels debt, makes permanent enemies)
- **Blackmail:** Ongoing leverage (+4 to negotiations with Kane, requires vigilance)
- **Sale to Rivals:** Other crime bosses/corporations would pay Cr500,000-1,000,000

### 2. The Ghost of Cthonia (kuh-THO-nee-uh)
**Access:** Fragmented Survey Data & Personal Log

**Content:** 
Points to uncharted moon **Cthonia (kuh-THO-nee-uh)** in the **Menkar** system (several parsecs off main trade routes). Dr. Aros's log:
> *"The structures are not of any known race. Geometric, crystalline... they hum in a frequency my sensors can't properly analyze. I had to leave before I could explore the primary subterranean entrance; the radiation spikes were becoming too dangerous. The site is pristine, completely unknown to the IISS. I've encrypted its location; can't risk a megacorp getting their claws on it."*

**Hook:** Coordinates to unknown alien archaeological site with mysterious radiation dangers

### 3. The "Sparrow" Correspondence  
**Access:** Series of heavily encrypted one-way communications

**Content:**
Messages sent TO Dr. Aros from unknown contact "Sparrow":
> *"They know. The Regina project was compromised. I'm burning this identity and going dark. Don't try to find me. I've enclosed the usual payment for your silence. Stay safe, old friend."*

Final message dated weeks before Aros abandoned ship. Includes digital wallet with untraceable credits.

**Hook:** Mystery of Sparrow's identity and the compromised "Regina project"

### 4. Project Prometheus
**Access:** Heavily Encrypted Research Database (Computers TN 12+ to decrypt)

**Content:**
- Genetic modification experiments for humans to survive extreme environments without life support
- Subjects developed unexpected psionic abilities as side effect
- Later entries discuss "controlled evolution" and "guided transcendence"  
- Final corrupted logs mention "stable hereditary traits" and warnings about "cascade mutations"

**Hook:** Forbidden research that may have gotten Dr. Aros killed - could revolutionize colonization or create new human subspecies

### 5. The Altaira Protocols
**Access:** Medical Database with Biometric Locks (named after deceased daughter)

**Content:**
- Revolutionary immortality through consciousness preservation
- Altaira died 3 days after Djinni incident aboard the *Stardust Drifter*
- Seven cloning attempts using preserved body (now missing)
- Consciousness data from Ancient sensors at Djinni site
- Combines Ancient preservation, classified Zhodani telepathic transfer
- Clone Seven achieved 73% consciousness integrity
- Corporate buyers lined up, several Imperial megacorps/nobles as secret funders

**Hook:** True immortality technology ready for deployment, original body and Clone Seven secured off-site

### 6. The Consciousness Matrices
**Access:** Quantum Data Storage Array (incompatible with standard readers)

**Content:**
- Complete neural maps of 12 individuals including "A.R." (possibly Aros)
- Technology for complete digitization/storage of human consciousness
- Notes about successful "re-instantiation" into cloned/synthetic bodies
- Disturbing entry: original Dr. Aros may have died years ago, players knew third copy

**Hook:** One stored consciousness keeps trying to communicate through ship systems

### 7. Project Omicron Particle
**Access:** Ancient Network Interface Protocol with Encrypted Logs

**Content:**
Quantum key unlocking dormant Ancient communication nodes. Activation revealed:
> *"System Reset Initiated - Contamination Threshold Exceeded - Species 7731 Industrial Markers Detected at Protected Site [DESIGNATION CORRUPTED] - Automatic Reclamation Sequence Commencing in 1,827 Cycles - All Non-Baseline Organic Matter Will Be Reconfigured to Original Template."*

- Human mining operation breached Ancient "Protected Site" ~1,820 cycles ago
- Triggered terraforming reset to destroy all current life
- Partial coordinates: "...327.4...[CORRUPTED]...Marches..."
- Three possible worlds in Spinward Marches
- Process begins with atmospheric auroras before reclamation

Dr. Aros's final note:
> *"The auroras should begin soon. We might have months, or mere days. I tried to abort - the system responded: 'Authority Not Recognized. Species 7731: Invasive. Quarantine Failed. Reclamation Authorized.' Someone needs to find which world and either evacuate the population or find an Ancient site with override authority. The seventeen dormant nodes I've located might hold the key - including one beneath Regina's capital."*

**Hook:** Imminent planetary catastrophe requiring urgent action

## Adventure Hooks Summary

### Immediate Threats
1. **Kane Contingency** - Leverage against debt or dangerous knowledge to possess
2. **Omicron Particle** - Planetary extinction event countdown has begun

### Investigation Opportunities  
3. **Sparrow Mystery** - Who are they and what was the Regina project?
4. **Consciousness Matrices** - Who is trying to communicate through the ship?

### Exploration Missions
5. **Ghost of Cthonia (kuh-THO-nee-uh)** - Unknown alien ruins in Menkar system
6. **Ancient Nodes** - 17 dormant sites including one beneath Regina

### Moral Dilemmas
7. **Project Prometheus** - Forbidden genetic enhancement technology
8. **Altaira Protocols** - Immortality for sale to highest bidders

### Corporate Intrigue
9. **Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run)** - They want this data back and will kill for it
10. **Secret Funders** - Imperial megacorps and nobles funding illegal research

## Player Decision Points

The players must decide:
- What to do with the Kane Contingency (use it, sell it, destroy it?)
- Which leads to pursue first
- Whether to report dangerous information to authorities
- How to handle corporate and noble interests in the data
- Whether to keep or sell the forbidden research
- How urgently to pursue the Omicron Particle threat

## Referee Notes

- Players should discover these files gradually during downtime
- Each file requires different skill checks to access
- Some files may be corrupted or incomplete, requiring additional investigation
- The data core itself becomes a valuable MacGuffin that others will pursue
- Not all hooks need to be pursued - they provide campaign flexibility
- The Omicron Particle provides a ticking clock if needed for urgency
