## Introduction Section

### Finding University Resources
**Primary Discovery Method:** Public knowledge - anyone can give directions

Players might learn about the university's capabilities through:
- **Education (TN 8):** "Any tech institute has quantum processors and recovery software. K'Kirka Tech rents time."
- **Admin (TN 9):** "Universities offset costs by renting lab time. K'Kirka Tech has excellent facilities."
- **Library Data (TN 7):** K'Kirka Technical Institute advertises quantum computing and data recovery services
- **Automatic if asking locals:** "The university? Big glass tower, can't miss it. Take the tram to University Station."

### What Others Say
- **Graduate student:** "The quantum lab? Building 7, sub-level 2. Professor Martinez runs it. Charges Cr 5,000 per day for external users."
- **University administrator:** "We provide computational resources to legitimate researchers. Proper credentials are required, of course."
- **Corporate researcher:** "K'Kirka Tech's quantum array is only TL-13, but it's well-maintained. Good enough for most commercial decryption."
- **Professor Vault (if met):** "The university has excellent resources. Pity they lack the vision to use them properly. Martinez is competent but unimaginative."

### Access Methods
- **Day Pass:** Cr 100 for building access (doesn't include equipment)
- **Academic Credentials:** Free access if you can prove academic affiliation
- **Student Escort:** Free if you befriend a student
- **Corporate Contract:** Full access with proper documentation
- **Student Access:** Befriend a graduate student who has lab privileges
- **After Hours:** Stealth (TN 9) to use equipment at night

**Location:** K'Kirka Technical Institute, Building 7

> **Read Aloud:** Building 7 houses the Institute's pride - its Computational Sciences Department. The lobby showcases holographic displays of molecular simulations and quantum equations that shift and dance in mesmerizing patterns. A security desk controls access to the lower levels where the expensive equipment resides. The receptionist, clearly a student working part-time, looks up from her studies. "Quantum lab access? You'll need to fill out these forms and provide proof of insurance. The daily rate is 5,000 credits, paid in advance."

## The Quantum Lab

### Professor Elena Martinez
The lab director is helpful but strictly by-the-book.

> **Read Aloud:** Professor Martinez is a woman in her forties with precisely styled hair and an immaculate lab coat. She gestures to the quantum processor - a pillar of crystallized circuits surrounded by cooling systems. "This is a Model Q7-3000. It can process quantum encryption, run consciousness simulations, or interface with Ancient technology - theoretically. What exactly is your research purpose?"

### Services Available

#### Quantum Processor Rental
- **Cost:** Cr 5,000/day
- **Capabilities:** Can unlock Consciousness Matrices files
- **Risk:** All activities are logged (could alert interested parties)
- **Success Rate:** Automatic with 4 hours of processing

#### Data Recovery Software Suite
- **Cost:** Cr 1,000 to use, Cr 10,000 to purchase license
- **Capabilities:** Can recover corrupted Cthonia (kuh-THO-nee-uh) data
- **Requirement:** Someone with Electronics or Computers skill
- **Success Rate:** 90% recovery with proper operation

#### Research Database Access
- **Cost:** Free with academic credentials, Cr 500 otherwise
- **Capabilities:** Can research Ancient technology, psionics (historical only), genetics
- **Useful for:** Understanding what you're looking at in the files

### The Lab Assistant

There's always a graduate student on duty who might be more flexible:

> **Read Aloud:** When Professor Martinez steps out, the lab assistant - a young man with coffee-stained clothes and three days of stubble - leans closer. "Between you and me, if you need after-hours access, I could 'forget' to lock up. Would need to cover my risk though - say, 2,000 credits?"

### After Hours Options

**If players sneak in or bribe the assistant:**
- No official logs kept
- 4 hours of uninterrupted access
- Risk: Security makes rounds every 2 hours (Stealth TN 7 to avoid)
- Complication: Another group might be using the lab illegally too

**Investigate (TN 11) or Carouse (TN 10) with staff reveals:**
Martinez is being pressured by Makhidkarun (mah-KHID-kah-run) to report any unusual quantum processing requests. She hasn't agreed but hasn't refused either. She's conflicted about academic freedom versus corporate funding.
