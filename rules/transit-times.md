# Jump Distances and Travel Times

## Overview

In Traveller, ships cannot jump within 100 diameters of any object larger than themselves. This document provides travel times from various celestial bodies to their jump limits.

## Travel Time Formula

**Time = 2 × √(Distance/Acceleration)**

Where:
- Distance is in kilometers
- Acceleration is in G (1G = ~10 m/s²)
- Time is in seconds

This formula accounts for accelerating to the midpoint, then decelerating for the remaining distance.

## Key Observations

1. **Planetary Jump Points**: Most ships jump from planetary 100-diameter limits rather than stellar limits due to the significant time savings.

2. **Gas Giants**: Despite their size, reaching jump distance from gas giants rarely takes more than a day, even with low-thrust drives.

3. **Small Bodies**: Asteroids and small moons allow very quick access to jump space, making them popular refueling stops.

4. **Thrust Economics**: The time savings between M1 and M3 drives are significant, but diminishing returns set in with higher thrust ratings.

5. **Strategic Considerations**: 
   - Systems with small mainworlds have faster access to jump routes
   - Gas giant refueling adds 12-24 hours to most journeys
   - Deep space (stellar limit) jumps are emergency measures only

## Practical Examples

**Typical Trading Route** (Earth-like world):
- Depart surface to jump point (M2 drive): 4.9 hours
- Jump (1 week)
- Arrive at destination jump point
- Travel to surface (M2 drive): 4.9 hours
- Total non-jump time: ~10 hours

**Gas Giant Refueling** (Saturn-sized):
- Approach from jump point: 15.0 hours (M2 drive)
- Skim and refine fuel: 2-8 hours
- Depart to jump point: 15.0 hours (M2 drive)
- Total time: 32-38 hours
