# Travel Zones

In the Traveller universe, systems are often given a travel advisory code by the Travellers' Aid Society (TAS) and the Third Imperium. There are two types of travel zones: Amber and Red.

## Amber Zone

**Amber Zones** indicate that Travellers should exercise caution when visiting such worlds. These are worlds that have been deemed dangerous by the Imperium, and Travellers are warned to be on their guard.

Key characteristics of Amber Zones:

* **Caution Required:** Travellers should be particularly careful when visiting these systems.
* **Various Dangers:** The Amber classification can indicate several types of hazards:
  * **Political Instability:** The world may be undergoing upheaval, revolution, or civil unrest.
  * **Xenophobia:** The local citizens may be hostile to outsiders or alien visitors.
  * **Natural Hazards:** The world may have naturally dangerous environments or conditions.
  * **Chaotic Situations:** The political or social situation may be unpredictable.
* **Warning, Not Prohibition:** Unlike Red Zones, travel to Amber worlds is not forbidden - visitors are simply advised to exercise extreme caution.

*Guidelines for Amber Classification:* A world with Atmosphere 10+, Government 0, 7, or 10, and Law Level 0 or 9+ should be considered for Amber status.

## Red Zone

A **Red Zone** (or "Red System") is a world or system to which all travel is strictly forbidden by the Imperium.

Key characteristics of Red Zones:

*   **Quarantine:** The system is under Imperial interdiction. The Imperial Navy will actively patrol the system's jump points to prevent unauthorized entry or exit.
*   **Extreme Danger:** The reasons for the "Red" classification are always severe. They can include:
    *   **Plague:** A highly contagious and deadly disease is rampant.
    *   **War:** The system is an active warzone, potentially involving weapons of mass destruction.
    *   **Hostile Environment:** The local environment is catastrophically dangerous due to natural disasters or technological failure.
    *   **Forbidden Culture/Tech:** The native population is being protected from cultural contamination (a "Preserve" world), or the world possesses forbidden technologies that the Imperium wants to contain.
    *   **Hostile Non-Human Species:** The dominant species is actively and dangerously hostile to humans.
*   **Consequences:** Ignoring the restriction is a serious crime. Unauthorized ships attempting to enter or leave a Red Zone can be fired upon without warning, boarded, and impounded. Their crews will face imprisonment or worse.

## Adventure Opportunities

For players, both Amber and Red Zones present significant adventure opportunities:

* **Amber Zones** offer moderate-risk scenarios where caution and preparation are essential. Adventures might involve navigating political upheaval, dealing with xenophobic populations, or surviving dangerous natural conditions.
* **Red Zones** are massive plot hooks for high-stakes adventures. Any mission requiring travel to a Red Zone—whether for smuggling, extraction, rescue, or research—is guaranteed to be extremely dangerous and challenging.

Both zone types should be used by referees to create tension, add complexity to travel planning, and provide hooks for exciting adventures in the Traveller universe.
