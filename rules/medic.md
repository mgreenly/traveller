## Injury Assessment

### Damage States
When PCs are injured, assess their condition based on characteristic damage:

- **Healthy:** All three physical characteristics at maximum values
- **Wounded:** One or more physical characteristic damaged
- **Unconscious:** Two or more physical characteristics at 0
- **Disabling Wound:** All three physical characteristics at 0 (roll on Disabling Wounds table - Traveller Companion p.53)
- **Dead:** Determined by Disabling Wounds table result

### Immediate Priorities
1. **Stabilization/First Aid** (if disabling wound indicates "will die unless given prompt medical assistance")
2. **First Aid** (if wounded, within 1 minute)
3. **Surgery** (if three characteristics damaged after first aid)
4. **Medical Care** (ongoing recovery)

**Important Note on Deterioration:** Wounded characters do NOT deteriorate over time UNLESS they need surgery (three damaged characteristics) AND have a negative END DM. In that case, they lose characteristic points equal to their negative END DM each day until surgery is performed. This makes surgery urgent for severely wounded characters with low Endurance.

## Field Medicine & Stabilization

### First Aid (Core Rules p.82)
**When to Use:** 
- PC is **conscious** (fewer than two characteristics at 0)
- Within **1 minute** of receiving the injury
- PC has taken damage but is still functioning

**Roll:** **INT + Medic vs 8+** (Core Rules p.82)
**Timing:** Must be done immediately after combat/injury (within 1 minute)
**Success:** Restores Effect + 1 characteristic points (minimum 1)
**Limitation:** Can only be successfully applied once per injury

**Equipment & Supplies Used:**
- **Medikit** (any TL version) - provides diagnostic tools and supplies
- **Bandages & Wound Dressings** - stop bleeding, protect wounds
- **Antiseptics & Antibiotics** - prevent infection
- **Painkillers** - allow patient to function
- **Panaceas** (optional) - general treatment drug for infections
- **Basic surgical tools** - for wound cleaning/closing

**Distribution:** Player chooses how to distribute restored points among damaged characteristics

**Example:** Medic rolls Effect 3, restores 4 points total. PC has lost 6 END, 3 STR. Player might restore 3 END, 1 STR.

## Disabling Wounds (Traveller Companion p.53)

When a Traveller takes a disabling wound (all three physical characteristics reduced to 0), roll 2D on the Disabling Wounds table with the following modifiers:

### Disabling Wounds Modifiers (Traveller Companion p.53)
- **Traveller's END DM:** Add as modifier
- **Final attack less than 3 points of damage:** +4
- **Final attack caused 4-6 points of damage:** +2  
- **Final attack caused more than 6 points of damage:** -2

### Disabling Wounds Table (Traveller Companion p.53)
| 2D Roll | Result |
|---------|--------|
| 2 or less | The Traveller is vaporised, shredded, spread all over the landscape or otherwise destroyed in graphic and gruesome fashion. |
| 3 | The Traveller is either killed outright in a dramatic fashion such as having their head blown off, or lingers just long enough to make a final speech to their friends/enemies/passers-by. They cannot be saved in either case. |
| 4-5 | The Traveller will die unless given prompt medical assistance. If they survive, they will lose the limb that was injured, or an eye, or suffer some similar major permanent injury as determined by the Referee. This can of course be repaired with cybernetics but the process will be slow and expensive. |
| 6-7 | The Traveller will die unless given prompt medical assistance and suffer permanent effects if they survive at all. Lose 1D from any one of STR, DEX or END and D3 from the others. |
| 8-9 | The Traveller will survive if given even the most basic emergency assistance and suffer no ill effects providing they receive proper medical treatment whilst recovering. If it is not, the Traveller will permanently lose D3 points from both STR and END. |
| 10-11 | The Traveller will survive despite their injuries, even without assistance, and will make a full recovery if medical attention is successfully provided. |
| 12+ | The Traveller is terribly hurt but will somehow cling to life and begin to recover even if medical attention is not provided. |

### Emergency Medical Assistance (Traveller Companion p.53)
**When Required:** Disabling Wounds table results 4-9
**What It Means:** "First aid will keep a Traveller with a severe wound from dying but will not prevent permanent effects. Only successful surgery and long-term care will suffice."

**Prompt Medical Assistance Requirements:**
- **Roll:** INT + Medic vs 8+ (standard First Aid check)
- **Equipment:** Medikit strongly recommended
- **Timing:** Must be applied immediately
- **Effect:** Prevents death but does NOT prevent permanent injuries
- **Follow-up:** Surgery and medical care required to prevent permanent effects

## Hit Location Table (Traveller Companion p.54)

When determining which limb is lost or injured (such as from Disabling Wounds result 4-5), roll 1D:

| 1D Roll | Location |
|---------|----------|
| 1 | Head |
| 2 | Arms |
| 3-5 | Torso |
| 6 | Legs |

### Unconsciousness Recovery (Core Rules p.83)
**Natural Recovery:** END check every minute
- Success: Regain consciousness
- Failure: Wait 1 minute, try again with cumulative +1 DM per failed attempt

## Hospital Treatment

### Surgery (Core Rules p.82)
- **When:** PC has three damaged characteristics after first aid
- **Where:** Hospital or ship's sickbay required (Core Rules p.82)
- **Roll:** **EDU + Medic vs 8+** (Core Rules p.82)
- **Success:** Restores Effect + 1 characteristic points (minimum 1)
- **Failure:** PC loses **3 + |Effect|** additional points (Effect is negative)

**Important:** Surgery is dangerous - failed attempts cause more damage!

**Goal:** Restore one characteristic to maximum to qualify for medical care

### Medical Care (Core Rules p.83)
- **When:** PC has 1-2 damaged characteristics (or after successful surgery)
- **Where:** Hospital or ship's sickbay with full bed rest (Core Rules p.83)
- **Healing Rate:** **3 + END DM + doctor's Medic skill** points per day (Core Rules p.83)
- **Distribution:** Points divided evenly among damaged characteristics

**High-Tech Procedure:** TL11+ hospitals typically use Slow Drug to accelerate healing (1 month of healing in 1 day) (Core Rules p.83, p.115)

### Natural Healing (Without Medical Care) (Core Rules p.83)
 - **Normal Rest:** **1D + END DM** points per day of full rest (Core Rules p.83)
 - **Severe Injury:** Only **END DM** per day if surgery is still needed (Core Rules p.83)
   - If END DM is negative, PC gets worse each day!
   - Surgery is essential for severely wounded PCs

## Medical Equipment

### Medikits (Core Rules p.114)
**TL8 Basic Medikit:** Cr1,000, 1kg
- Contains diagnostic devices, surgical tools, drugs, antibiotics

**TL10 Advanced Medikit:** Cr1,500, 1kg
- **DM+1** to first aid Medic checks

**TL12 Superior Medikit:** Cr5,000, 1kg
- **DM+2** to first aid Medic checks

**TL14 Elite Medikit:** Cr10,000, 0kg (effective mass)
- **DM+3** to first aid Medic checks
- Medical densitometer, quantum brain scanners

### Emergency Equipment
**Cryoberth (TL10):** Cr50,000, 200kg
- Instant stasis for severely injured PCs
- Internal power: 1 week
- Use when surgery isn't immediately available

**Medical Supplies:** Various costs (Cr5 - Cr1,000s)
- Basic: Bandages, antiseptics, diagnostic tools
- Advanced: Surgical instruments, trauma stabilizers
- Requires Medic skill to use properly

## Drugs & Medications

### Emergency Drugs
**Panaceas (TL8+):** Cr200/dose (Core Rules p.115)
- Allows **Medic 0** check for treating infections/diseases (Core Rules p.115)
- Safe for any wound or illness
- Won't make things worse

**Stims (TL8):** Cr50/dose (Core Rules p.115)
- Removes Fatigue but causes 1 point damage (Core Rules p.115)
- Cumulative damage if used repeatedly without sleep
- **Risk:** Second use = 2 damage, third use = 3 damage, etc.

### Combat Enhancement
**Combat Drugs (TL10):** Cr1,000/dose (Core Rules p.115)
- **DM+4** to Initiative rolls (Core Rules p.115)
- Free Reaction every round (Core Rules p.115)
- **-2** to all damage received (Core Rules p.115)
- Kicks in after 3 rounds, lasts 10 minutes
- **Side Effect:** Fatigued when it wears off

### Healing Acceleration
**Slow Drug (TL11):** Cr500/dose (Core Rules p.115)
- **Hospital/Sickbay Only** - lethal outside medical facility (Core Rules p.115)
- 30x normal metabolism = 1 month healing in 1 day (Core Rules p.115)
- Requires life support and cryotechnology

### Radiation Treatment
**Anti-rad (TL8):** Cr1,000/dose (Core Rules p.115)
- Must be taken within 10 minutes of exposure (Core Rules p.115)
- Absorbs up to 100 rads per dose (Core Rules p.115)
- **Limit:** Once per day (more causes permanent END damage)

## Costs & Insurance

### Hospital Treatment Costs (Core Rules p.114)
**Medical Care:** Cr250 per month per Tech Level (Core Rules p.114)
- TL8 hospital: Cr2,000/month
- TL12 hospital: Cr3,000/month
- At TL11+, Slow Drug is often standard procedure

**Surgery:** **1D × Cr50 × Tech Level** per operation (Core Rules p.114)
- TL8: 1D × Cr400 average
- TL12: 1D × Cr600 average
- Multiple operations may be needed for severe injuries

**Characteristic Replacement:** Cr5,000 per point lost (Core Rules p.114)
- Cloning limbs or cybernetic replacement
- For permanent characteristic damage

### Insurance Considerations
**Imperial Standard Coverage:**
- Basic emergency treatment usually covered
- Major surgery may require pre-authorization
- Cybernetic replacement typically excluded
- Combat injuries may void coverage

**Travellers' Insurance:**
- Higher premiums but covers dangerous activities
- Emergency evacuation included
- Combat injury coverage available
- May require medical examination

**Corporate Plans:**
- Megacorp employees often have premium coverage
- May include experimental treatments
- Coverage varies by employment level

## Special Situations

### Augmented Characters (Core Rules p.83)
**Treatment Difficulty:** Medical checks suffer **negative DM** equal to: (Core Rules p.83)
**(Highest implant Tech Level) - (Medical facility Tech Level)**

**Example:** TL13 cybernetic eyes treated in TL10 hospital = **DM-3** to all Medic checks

### Alien Physiology
**Non-Human PCs:** May require specialized knowledge
- Vargr, Aslan: Similar enough to humans for basic treatment
- Exotic aliens: Require Life Sciences (Biology) checks
- Unknown species: Medic checks at significant negative DMs

### Battlefield Medicine (Core Rules p.69)
**Extended Action:** **INT + Medic**, 1D minutes (Core Rules p.69)
- Can be performed during combat
- Requires multiple rounds to complete
- May be interrupted by enemy action

### Radiation Exposure (Core Rules p.81)
**Treatment:** Anti-rad drugs only way to remove accumulated rads (Core Rules p.81)
**Prevention:** Vacc suits reduce exposure (Core Rules p.81)
**Ship Protection:** Spacecraft hull reduces exposure by 500 rads (Core Rules p.81)

### Emergency Procedures Checklist
1. **Assess Condition:** Count characteristics at 0
2. **Stabilize if Unconscious:** Medic check to prevent death
3. **Apply First Aid:** If conscious, within 1 minute
4. **Transport to Medical:** If surgery needed
5. **Arrange Payment:** Medical bills can be substantial
6. **Monitor Recovery:** Track daily healing progress

---

**Referee Notes:**
- Medical treatment should feel expensive and time-consuming
- Failed medical checks can be deadly - emphasize the risk
- Consider requiring specific medical training for advanced procedures
- Use medical costs as adventure motivation (PCs need money for treatment)
- Remember that medical care requires bed rest - PCs are out of action during recovery
