## Admin
**Use:** Bureaucracy, paperwork, dealing with officials, managing organizations
- **INT + Admin:** Understanding regulations, filing paperwork correctly
- **SOC + Admin:** Navigating bureaucratic hierarchies, dealing with officials
- **EDU + Admin:** Knowledge of proper procedures and protocols

## Advocate
**Use:** Legal matters, contracts, courtroom procedures, legal research
- **INT + Advocate:** Understanding legal complexities, finding loopholes
- **EDU + Advocate:** Knowledge of law and precedents
- **SOC + Advocate:** Courtroom presence, swaying judges/juries

## Animals
**Use:** Handling, training, riding animals, veterinary care
- **DEX + Animals:** Riding, physical animal handling
- **INT + Animals:** Training animals, understanding behavior
- **EDU + Animals:** Veterinary knowledge and care

## Art
**Use:** Creating or performing artistic works
- **DEX + Art:** Physical performance, playing instruments, painting
- **INT + Art:** Composition, creative design
- **SOC + Art:** Stage presence, connecting with audience

## Astrogation
**Use:** Plotting jump courses, calculating jump coordinates
- **EDU + Astrogation:** Standard jump calculations
- **INT + Astrogation:** Complex or unusual jump plotting
- **DEX + Astrogation:** Quick calculations under pressure

## Athletics
**Use:** Physical activities, climbing, jumping, running, swimming
- **STR + Athletics:** Climbing, jumping, swimming
- **DEX + Athletics:** Balance, coordination tasks
- **END + Athletics:** Long-distance running, endurance activities

## Broker
**Use:** Finding buyers/sellers, negotiating trade deals, evaluating goods
- **INT + Broker:** Evaluating market conditions, finding opportunities
- **SOC + Broker:** Negotiating prices, building trade relationships
- **EDU + Broker:** Knowledge of trade regulations and practices

## Carouse
**Use:** Social drinking, fitting in at parties, gathering information socially
- **SOC + Carouse:** Making social connections, being the life of the party
- **END + Carouse:** Drinking contests, staying coherent while intoxicated
- **INT + Carouse:** Gathering information while socializing

## Deception
**Use:** Lying, disguises, misdirection, forgery
- **INT + Deception:** Creating believable lies, planning deceptions
- **SOC + Deception:** Delivering lies convincingly, acting
- **DEX + Deception:** Sleight of hand, physical misdirection

## Diplomat
**Use:** Formal negotiations, protocol, international relations
- **SOC + Diplomat:** Formal negotiations, following protocol
- **INT + Diplomat:** Understanding negotiating positions, finding compromises
- **EDU + Diplomat:** Knowledge of diplomatic procedures and history

## Drive
**Use:** Operating ground vehicles
- **DEX + Drive:** Standard driving, maneuvering
- **INT + Drive:** Navigation, route planning
- **STR + Drive:** Controlling heavy vehicles

## Electronics
**Use:** Operating and repairing electronic devices, computers, sensors
- **INT + Electronics (computers):** Programming, hacking, data manipulation
- **EDU + Electronics (comms):** Operating communication systems
- **INT + Electronics (sensors):** Interpreting sensor data
- **DEX + Electronics (remote ops):** Operating drones and remote systems

## Engineer
**Use:** Maintaining and repairing starship systems
- **INT + Engineer (m-drive):** Diagnosing drive problems
- **EDU + Engineer (j-drive):** Jump drive maintenance
- **INT + Engineer (power):** Power distribution and management
- **STR + Engineer (life support):** Physical repairs to life support

## Explosives
**Use:** Placing, disarming, and creating explosive devices
- **INT + Explosives:** Calculating blast effects, choosing explosives
- **DEX + Explosives:** Placing charges, disarming bombs
- **EDU + Explosives:** Knowledge of explosive types and chemistry

## Flyer
**Use:** Operating aircraft and flying vehicles
- **DEX + Flyer:** Piloting, maneuvering
- **INT + Flyer:** Navigation, flight planning
- **EDU + Flyer:** Understanding flight systems

## Gambler
**Use:** Games of chance, understanding odds, cheating
- **INT + Gambler:** Calculating odds, card counting
- **DEX + Gambler:** Sleight of hand, cheating physically
- **SOC + Gambler:** Reading opponents, bluffing

## Gun Combat
**Use:** Using personal firearms
- **DEX + Gun Combat:** Aiming and firing
- **INT + Gun Combat:** Tactical positioning
- **STR + Gun Combat:** Controlling recoil on heavy weapons

## Gunner
**Use:** Operating vehicle and starship weapons
- **DEX + Gunner (turret):** Tracking and firing at targets
- **INT + Gunner (ortillery):** Calculating orbital bombardment
- **EDU + Gunner (screen):** Operating defensive screens

## Heavy Weapons
**Use:** Operating support weapons, artillery
- **STR + Heavy Weapons:** Managing heavy weapon systems
- **INT + Heavy Weapons:** Calculating indirect fire
- **DEX + Heavy Weapons:** Direct fire with heavy weapons

## Investigate
**Use:** Searching for clues, research, analysis
- **INT + Investigate:** Analyzing evidence, making deductions
- **EDU + Investigate:** Research, using databases
- **SOC + Investigate:** Interviewing witnesses

## Jack-of-all-Trades
**Use:** Attempting untrained skills at reduced penalty
- Reduces unskilled penalty by skill level
- Can use any characteristic appropriate to the task

## Language
**Use:** Speaking and understanding foreign languages
- **EDU + Language:** Proper grammar and formal usage
- **INT + Language:** Understanding context and idioms
- **SOC + Language:** Accent and cultural appropriateness

## Leadership
**Use:** Commanding others, inspiring groups, organizing teams
- **SOC + Leadership:** Inspiring and motivating
- **INT + Leadership:** Tactical planning, organizing
- **STR + Leadership:** Leading through intimidation

## Mechanic
**Use:** Repairing mechanical devices and vehicles
- **INT + Mechanic:** Diagnosing problems
- **EDU + Mechanic:** Understanding schematics
- **STR + Mechanic:** Physical repairs

## Medic
**Use:** First aid, surgery, diagnosis, treatment
- **EDU + Medic:** Diagnosis, knowledge of conditions
- **INT + Medic:** Surgery, emergency treatment
- **DEX + Medic:** Delicate procedures

## Melee
**Use:** Close combat with or without weapons
- **STR + Melee:** Damage and overpowering
- **DEX + Melee:** Accuracy and finesse
- **END + Melee:** Prolonged combat

## Navigation
**Use:** Finding your way on planet surfaces
- **INT + Navigation:** Route planning, using maps
- **EDU + Navigation:** Using navigation equipment
- **END + Navigation:** Long-distance travel on foot

## Persuade
**Use:** Convincing others, changing minds, argumentation
- **SOC + Persuade:** Charm and likability
- **INT + Persuade:** Logical arguments
- **STR + Persuade:** Intimidation

## Pilot
**Use:** Operating spacecraft
- **DEX + Pilot:** Ship maneuvering
- **INT + Pilot:** Navigation and plotting courses
- **EDU + Pilot:** Understanding ship systems

## Profession
**Use:** Specific professional knowledge and practice
- Varies by profession type
- Generally **INT** or **EDU** for knowledge
- **DEX** or **STR** for physical professions

## Recon
**Use:** Observation, spotting hidden things, surveillance
- **INT + Recon:** Spotting patterns, analytical observation
- **EDU + Recon:** Using surveillance equipment
- **END + Recon:** Long-term surveillance

## Science
**Use:** Scientific knowledge and research
- **EDU + Science:** Theoretical knowledge
- **INT + Science:** Practical application, experimentation
- **DEX + Science:** Laboratory work

## Seafarer
**Use:** Operating watercraft, naval knowledge
- **DEX + Seafarer:** Boat handling
- **INT + Seafarer:** Navigation at sea
- **END + Seafarer:** Long voyages

## Stealth
**Use:** Moving unseen, hiding, avoiding detection
- **DEX + Stealth:** Moving quietly
- **INT + Stealth:** Choosing hiding spots, timing movement
- **END + Stealth:** Remaining still for long periods

## Steward
**Use:** Service, hospitality, caring for passengers
- **SOC + Steward:** High-class service, etiquette
- **EDU + Steward:** Knowledge of cuisine and customs
- **DEX + Steward:** Food preparation and presentation

## Streetwise
**Use:** Urban survival, criminal contacts, black markets
- **INT + Streetwise:** Finding black markets, understanding criminal operations
- **SOC + Streetwise:** Fitting in with criminals, making contacts
- **EDU + Streetwise:** Knowledge of criminal organizations

## Survival
**Use:** Wilderness survival, finding food/shelter
- **INT + Survival:** Finding resources, navigation
- **EDU + Survival:** Knowledge of dangers, edible plants
- **END + Survival:** Enduring harsh conditions

## Tactics
**Use:** Military planning, small unit combat coordination
- **INT + Tactics (military):** Planning operations
- **EDU + Tactics (naval):** Space combat tactics
- **SOC + Tactics:** Coordinating with allied units

## Vacc Suit
**Use:** Operating in vacuum, zero-G movement
- **DEX + Vacc Suit:** Zero-G maneuvering
- **INT + Vacc Suit:** Suit diagnostics and repair
- **END + Vacc Suit:** Extended EVA operations
