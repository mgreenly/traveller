## Fuel Types and Pricing

### Refined Fuel (Core Rules p.156)
- **Cost:** Cr500 per ton
- **Where:** Starports and facilities that regularly host spacecraft
- **Safety:** Safe for jumping

### Unrefined Fuel (Core Rules p.156)
- **Cost:** Cr100 per ton
- **Where:** Out-of-the-way places, wilderness refueling
- **Danger:** Using unrefined fuel when jumping gives **DM-2** to jump check (Core Rules p.157)
- **Note:** Can be refined using fuel processors

## Fuel Consumption

### Jump Drive Fuel (Core Rules p.157, p.180)
**Consumption per jump:** 10% of hull tonnage × jump number

Examples:
- 100-ton ship, Jump-1: 10 tons of fuel
- 100-ton ship, Jump-2: 20 tons of fuel
- 200-ton ship, Jump-1: 20 tons of fuel
- 200-ton ship, Jump-3: 60 tons of fuel

**Important:** 
- Jumps less than 1 parsec still consume Jump-1 fuel
- All jumps take roughly one week (148 + 6D hours)

### Power Plant Fuel (Core Rules p.180)
**Standard consumption:** 10% of power plant size provides 4 weeks of operation
- Minimum 1 ton regardless of power plant size
- Can be extended by adding more fuel tankage
- Example: Doubling fuel provides 8 weeks of operation

### Manoeuvre Drive
**No separate fuel consumption** - powered by the power plant

## Refueling Methods

### Starport Refueling (Core Rules p.156)
- **Time:** 1D hours to refuel a typical ship
- **Refined fuel:** Cr500 per ton
- **Unrefined fuel:** Cr100 per ton (if available)

### Wilderness Refueling (Core Rules p.157)
Ships with **fuel scoops** can gather unrefined fuel from:

#### Water Sources
- **Method:** Using hoses to pump from bodies of water
- **Result:** Unrefined fuel
- **Requirements:** Fuel scoops

#### Gas Giant Skimming (Core Rules p.157)
- **Requirements:** Fuel scoops (built into streamlined ships)
- **Skill Check:** Difficult (10+) Pilot check
- **Time:** 1D hours
- **Characteristic:** DEX
- **Result:** Unrefined fuel
- **Note:** Fuel processors can refine it afterward

## Fuel Processing

### Fuel Processors (Core Rules p.185)
- **Function:** Convert unrefined fuel to refined fuel
- **Rate:** 20 tons per day per ton of processor
- **Cost:** Cr50,000 per ton
- **Power:** 1 Power per ton

Example: A 2-ton fuel processor can refine 40 tons of unrefined fuel per day

### Fuel Scoops (Core Rules p.185)
- **Streamlined ships:** Built-in automatically
- **Unstreamlined ships:** Can be added
- **Cost:** MCr1
- **Tonnage:** 0 tons
- **Function:** Allows gathering unrefined fuel from gas giants or water

## Extra Fuel Storage

### Standard Fuel Tanks (Core Rules p.180)
- **Cost:** No cost for fuel tank space
- **Location:** Internal to hull
- **Access:** Direct feed to jump drive and power plant

### Collapsible Fuel Tanks (Core Rules p.184-185)
- **Function:** Flexible bladders that expand when filled
- **Location:** Takes up cargo space
- **Cost:** Cr500 per ton
- **Space when empty:** 1% of full capacity
- **Limitation:** Cannot pump directly to jump drive - must complete jump first, then transfer
- **Use:** Extend range without permanent tanks

### Drop Tanks (Core Rules p.204)
- **Function:** External tanks that can be jettisoned
- **Use:** Extend jump range temporarily
- **Note:** Mentioned but not detailed in Core Rules

## Jump Fuel Requirements

### 100-Diameter Limit (Core Rules p.157)
Ships can only safely jump when more than 100 diameters from any object larger than the ship.

Example distances:
- Earth: 1.27 million km
- Sol: 140 million km  
- Moon: 300,000 km

**Jumping within limit:** DM-4 to jump check

### Jump Preparation Modifiers (Core Rules p.157)
| Condition | DM |
|-----------|-----|
| Jump drive not maintained | -1 per month behind |
| Using unrefined fuel | -2 |
| Within 100-diameter limit | -4 |

## Important Notes

1. **Misjump Risk:** Using unrefined fuel or poor maintenance increases misjump chance
2. **Fuel Transfer:** Collapsible tanks cannot feed jump drive directly
3. **Streamlined Advantage:** Streamlined ships have built-in fuel scoops
4. **Processing Time:** Plan ahead when using fuel processors - they take time
5. **Emergency Fuel:** Always maintain reserve for power plant operations
