# Swift Passage (Modified Far Trader)

## Registry
- **Registry Number:** FT-7734-M
- **Ship Class:** Modified Type-A2 Far Trader
- **Tonnage:** 200 tons

## Description
A Far Trader optimized for speed and sensor capability. Built for a failed courier service that needed to deliver high-value, time-sensitive cargo. The ship sacrifices some cargo space for exceptional thrust and advanced electronics, making it one of the fastest merchant vessels in its class.

- **Computer:** Advanced Computer/10 (+MCr 5)
- **Sensors:** High-performance suite (+MCr 4.88)

## Special Features
- **High Speed:** Thrust-3 allows rapid in-system travel and escape capability

## Notes
The Swift Passage is ideal for time-critical deliveries, courier work, or situations where speed is more valuable than cargo capacity. The Thrust-3 capability means it can outrun most civilian ships and many military vessels. The lack of weapons is its main vulnerability.
