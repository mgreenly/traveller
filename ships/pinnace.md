# Pinnace

## Standard Pinnace

**Class:** Small Craft  
**Tech Level:** 12  
**Tonnage:** 40 tons (Streamlined)  
**Hull Points:** 16  

### Performance
- **Thrust:** 5
- **Jump:** None
- **Power Plant:** Fusion (Power 30)
- **Fuel:** 4 weeks operation

### Electronics
- **Computer:** Computer/5
- **Sensors:** Basic
- **Software:** Library, Manoeuvre, Intellect

### Armament
- **Hardpoints:** 1 Fixed Mount (empty)

### Accommodations
- **Crew:** 1 (Pilot)
- **Cabin Spaces:** 6

### Cargo
- **Cargo Capacity:** 23 tons

### Financial
- **Purchase Cost:** MCr 8.712
- **Maintenance:** Cr 726/month

### Description
The pinnace is a popular choice as an auxiliary vessel for adventuring or exploratory ships, as it has the speed, range and cargo capacity to support extended missions. It combines a generous cargo hold with a speed that leaves most star ships trailing far behind and can be configured for light combat operations with the addition of weaponry to its fixed mount.

## Slow Pinnace

**Class:** Small Craft  
**Tech Level:** 12  
**Tonnage:** 40 tons (Streamlined)  
**Hull Points:** 16  

### Performance
- **Thrust:** 3
- **Jump:** None
- **Power Plant:** Fusion (Power 20)
- **Fuel:** 4 weeks operation

### Electronics
- **Computer:** Computer/5
- **Sensors:** Basic
- **Software:** Library, Manoeuvre, Intellect

### Armament
- **Hardpoints:** 1 Fixed Mount (empty)

### Accommodations
- **Crew:** 1 (Pilot)
- **Cabin Spaces:** Not specified

### Cargo
- **Cargo Capacity:** 32.8 tons

### Financial
- **Purchase Cost:** MCr 5.787
- **Maintenance:** Cr 482/month

### Description
Like the slow boat, the slow pinnace trades speed and raw performance for increased cargo space, although this craft is based on the traditional pinnace. As a larger hull, it provides even more cargo carrying capacity than a slow boat and many are customised to become troop or vehicle transports, or to serve as fuel skimmers for larger ships.
