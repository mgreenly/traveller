# Lucky Strike (Modified Scout Ship)

## Registry
- **Registry Number:** SC-4471-M
- **Ship Class:** Modified Type-S Scout/Courier
- **Tonnage:** 100 tons

## Description
A heavily modified Scout ship optimized for deep-space prospecting and exploration. The previous owner invested heavily in upgrades after a successful mineral strike, only to lose the ship to gambling debts. The ship features military-grade sensors and extended jump range, making it ideal for reaching remote systems.

## Modifications from Standard
- **Jump Drive:** Upgraded to Jump-3 capability (+MCr 10)
- **Sensors:** Military Grade for prospecting (+MCr 2)
- **Weapons:** Beam Laser installed in turret (+MCr 1)
- **Armor:** Reinforced to Armor 6 (+MCr 2.4)
- **Fuel:** Extra 10-ton tank installed (reduces cargo to 2 tons)
