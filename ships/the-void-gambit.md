# The Void Gambit (Modified Type A Free Trader)

## Description
*The Void Gambit* is a heavily modified Type A Free Trader that perfectly reflects its captain's personality: pragmatic, fast, and surprisingly tough. Its cargo bay is smaller than a standard Free Trader's, sacrificed to make room for upgraded and overclocked maneuver drives that give it exceptional agility for a ship of its size. It has a few hidden compartments for smuggling and its transponder codes can be easily swapped, making it an ideal vessel for operating on the fringes of the law.

## Ship Stats (Based on a Type A Free Trader)
*   **Hull:** 200 tons
*   **M-Drive:** 2G (Overclocked to 3G)
*   **J-Drive:** 1 parsec
*   **Power Plant:** 2G
*   **Fuel:** 22 tons (2 parsecs of jump fuel)
*   **Bridge:** Standard
*   **Computer:** Standard (Computer/1)
*   **Sensors:** Standard
*   **Weapons:** 2x Double Turrets (Beam Lasers)
*   **Craft:** None
*   **Software:** Jump Control/1, Maneuver/0
*   **Special:** Hidden Cargo Compartments (10 tons)
*   **Cargo:** 68 tons
